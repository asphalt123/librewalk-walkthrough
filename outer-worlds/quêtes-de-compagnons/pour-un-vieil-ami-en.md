
## Quest Start
1. While exploring the Halcyon system, regularly visit **Scylla** until Félix mentions his old friend Harlow.  
2. Travel to the lost rock and land on the landing pad of **Harlow’s base**.  

## Talk to Voelker and Meet Harlow
1. Speak with **Voelker** at the base.  
2. Enter the building and talk to **Harlow**.  
3. Accept the request to pursue **Trask**.

## Visit the Precursor and Talk to Rosana
1. Go to the **Precursor** and find **Rosana** at the local bar.  
2. Show her **Harlow’s Emblem** to unlock the dialogue about her ex‑husband.

## Reach Trask’s Camp
1. Travel to the **Edgewater** area.  
2. Head north and eliminate the primates blocking the path.  
3. Locate Trask’s hidden camp in the hills.

## Choose How to Deal with Trask  

### Option 1 – Kill Trask and Take His Ring
1. Defeat **Trask**.  
2. Loot **Trask’s Ring** from his corpse.  
3. Return to **Harlow** to complete the objective.

### Option 2 – Interrogate Trask and Gather Evidence
1. Talk to **Trask** and ask for proof of Harlow’s betrayal.  
2. Request that he hand over his ring so you can fake his death.  
3. Return to **Harlow’s base**, enter the western corridor, and retrieve the documents from Trask’s stash.  
4. Show the evidence to **Harlow**; he will reveal himself, attack, and you must kill him and his men.  

## Complete the Quest
1. After the battle, go back to the **Impostor** and speak with **Félix** to finish the quest.  
2. Receive **1500 Octos** and **Harlow’s Emblem**, which unlocks the **Independent** skill for Félix.

## Alternative Outcomes
- Showing the evidence to **Voelker** does not change the outcome; the bandits remain loyal to their leader.  
- If you show the evidence to **Harlow**, you can only avoid the fight by claiming you were misled by Trask; accusing him without evidence makes him hostile.
