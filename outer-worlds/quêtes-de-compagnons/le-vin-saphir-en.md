
## Acquisition  
**Parvati**

## Reward  
**XP**

## Associated Quest  
**“Passport for Adventure”**

## Steps

1. During your first visit to the **Precursor**, go to the ship’s airlock. Parvati will be waiting with a request. Accept her request, add her to your crew, and then leave the **Impostor**.

2. Proceed to the engine room at the opposite end of the ship.  
   - Talk to **Junlei Tennyson**.  
   - Introduce Parvati to Junlei.  
   - While the conversation takes place, stay back and quietly encourage Parvati.

3. Return to the **Impostor** and speak with Parvati again. Agree to take her for a drink on the **Precursor**.

4. Head back to the **Precursor** and make your way to the local bistro.

5. Talk to Parvati at the bistro and buy her a drink (she prefers wine).  
   - Ask her about her feelings.  
   - Cheer her up and try to convince her to make the first move.

6. After the conversation, go back to the **Impostor** and speak with Junlei one more time to complete the quest.
