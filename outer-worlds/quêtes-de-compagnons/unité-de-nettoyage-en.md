
## Acquisition
- **Reward:** 1125 Octos, **SAM** joins your crew  
- Associated quest: **The Hammer of Vulcan**

## Steps
1. After completing the quest “A Stranger in a Strange Land” and launching the Impostor, go to the ship’s deck.  
2. Examine the **SAM unit** inside the closet.  
3. Talk to **ADA** to receive new objectives.  
4. Return to your cabin, access the research terminal, and read the three messages about the robot.  
5. Travel to **Roseway**.  
6. Speak with **Orson Shaw** as part of “The Hammer of Vulcan” quest.  
7. Progress the quest until you can unlock the armory door.  
8. Inside the armory, retrieve the **acid macerator** from the shelf.  
9. Return to the Impostor and insert the **acid macerator** into the cleaning unit.  
10. The **SAM** unit reactivates and joins your crew.
