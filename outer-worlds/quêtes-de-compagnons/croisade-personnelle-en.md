
## Acquisition
- Talk to **Ellie** at the ship’s airlock during your first visit to **Byzantium** to unlock the quest.

## Steps
1. **Enter Byzantium** and head west to **Ellie's house**.  
2. Inside, go into the right‑hand room to meet **Ellie's parents**. Choose any dialogue options.  
3. Exit to the street and talk to **Ellie** again to receive new objectives.  
4. Follow the quest marker to the **mailbox** and inspect it.  
5. **Travel to Fallbrook**.  
6. In Fallbrook, locate the **insurance company** and speak with the employee.  
7. Obtain a modified insurance clause for **Ellie**. You can either:  
   - **Bribe** the employee, or  
   - Use **Intimidation** (or another relevant skill) to persuade them.  
8. Return to **Ellie** in **Byzantium**, talk to her to finish the mission and claim the reward.

## Reward
- **1250 Octons**  
- **Framed Insurance Check**
