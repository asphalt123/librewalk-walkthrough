
## Acquisition

**Reward:** **1000 Octos** + **Ellie** joins your crew  

## Step‑by‑Step Guide

1. On the **Precursor**, go to the infirmary. Talk to **Ellie** near the entrance and accept to visit **Jessie** to start the companion quest.  
2. Speak with **Dr. Mfuru** and try to persuade him to let you in. When he refuses, proceed to the main infirmary room, approach the back door, and pick up the **Mardete badge** from the tablet.  
3. Use the **Holographic Sail** (unlocked with the badge) to enter the restricted area through the door.  
4. Quickly head to the right hallway, approach **Jessie**’s room, and use the intercom to have her open the door.  
5. Enter the small room and question **Jessie** about her problems with the council. After the dialogue, leave the area before your disguise fails. If you run out of time, you can:
   - Slip past the guard behind him, **or**
   - Go through the operating room, unlock the door on the right, and follow the tunnel to the motel.  
6. Return to **Ellie**, then go see **Udom** at the Halcyon Council.  
7. Resolve **Jessie**’s issue by choosing one of the following:
   - Pay **1890 Octos** to **Udom**, **or**
   - Convince **Udom** (Persuasion 20) to sign a servitude contract for **Jessie**.  
8. After the resolution, talk to **Jessie**; she will give you **1000 Octos** as a reward.  
9. Speak with **Ellie** again and accept her onto the **Impostor**. She will now be a member of your crew.
