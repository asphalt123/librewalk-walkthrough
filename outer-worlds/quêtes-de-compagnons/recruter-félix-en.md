
## Prerequisites
- None required beyond reaching the **Precursor** for the first time.  
- You will need to complete the quest **“Passport to Adventure”** to speak with **Udom** and authorize the **Impostor** to launch.

## Steps
1. On your first visit to the **Precursor**, go to the docks and **talk to Félix**.  
2. Proceed with the quest **“Passport to Adventure”**: find **Udom**, speak with him, and obtain his permission for the **Impostor** to take off.  
3. Return to the area in front of your ship, **talk to Félix** again, and choose to **accept him aboard**.
