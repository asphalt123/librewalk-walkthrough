
## Acquisition  
**Max the Vicar** (recruits to your crew)

## Reward  
**Max** joins your team

## Associated Quest  
*“A Stranger in a Strange Land”*

## Steps

1. **Go to Edgewater**  
   - Head to the temple and talk to **Max the Vicar**.  
   - Ask about his beliefs and his profession until he requests your help retrieving a **precious manual**.

2. **Travel East** to locate the **collector's house**.  
   - Enter the small room behind the counter.  
   - Read the document placed on the furniture.

3. **Travel West** to find the entrance of a small cave.  
   - Proceed through the tunnel.  
   - Near the collector’s hideout, shoot the mines to detonate them.

4. **Unlock the safe** (requires **lockpicking 5**) to obtain the **journal of Mr. Bakonu**.

5. **Return to Max** at the temple.  
   - Give him the **precious manual**.  
   - Accept his request to board the **Impostor**.

6. **Complete the quest**  
   - After finishing “A Stranger in a Strange Land,” you will find **Max** aboard your ship.
