
## Walkthrough

1. **Complete the previous Parvati quest**, then talk to your **mechanic on the Impostor** to receive the new mission.  
2. Travel to the **Precursor** with Parvati and obtain the **perfume** from **Gladys**.  
3. Give the perfume to **Parvati** to unlock new objectives. Return to the **Impostor** and head to **Stellar Bay**.  
4. In Stellar Bay, speak with the **head chef** and request a **Powder Keg Stew**.  
   - If you have **55 Intimidation**, you can obtain it for free.  
   - Without the required skill, pay **3000 Octos** for the stew.  
5. After acquiring the stew, go to **Monarch**, then travel to the abandoned town of **Cascadia**.  
6. Enter Cascadia, eliminate the bandits, and proceed to the old **Rizzo's Store** in the western part of town.  
7. Inside Rizzo's Store:  
   - Kill the remaining marauders.  
   - Go upstairs and retrieve the **Little Heart Cakes** from the shelf behind the bar.  
8. Give the **Little Heart Cakes** to **Parvati**, then leave Cascadia and travel to **Byzantium**.  
9. At **Jolicoeur's Haberdashery**, talk to **Celeste** and purchase an outfit for Parvati.  
   - Base price: **6000 Octos**.  
   - With **65 Persuasion** or **65 Intimidation**, you can haggle the price down to **3000 Octos**.  
10. Hand the new outfit to **Parvati**, then return to your ship.  
11. Dock at the **Precursor** and speak with Parvati to receive the final objectives.  
    - Go up to the kitchen, approach the two women, then exit onto the docks.  
12. Return to your ship, talk to **Parvati** once more to complete the quest and claim your reward: **2500 Octos** and a **photo of Junlei and Parvati**.
