
## Starting the Quest
1. After completing **“The Illustrated Manual”** and retrieving the manuscript, talk to **Max** to trigger the hermit search quest.
2. Go to the **Precursor** and approach the **Security Post**.

## Gaining Access to the Security Post
Choose one of the following methods:

### 1. Bribe Lee Flores  
- Talk to **Lee Flores** and give him **675 Octos** to let you in.

### 2. Persuade Lee Flores  
- Use at least **45 Persuasion** to convince Lee to let you enter.  
- If you lack sufficient Persuasion, complete the quests **“Who Goes There?”** and **“Ship, Sweet Ship”** to lower the required Persuasion to **30**, then **15**.

### 3. Infiltrate via Lockpicking (30 Lockpicking) – Version 1  
- Go to the **Precursor Hangar** and reach the back door of the Security Post.  
- Wait for the corridor to clear, pick the lock, and enter.  
- Retrieve **Mardet’s Badge** from the shelf and equip the **Holographic Veil** to disguise yourself.

### 4. Infiltrate via Badge – Version 2  
- Pick up **Mardet’s Badge** from the crates near the Security Post.  
- Walk through the door; the disguise activates automatically.

## Retrieve Information
1. Enter the small back room of the Security Post.  
2. Access the terminal and collect the data.  
3. Give the **data cartridge** to **Max** to receive new objectives.

## Return to Max
1. Travel to the **Fallbrook Outpost** on **Monarch**.  
2. Locate the **hermit’s house** and take the document from the shelf for additional information.

## Locate the Hermit
1. Head north to the tunnel outside the town.  
2. Talk to the **Hermit**.  
3. During the conversation, keep **Max’s** aggression in check so he does not kill the hermit.

## Travel to the Final Encounter
1. Return to the **Impostor** and set course for **Scylla**.  
2. Upon arrival, proceed to the abandoned outpost and enter the hermit’s dwelling.

## Final Sequence
1. Speak with the old woman inside.  
2. Enter the meditation room on the left.  
3. Interact with the **Incense Burner** to trigger the hallucinatory sequence.  
4. During the visions, talk with your companions and let the sequence play out.  
5. After the dialogue ends, the quest completes.  

## Rewards
- **2625 Octos**  
- **Incense Burner** (unlocks Max’s **“In Harmony”** skill)
