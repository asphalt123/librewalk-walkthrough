
## Acquisition  
Recruit **Nyoka** to your team.  

## Reward  
**2375 Octos** + **Framed Photo** (unlocks the **Team** skill for Nyoka).  

## Associated Quest  
**Radio Free Monarch**  

## Walkthrough  

1. After recruiting Nyoka, travel to **Monarch** with her.  
   - During the conversation, accept Nyoka’s proposal to start the mission.  

2. Proceed to **Hayes’ grave**.  
   - Eliminate the hostile creatures there.  
   - Retrieve the **medallion** from the grave and give it to Nyoka.  

3. Continue the **Radio Free Monarch** quest until you encounter **Hiram**.  
   - Persuade Hiram to assist Nyoka during the dialogue.  

4. Head to the **Edgewater** region and make your way to the abandoned outpost northwest of the area.  
   - Defeat the bandits inside.  
   - Use the door controls to unlock the building’s entrance.  

5. Inside the outpost:  
   - Loot the **medallions** from the corpses.  
   - Read the information block on the bed.  
   - Talk to Nyoka to receive the next objectives.  

6. Travel to **Scylla**.  

7. On Scylla:  
   - Locate the three quest markers.  
   - Eliminate the **rotting primates** and collect **pheromone glands** from them.  

8. Return the **pheromone glands** to Nyoka, then go back to **Monarch**.  

9. Back on the hostile planet, locate Nyoka’s former team hideout.  
   - Unlock the door using the terminal.  

10. Inside the hideout:  
    - Clear out the monsters.  
    - Search the two corpses and retrieve the **medallions**.  

11. Interact with the **Mantisaur pantry** and deposit the glands.  
    - Defeat all creatures that emerge from the tunnel.  

12. After the battle, talk to Nyoka to complete the quest.  
    - Receive the **Framed Photo**, which unlocks the **Team** skill for Nyoka.  

13. (Optional) Convince Nyoka to keep the **medallions**; they will appear in her room on the **Impostor**.
