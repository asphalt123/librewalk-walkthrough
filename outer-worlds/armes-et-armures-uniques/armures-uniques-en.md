
## List of Armors and Their Locations

- **Hibernation Helmet** – Found in Phineas Welles’ laboratory.  
- **Defective Moon‑Man Helmet** – Located in the storage room of the Spacer’s Choice shop in the Precursor settlement. Requires **Lockpicking 20**.  
- **Welding Goggles** – Reward for completing the quest **“Ship, Sweet Ship.”**  
- **Raptidon Guardian Armor** – Found in a bedroom on the upper floor of the Roseway laboratory’s habitation quarters.  
- **Researcher Lab Coat** – Reward for the quest **“Roseway at Full Teeth”** if you turn the scientific research over to Anton Crane.  
- **Pretty Hat** – Found in the abandoned church south of Stellar Bay.  
- **Iconoclast Apostle Helmet** – Reward for the quest **“The Transporter.”**  
- **Iconoclast Apostle Armor** – Reward for the quest **“The Price of the Press.”**  
- **Purple Police Helmet** – Found in the old customs post within the Cascadia ruins.  
- **Survivalist Marauder Helmet** – Behind a locked door (Lockpicking **55**) on the upper floor of a building in the marauder camp near the river, southwest of Stellar Bay.  
- **Survivalist Marauder Armor** – Found in a chest at the marauder camp near the river.  
- **Gluco‑Mando Helmet** – Inside a safe on the ground floor of the former Rizzo factory in Cascadia. Requires **Lockpicking 40**.  
- **Gluco‑Mando Armor** – Retrieved from the corpse of Herbert in the bridge shelter of Cascadia.  
  - *Access*: Go to the roof of the Rizzo factory, climb over the wall, use the side elevator, then reach the shelter. A **Lockpicking 40** skill is needed to enter.  
- **Researcher Mask** – Inside a safe on satellite **SRH‑1084** during the quest **“The Ice Palace.”**  
  - *Access*: Open the door from the office terminal (**Hacking 65**) or pick it (**Lockpicking 100**).  
- **MSI Elite Helmet** – Reward for the quest **“His Name Is BOLT.”**  
- **MSI Elite Armor** – Reward for the quest **“Hidden Errors.”**  
- **Hemlock Eye Patch** – Found on a corpse in the abandoned Monarque relay station. Only accessible during the quest **“The Swindler.”**  
- **SubLight Contractor Helmet** – Reward for the quest **“Clive the Butcher.”**  
- **Chest‑Piercer Vest** – Found in a trash can in Harlow’s hideout on Scylla. Only accessible during the quest **“For an Old Friend.”**  
- **Chimera Suit** – Reward for the mission **“The Reckless Spacesuit.”**  
- **Certified Explorer Hat** – Reward for the mission **“Halcyon in Eighty Hours.”**  
- **SubLight Vice‑President Armor** – Reward for the quest **“Chimeric Experiments”** if you decide to eliminate Dr. Chartrand.
