
## Overview
- **Acquisition:** Automatic  
- **Reward:** Variable  
- **Associated Quest:** **Hidden Errors**

## Initial Steps
1. After completing the **Radio Free Monarque** (or **Pirate Radio**) quest, leave the Devil's Peak relay station and travel to the crash site northwest.  
2. Enter the crashed ship and use the terminal to retrieve the **Command Key**.  
3. Open the door behind you and climb the ladder.  
4. At the top, go through the door, place the **Map** in the left console, then pull the right lever to release the **Module**.  

## Option 1 – Deliver the Module to **Sanjar**
- Travel to **Stellar Bay** and give the **Module** to **Sanjar**.  
- Exit the town and fight your way through **Iconoclast** troops.  
- Continue until you reach the old church, then eliminate **Graham** and **Zora**.  
- Return to **Sanjar** to finish the quest and receive the unique weapon **MSI Sauthon Grasper**.  

## Option 2 – Deliver the Module to **Graham**
- Go to **Amber Heights** and enter **Graham**'s office; you will be confronted by **Zora**.  
- Remain loyal to **Graham** during the dialogue, then proceed to the Iconoclast leader on the upper floor.  
- After handing over the **Module**, leave the camp and follow the troops along the road to **Stellar Bay**, then turn toward the ruins west of the town.  
- Pursue **Sanjar** in the ruins and eliminate him to complete the objective.  
- Return to **Graham** to finish the quest and receive the unique weapon **The Little Word**.  

## Option 3 – Negotiate a Truce Between **MSI** and the **Iconoclasts**
- Talk to **Sanjar** and propose a truce between the two factions.  
- Leave the town and travel to the Cascadia ruins. Enter the old **Rizzo** factory through the rear door.  
- Inside, go to the back‑left office, use the terminal, and retrieve **Zora's evaluations**.  
- Return to **Sanjar** and give him the cartridge so he accepts your proposal.  
- Go to **Amber Heights**, speak with **Zora**, and agree to support her against **Graham**. Then go upstairs to confront the Iconoclast leader.  
- After eliminating **Graham**, talk to **Zora** again and use dialogue skills to persuade her to accept a truce with **MSI**.  
- Proceed to the abandoned church in the Stellar Bay ruins to oversee the negotiations. The mission ends and you receive the unique weapon **Gentle Voixen**.  

## Additional Notes
- You may ally with **Zora** and give the **Module** to her instead of **Graham**; the quest proceeds similarly, and if you cannot reconcile the factions, she will become the Iconoclast leader.  
- To convince **Sanjar** to accept the truce, you must have consulted his evaluations on the **ARMY terminal** during the **Hidden Errors** quest.
