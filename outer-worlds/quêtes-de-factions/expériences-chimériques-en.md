
## Quest Overview  
- **Acquisition**: From **Lilya Hagen**  
- **Reward**: Variable (see options below)  
- **Associated Quest**: “Clive the Butcher”  

## Steps  

1. **Obtain the mission** from **Lilya Hagen**.  
2. **Travel to Byzance** (the lower area) and **activate the elevator** using the terminal.  
3. **Ride the elevator** down to the **basement laboratory**.  

### Choose the fate of **Dr. Eva Chartrand**  

#### Option 1 – Kill **Dr. Eva Chartrand**  

- **Eliminate all enemies** in the laboratory.  
- **Return to Lilya Hagen** on the **Precursor**.  
- **Rewards**: 2,188 **Octos** and the **Vice President's Armor (SubLight)**.  
- **Additional reward** (if you completed “Clive the Butcher” for Catherine on Monarch): go to your cabin and retrieve the unique **Negotiator** weapon from the desk.  

#### Option 2 – Spare **Dr. Eva Chartrand**  

- **Spare the scientist** and persuade her to continue her work for **Welles** or the **Council**. Choosing the **Council** grants a small reputation boost with that faction.  
- **Convince the guard** to leave you alone by saying “**Sophia needs me alive**”.  
- **Leave the area**.  
- **Report back to Lilya Hagen**; she will be dissatisfied and will not give any reward.
