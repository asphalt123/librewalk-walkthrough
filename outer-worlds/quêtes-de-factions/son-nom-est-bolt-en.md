
## Quest Details
- **Acquisition**: Sanjar  
- **Reward**: 2188 Octos + **Elite MSI Helmet**  
- **Associated Quests**: “Hidden Errors”, “Free Radio Monarch”

## Steps
1. Travel to **Stellar Bay** and speak with **Sanjar**. Accept the mission.  
2. Exit the town and head northwest to the ruins.  
3. Search the corpse near the bridge exit and pick up the **ARMEE Building Key**.  
4. Go to the indicated ARMEE building and unlock the front door with the key.  
5. Inside, use the ground‑floor terminal to view the administrative evaluations. Delete the data to complete the optional objective and earn an extra 750 Octos from **Sanjar**.  
6. Go upstairs, move onto the balcony, and talk to **Huxley**.  
7. Unlock the storage room door using the **Access Card**.  
8. Retrieve the **BOLT‑52 Data Cartridge** from the left‑hand furniture.  
9. Return to **Sanjar** to finish the mission.  

## Optional Bonus
- After freeing **Huxley**, ask for a reward and persuade him to promote you to the Iconoclasts, granting a small reputation boost with that faction.
