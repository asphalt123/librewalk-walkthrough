
## Starting the Quest
1. Go to **Amber Heights** and talk to **Graham**, leader of the Iconoclasts, to receive the quest.  
2. After the dialogue, leave the camp and head north to the **Bayside warehouse**.  
3. Approach **Niles** and initiate conversation.

## Choices with Niles
- **Convince Niles to leave** – use either **55 Bluff**, **55 Persuade**, or **100 Intimidate**.  
- **Eliminate everyone** – kill all SubLight employees (this will significantly lower your reputation with the SubLight faction).

## Warehouse
4. Proceed to the warehouse and use the **intercom** to contact **Carlotta**.  
5. Enter the warehouse interior and retrieve the **cargo**.  

## Optional Loot
- Collect **provisions for Zora** **or** **ammo for Graham** before leaving the warehouse.

## Completion
6. Return to **Amber Heights** and speak with **Graham** to finish the quest.  

**Reward:** 2188 **Octo** + **Iconoclast Apostle Helmet**.
