
## Acquisition
- **Lilya Hagen**

## Reward
- **2188 Octos**

## Associated Quest
- “Cosmocrime Syndicate”

## Steps
1. After completing the “Cosmocrime Syndicate” quest, talk to Lilya again to receive this quest.  
2. Return to your ship and travel to satellite **SRH‑1084**.  
3. Enter the hangar and head to the north‑west corridor.  
   - Destroy the floor mines.  
   - Descend to the lower level.  
4. Use the terminal to restore power in the base and make contact with UDL troops.  
5. Decide how to handle the corporate troops:  
   - **Negotiate** – offer money, intimidate (65 Intimidation), or lie (65 Deception).  
   - **Eliminate** – fight the assault troops if you lack the required skills or prefer not to spend Octos.  
6. After either outcome, go back upstairs and head to the crates in the south‑east.  
   - Pass through the vent and enter the room at the far right.  
7. In the office, retrieve the **blank map** from the left‑hand furniture and use the terminal to encode it.  
8. Return the opposite way and pass through the eastern door.  
9. Descend the stairs and open the right‑hand door using the **access card**.  
   - Use the terminal to disable the security system of the adjacent room.  
10. Return to the corridor, enter the next room, pass through its door, and head west.  
    - Cross the laboratory and enter the office at the top of the stairs.  
11. Use the terminal to access security routines and insert the **cartridge** provided by Lilya to complete the objective.  
    - If you have **hacking** (65), you can also unlock the nearby door and retrieve the **Seeker Mask** from the safe.  
12. Return to your ship and report back to Lilya to finish the mission.
