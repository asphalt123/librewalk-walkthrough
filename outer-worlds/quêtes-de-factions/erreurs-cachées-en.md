
## Quest Details
- **Acquisition**: Sanjar  
- **Reward**: 2,188 Octos + MSI Elite Armor  
- **Related Quests**: “His Name is BOLT”, “Free Radio Monarch”, “Weapons of the Great Void”

## Starting the Quest
1. Complete the quest **“His Name is BOLT.”**  
2. Accept Sanjar’s proposal that appears after the dialogue.  
3. Travel to **Fallbrook** and talk to **Catherine**.  
4. Choose one of the following options to continue:

## Finding the UDL Laboratory

### Option 1 – Locate Catherine’s Courier
1. Accept the task to find the missing SubLight employee.  
2. Go to the **Destroyed Bridge**.  
3. Inspect the automatic loader, then follow the blood trails to the hidden hatch in the tall grass.  
4. Descend carefully, jumping over mines and avoiding electric arcs, until you reach **Arthur** at the end of the cave.  
5. Talk to Arthur for new information and leave the area.  
6. Return to **Catherine**, report the findings, and gain reputation.  
7. Proceed to the **UDL Laboratory** location.

### Option 2 – Interrogate Huxley (Requires 100 Lie or 100 Intimidation)
1. Use the required dialogue skill to force **Catherine** to reveal her information.  
2. Speak with **Westley** and **Yvonne** to update your objectives.  
3. Travel to **Amber Heights** and talk to **Huxley** to learn the exact location of the UDL lab.

## Retrieving the Lab Data
1. Arrive at the **UDL Laboratory**.  
2. Watch the scientists die, then neutralize the bandits.  
3. Enter the main building.  
4. Go to the right‑hand room, take the stairs to the upper floor.  
5. Enter the control room on the left and use the terminal to **download the data**.  
6. While inside, pick up the **Access Card** from the desk.  
7. Use the card to access the **Gluant Weapon** on the ground floor and complete the objective for the quest **“Weapons of the Great Void.”**  
8. Return to **Sanjar** to finish the quest.

## Alternative Paths
- **Direct Lab Approach**: You may skip the intermediate information‑gathering steps and head straight to the UDL lab.  
- **Bribe or Persuade Huxley**: Offer money or use dialogue skills to convince Huxley to help. She will give you the **Access Card** for the Chastity hideout, allowing you to use terminals around the lab to trigger the alarm or unlock the large north door.  
- **If Huxley Is Dead**: Should Huxley die during “His Name is BOLT,” you cannot interrogate her for information, so follow the courier‑finding route instead.
