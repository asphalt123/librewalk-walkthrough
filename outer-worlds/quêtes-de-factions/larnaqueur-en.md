
## Quest Activation
1. Complete the quest **“The Price of the Press.”**
2. After it ends, go to the **infirmary** and locate **Zora**.
3. Speak with her and accept her request to start the mission.

## Travel to the Relay Station
1. Head west toward the **relay station**.
2. Clear any enemies in the surrounding area.

## Unlock the Relay Station
1. Use the **access card** that Zora gave you to unlock the building’s door.

## Inside the Relay Station
1. Locate the corpse holding the unique **Hemlock eyepatch armor** and take it.
2. On the left side of the room, open the crate and collect the **data block**.

## Return to Zora
1. Travel back to the **infirmary** where Zora is waiting.
2. Report the findings to her to complete the quest.

**Reward:** 750 Octos and information on Graham.  

**Associated quests:** “The Price of the Press,” “Tomorrow the Canines.”  

*Note: Completing this mission may allow you to negotiate a truce between MSI and the Iconoclasts during “Tomorrow the Canines.”*
