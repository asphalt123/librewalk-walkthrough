
## Accept Quest
1. After completing **Stellar Retriever**, talk to **Lilya Hagen** to start the quest.  
2. Travel to **Stellar Bay** and go to the docks behind the cannery.  
3. Find the hidden corpse behind crates and read the bloody message.

## Travel to Fallbrook
4. Leave Stellar Bay and head southeast to **Fallbrook**.  
5. Locate **Catherine** and give her the message.

## Proceed to Cascadia
6. Exit the colony and travel west to **Cascadia**.  
7. Follow the outer wall on the left, enter the town, and eliminate the bandits in the streets.  
8. Enter the building marked by the quest marker and use the terminal.  
9. Enter the password found on the bloody message to open the ground‑floor door.  
10. Take the elevator down to **Rizzo's secret laboratory**.

## Enter the Laboratory
11. At the lab entrance, turn right and enter the first room on the left.  
12. Either convince the security robots you have permission (**60 Lie**) or destroy them.  
13. Proceed through the southern door and use the terminal to contact **ADA**.  
14. Read the messages to learn about the ventilation system.  
15. Unlock the door on the left and walk down the corridor to the next section.

## Neutralize the Mantisaur
16. Continue to the end of the corridor, turn right, destroy the mines on the left, and cross the electric arcs.  
17. Enter the left‑hand room and neutralize the robots.  
18. Head to the far‑back corridor, go north, and move slowly to avoid steam jets.  
19. At the far end, open the door and interact with the terminal on the right.  
20. Choose one of the following options to deal with the **Mantisaur**:  
    - If **Ellie** is in the party, have her modify the CO₂ level to kill all mantisaur (use **Surgery**).  
    - If **Ellie** is not present but you have read the ventilation messages, perform the CO₂ adjustment yourself (**60 Science** or **100 Hacking**).

## Transfer the Alta-Vitae to the Impostor
21. After the mantisaur issue is resolved, unlock the northern door and continue down the corridor.  
22. Enter the first room on the right, climb the ladder at the back, and use the terminal to align the supply system.  
23. Return to the previous room, examine the preserved body in the tube, then go to the office at the far right.  
24. Read the terminal messages, then head to the stairs at the back to reach the quest marker.  
25. Use the terminal to choose how to siphon the **Alta-Vitae**:  
    - If you have the required skills, transfer it yourself (**40 Engineering** or **60 Hacking**).  
    - If **Parvati** is in the party, let her handle the transfer (Engineer talent).  
    - If **Max** is in the party, let him handle the transfer (Hacking talent).

## Retrieve the Control Ray and Finish
26. From the terminal area, go east, jump onto the platform, climb the ladder, and jump to the next platform.  
27. Ascend to the upper level and collect the **Control Ray** (for the “Grand Void Weapons” quest).  
28. Turn around, take the elevator back up, open the barricaded door, and return to the **Impostor** ship.  
29. Speak to **Lilya Hagen** to complete the mission.

## Alternative Transfer (No Skill Required)
- If you lack the necessary skills, you can still move the **Alta-Vitae** to the Impostor by overloading the system regulators. This method prevents further exploration of Rizzo’s lab without taking damage.
