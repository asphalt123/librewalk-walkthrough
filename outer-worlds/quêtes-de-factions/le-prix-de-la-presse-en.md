
## Acquisition
**Graham**

## Reward
**2188 Octos** + **Iconoclast Apostle armor**

## Associated Quests
- “The Transporter”
- “Free Radio Monarch”

## Walkthrough

1. After completing **The Transporter**, obtain the quest from **Graham** and head to Terra‑1 Publishing.
2. Approach the **Van Noy** and initiate dialogue.
3. During the conversation:
   - Ask the **Van Noy** to return to Amber Heights.  
   - Accept to search for their doctor.
4. Proceed to the main building, turn right, and eliminate the hostile creatures.
5. In the small building at the back, loot the doctor’s corpse to acquire the **medical kit** and the unique weapon **Euthanasia Kit**.
6. Return to the **Van Noy** and give them the **medical kit** to complete the optional objective.
7. Enter the main building of Terra‑1 Publishing and begin clearing the area.
8. After the combat:
   - Go to the western room and ascend the stairs.  
   - Enter the second room on the left to locate the **terminal** that allows you to repair the **press**.
9. Once the press is repaired, report back to **Graham**.
10. Because you successfully extracted the **Van Noy**, you receive an additional **750 Octos**.

### Alternate Path
- During the dialogue with the **Van Noy**, use 60 points of Persuasion, Intimidation, or Deception to convince them to assault the building. They will then assist you during the zone‑clearing phase.
