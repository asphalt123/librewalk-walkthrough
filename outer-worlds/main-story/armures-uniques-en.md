
## List of Armors and Locations

- **Hibernation Helmet** – Found in Phineas Welles' laboratory.  
- **Defective Moon-Man Helmet** – Found in the storage room of the Spacer’s Choice store in the Precursor. Requires a lockpicking skill of 20.  
- **Welding Goggles** – Reward for the quest “Ship, Sweet Ship”.  
- **Raptidon Guardian Armor** – Found in a bedroom on the upper floor of the Roseway laboratory housing quarters.  
- **Researcher Lab Coat** – Reward for the quest “Roseway With All Teeth” if you hand the scientific research to Anton Crane.  
- **Nice Hat** – Found in the abandoned church south of Stellar Bay.  
- **Iconoclast Apostle Helmet** – Reward for the quest “The Transporter”.  
- **Iconoclast Apostle Armor** – Reward for the quest “The Price of the Press”.  
- **Purple Police Helmet** – Found in the old customs post in the Cascadia ruins.  
- **Survivalist Marauder Helmet** – Behind a locked door (55 lockpicking) on the upper floor of a building in the marauder camp near the river, southwest of Stellar Bay.  
- **Survivalist Marauder Armor** – Found in a chest in the marauder camp near the river.  
- **Gluco-Mando Helmet** – Found in a safe on the ground floor of the former Rizzo factory in Cascadia. Requires a lockpicking skill of 40.  
- **Gluco-Mando Armor** – Retrieved from the corpse of Herbert in the bridge refuge of Cascadia. To access: go to the roof of the Rizzo factory, jump over the wall, use the side elevator, then unlock the refuge (40 lockpicking).  
- **Researcher Mask** – Found in a safe on satellite SRH-1084 during the quest “The Ice Palace”. Access requires opening the door from the office terminal (65 hacking) or lockpicking (100 lockpicking).  
- **MSI Elite Helmet** – Reward for the quest “His Name Is BOLT”.  
- **MSI Elite Armor** – Reward for the quest “Hidden Errors”.  
- **Hemlock Eyepatch** – Found on a corpse in the abandoned Monarque relay station. Only accessible during the quest “The Swindler”.  
- **SubLight Contractor Helmet** – Reward for the quest “Clive the Butcher”.  
- **Chest-Piercing Vest** – Found in a trash can in Harlow’s hideout on Scylla. Only during the quest “For an Old Friend”.  
- **Chimera** – Reward for the mission “The Reckless Spacesuit”.  
- **Certified Explorer Hat** – Reward for the mission “Halcyon in Eighty Hours”.  
- **SubLight Vice President Armor** – Reward for the quest “Chimeric Experiments” if you choose to eliminate Dr. Chartrand.
