
## Acquisition
- **Minister Clarke**

## Reward
- None

## Associated Quest
- **“The City and the Stars”**

## Walkthrough
1. While on the quest **“The City and the Stars”**, locate and speak to **Minister Clarke**.  
2. During the dialogue, the minister will automatically assign you the mission to infiltrate the Council HQ.  
3. Follow the objectives of **“The City and the Stars”** until you reach the Halcyon Council headquarters.  
4. Inside the headquarters, find the **former minister’s office**.  
5. Enter the office and **use the computer** to **send a message to Earth**.
