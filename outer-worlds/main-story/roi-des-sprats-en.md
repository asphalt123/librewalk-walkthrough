
## Acquisition
- **Nelson Mayson** (quest giver) – Reward: Variable

## Steps
1. At the Fallbrook outpost on Monarch, talk to **Nelson** and accept his proposal to start the mission.  
   - Optionally intimidate him during dialogue to gain a reputation bonus with **SubLight**.

2. Proceed to the eastern part of the area and unlock the storage room door.

3. Inside the storage room:
   - Eliminate all **Raptidons**.
   - Search the bodies of the **Sprats** to collect the drug.

4. Return to **Nelson** and report your findings to receive the next objectives.

5. Exit to the street, follow the quest marker, and enter the building on your left to meet **Bertrand**.

6. During dialogue with **Bertrand**, use either Persuasion or Medicine to convince him you are not there to harm the **Sprats**.  
   - If you fail, you must kill him.  
   - In either case, retrieve the drug from the **Sprats'** excrement.

7. After completing the task, return to **Nelson** to finish the quest and claim your reward.

## Alternative Outcomes
- During the initial dialogue with **Nelson**, use Charm to negotiate a higher payment (1,125 Octos instead of 375).  
- After retrieving the drugs, you can lie to **Nelson** and keep the drugs for yourself, but this will impose a reputation penalty with **SubLight**.
