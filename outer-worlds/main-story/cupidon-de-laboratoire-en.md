
## Acquisition
- Talk to **Gilles Molina** at the entrance of the maintenance tunnels in Byzance.

## Reward
- **1125 Octos**

## Associated Quest
- “The City and the Stars”

## Steps
1. Go to the entrance of the maintenance tunnels in Byzance.  
2. Speak with **Gilles Molina** to activate the mission.  
3. After the dialogue, take the side elevator to reach the tunnel area.  
4. Follow the quest markers and capture the **Sprats**.  
5. Return to **Gilles Molina** and collect your reward.
