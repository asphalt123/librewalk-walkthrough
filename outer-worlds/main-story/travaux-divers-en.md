
## Acquisition
- Talk to **Branson** at the Amber Heights Iconoclast camp on Monarch.
- Offer your services to receive the quest.

## Reward
- **375 Octos**

## Quest Steps

### 1. Choose a repair method

You have three ways to resolve the camp’s technical problems.

#### Remote Repair (requires 55 **Engineering**, 55 **Science**, 55 **Hacking**)
1. Go to the small building north of the camp.  
2. Activate the terminal.  
3. Use the terminal to reactivate the faulty systems for the Iconoclasts.

#### Manual Repair – Bar Lever
1. Go to the back of the bar.  
2. Navigate around the exposed cable to reach the lever.  
3. Pull the lever down.

#### Manual Repair – Clinic Lever
1. Proceed to the clinic.  
2. Jump onto the rocks opposite the clinic.  
3. Leap over the steam jets.  
4. Pull the lever down to fix the problem.

#### Manual Repair – Bayside Terrace Communications Lever
1. Exit the camp and head north toward the Bayside Terrace ruins.  
2. Locate the lever there.  
3. Pull the lever down to reactivate communications.
