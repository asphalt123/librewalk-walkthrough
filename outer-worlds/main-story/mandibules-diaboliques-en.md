
## Starting the Quest
1. Go to the **E3 barracks** located near the summit of **Devil's Peak**.
2. Talk to the mercenaries.
   - Use **bribery** or **persuasion** to avoid a fight.  
   - Do **not** claim you came from the caves, or they will attack.
3. Offer your services to help locate their leader **Berthold**.

## Finding the Cave Entrance
4. Follow the trail upward to the quest marker.
5. Reach the entrance to the caves.

## Inside the Caves
6. Enter the caves and head **left**.
7. Defeat the **giant regal mantis**.
8. Talk to **Berthold** after the battle.
9. Exit the caves and return to the **E3 barracks**.

## Obtaining the Access Card and Weapon
10. Speak with **Berthold** again to receive the **Access Card** for the **E3 weapon cache**.
11. Persuade **Berthold** to accompany you during the assault on the relay station (part of the “Radio Free Monarch” mission).
12. Enter the nearby building.
13. Retrieve the unique weapon **Thunder** from the furniture near the door.
14. Use the **Access Card** to unlock the crates on the right side of the room.

## Alternative Paths
- **Cave‑first approach**: If you reach Devil's Peak through the caves, you can start the quest by killing the mantis first, then talking to **Berthold**.
- **Skill‑based persuasion**: With the right dialogue skills, you can convince the mercenaries to assist you without dealing with **Berthold** at all.
- **Killing mercenaries**: If you kill the mercenaries, you will lose the ability to interact with **Berthold**, even after rescuing him in the caves.
