
## Starting the Mission  

1. Go to the **Jolicoeur Tailor Shop** in Byzance.  
2. Talk to **Celeste** to activate the mission.  

## Collect the Three Armor Sets  

1. **Iconoclast Armor** and **Spaceman Armor**  
   - Travel to **Amber Heights**.  
   - Speak with the quartermaster of the Iconoclasts and purchase the required armor pieces.  

2. **Marauder Armor**  
   - Hunt **marauders** throughout **Halcyon**.  
   - Loot their corpses for the necessary components.  

3. After obtaining all three sets, return to **Celeste**.  
   - Equip each armor set one at a time.  
   - Talk to **Celeste** again to confirm the objective.  

## Gather Crafting Materials  

1. **Primate Leather** – Go to **Scylla** and collect it from the wildlife.  
2. **Raptidon Flank Leather** – Travel to **Monarch** and obtain it from the local fauna.  
3. **Regal Mantis Chitin** – Head to the southwest of **Stellar Bay** and defeat the giant regal mantis to harvest its chitin.  

## Return to Celeste and Continue the Quest  

1. Bring the three materials back to **Celeste** for new objectives.  
2. Proceed to **Halcyon Parcel Service**.  
   - Speak with **Olive**.  
   - Then speak with **Wanda**.  

## Retrieve the Parcel  

1. Leave the Parcel Service area and follow the new quest marker.  
2. Pick up the **message** attached to the door.  
3. Return to **Halcyon Parcel Service**.  
   - Talk to **Wanda** again, then to **Olive**.  
   - Show the **power of attorney** given by **Celeste** to obtain the parcel.  

## Discover Celeste’s Fate  

1. Travel to **Celeste’s shop**.  
2. Talk briefly with the guards stationed there.  
3. Search **Celeste’s corpse** for the **office key**.  

## Complete the Mission  

1. Open the left-hand door using the key.  
2. Retrieve the unique **Chimera Armor**.  
3. The mission is now complete.
