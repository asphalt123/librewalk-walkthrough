
## Steps

1. In **Byzance**, go to the Exploration Office and talk to **Alonzo Vallejos** to receive the side mission.  
2. After the dialogue, exit the building, follow the quest marker, and interact with the first **beacon**.  
3. Travel to **Edgewater**, follow the marker, and interact with the second **beacon**.  
4. Head to **Scylla**, approach the objective location, and quickly interact with the **beacon** located outside the protective shield.  
5. Proceed to **Stellar Bay**, locate the final **beacon** in the southwest part of the town, and interact with it.  
6. Return to **Alonzo Vallejos** to complete the quest and obtain the **Certified Explorer Hat**.
