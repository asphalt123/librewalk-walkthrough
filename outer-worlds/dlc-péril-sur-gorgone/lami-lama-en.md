
## Acquisition
- Talk to **Raquel** near the fossil southeast of Sprat Cabin and accept the mission.

## Objective
- Retrieve the **package** for **Captain Babbage** and return it to **Raquel**.

## Steps
1. Speak with **Raquel** and agree to locate the **package**.
2. Fight your way through the **Marauder camp** east of your position to reach **Lama Rock**.
3. Collect the **package** from the altar at **Lama Rock**.
4. Return to **Raquel** with the **package**.

## Completion Options

- **Give the package without negotiating**  
  - Reward: **750 credits** + **Chrono‑Adaptation suit**.

- **Negotiate the hand‑over** (base reward + **3,250 credits**)  
  1. In dialogue with **Captain Babbage**, choose the line: “because you think I’m just going to hand you the package like that?”  
  2. Then select: “I’ll take whatever you have to offer” to receive the 3,250‑credit bonus.

- **Intimidate the captain** (requires 80 Intimidation) **or** **Lie** (requires 80 Deception) (base reward + **3,438 credits**)  
  1. After choosing to negotiate, pick either the intimidation or deception option.  
  2. This grants an additional 3,438 credits at the end of the quest.
