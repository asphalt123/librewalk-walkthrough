
## Main Quest Steps

1. **Activate the quest**.  
2. Head **south**, then follow the waypoint **west**.  
3. In the next room, **go around the steam jets on the right** and **enter the back door**.  
4. On the other side, **examine the fuel containers on the left shelf** using the **Anomaly Amplifier** and **defuse the bomb**.  
5. **Talk to Ludovico**.  
6. **Leave the area**.  
7. Return to your **suite** and **use the terminal** to report to the **Administrator**.
