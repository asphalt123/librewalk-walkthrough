
## Main Path

1. From **the Impostor**, travel to the **Precursor auxiliary maintenance hangar**.  
2. If you have already completed **“Catapulted into Orbit,”** a pirate ambush will trigger as part of **“Mercenaries vs. Mercenaries.”**  
   - Defeat the pirates, then disembark.  
3. Proceed down the main corridor and talk to the **guard**.  
   - Use **bribe** or **lie** (requires **85 Speech**) to convince him to let you pass.  
4. After the conversation, cross the area, open the far‑back door, and descend the stairs.  
5. Clear the lab of **robots**.  
6. Once the lab is clean, head west along the corridor, destroy the **drones**, and take the **elevator** at the far end.  
7. On the final level, neutralize the **security drones** in the plantation and meet **Marion** in her lab.  
8. Retrieve the **VAH Pass**.  
9. Talk to Marion for advice and **persuade** her (requires **55 Persuasion**) to obtain her **security code** (optional but saves time later).

## Alternative Options

- **All‑out aggression:**  
  - Skip negotiations and kill everyone in the area, including **Marion**.  
  - You will still obtain the **VAH Pass**, but you will miss the extra information from Marion.

- **Stealth infiltration:**  
  - Avoid both negotiating and killing.  
  - Sneak into the lab via the side passage on the left side of the main corridor.  

---  

**Key Items:** **VAH Pass**, **Security Code** (optional)  
**Key Skills:** **Speech (85)** for bribery/lying, **Persuasion (55)** for Marion.
