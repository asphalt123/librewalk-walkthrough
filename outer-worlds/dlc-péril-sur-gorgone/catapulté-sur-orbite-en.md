
## 1. Reach the Pharmacy on Olympus  
1. From the **Impostor**, travel to the specialized pharmacy unit located on Olympus.  
2. If you have already finished the “Mechanical Pseudapple” quest, Flibusters will ambush you on arrival and the “Mercenaries vs. Mercenaries” side quest will begin.  
3. Land on Olympus and proceed inside the station.  

## 2. Convince Kelley to Open the Door *(55 Persuasion or 90 Deception + 40 Medicine or 90 Intimidation)*  
1. Interact with the intercom near the right‑hand door and attempt to persuade the woman locked in the locker to open it.  
2. **If successful**:  
   - Talk to **Kelley** and ask about **Dr. Jasper**.  
   - Agree to bring her aboard the **Impostor**.  
   - Receive the unique armor **Mono‑lens Cellular Stability Armor**.  
   - You may also extort **500 Octo** from her for the rescue.  

## 3. Traverse the Station to Reach Kelley *(if persuasion fails)*  
1. Go north through the first door and **neutralize the security drones**.  
2. Take the **elevator** to the upper level.  
3. Eliminate the next group of robots and pass through the large southwest door.  
4. Head southeast, then **descend the ladder** to reach the area where Kelley is located.  

## 4. Interrogate Dr. Jasper Low  
1. From Kelley’s position, climb the ladder and **clear all remaining robots**.  
2. Enter the southwest room and **collect the Habitat Station Map** from the desk.  
3. Use the map to **unlock the door** and meet **Dr. Jasper Low**.  

## 5. Reactivate the Station Turbine  
1. Open the indicated door using **Jasper’s access card**.  
2. Ride the elevator to the top of the tower.  
3. Access the terminal and attempt to restart the turbine.  
4. **Clear the path of robots** and **lower both levers** to reactivate the two turbine generators.  
5. Return to the terminal and **restart the turbine** permanently.  

## 6. Return to Jasper for Rewards  
1. Speak with **Jasper** again. He will give you the **Chemistry Lab password**.  
2. Receive the unique weapon **Employee Perks**.  
3. **Optional (55 Intimidation or 90 Persuasion)**: Ask for additional information to obtain the **Chemistry Lab security pass**, which speeds up the “Perilous Experiment” quest.  

## 7. Alternative Outcomes  

- **Kill Dr. Low** instead of repairing the turbine. Loot the **Chemistry Lab password** and the **Employee Perks** weapon from his corpse.  
- **Non‑violent option**: Use **90 Lockpicking** to steal the password from Dr. Low without killing him.
