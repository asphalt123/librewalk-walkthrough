
## Start  
1. Go to the **Chemistry Lab entrance**.  
2. Use **Jasper’s recording** to unlock the building door.  
3. Enter the lab, head east to the cave, and follow the walkways.  

## If you have Jasper’s **Security Pass** (from “Catapulted to Orbit”)  
1. Proceed directly to the **locked door** in the east.  
2. Open the door with the **Security Pass**.  
3. Carefully navigate the **trapped tunnel** ahead.  
4. Continue toward the objective further east.  

## If you do **not** have the **Security Pass**  
1. Go down to the lower level.  
2. Take the **southern tunnel**.  
3. Fight or avoid the **contaminated primates** to reach **Jasper’s office**.  

## Unlock the Synthesis Building and Finish  
1. At Jasper’s office, use the **terminal** to force‑unlock the **Synthesis and Manufacturing building**.  
2. Exit through the **north door** from your current position to leave the building and complete the quest.
