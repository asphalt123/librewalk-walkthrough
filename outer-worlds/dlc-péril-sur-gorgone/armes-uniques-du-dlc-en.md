
## Doctor’s Orders  
- Go to **Ambrose Manor**.  
- Retrieve the kitchen access card from the guard’s shack.  
- Use the card to enter the kitchen on the ground floor.  
- Inside, go through the tunnel and ascend to Olivia’s office via the secret passage.  
- Take the bedroom access card from Olivia’s desk.  
- Unlock the bedroom door, exit into the hallway.  
- Open the bedroom door at the far end of the hallway and take the unique weapon from the left‑hand furniture.  

## Grilled  
- Find the weapon on the corpse of **Wilhelmina Ambrose**.  

## Deconstruction Tool  
- Purchase from **Khaleel** at his store in the Gorgon landing area.  

## Overheat  
- At Roscoe’s location on the first floor of the Sprat’s Cabin, jump onto the conveyor belt.  
- Crouch to move forward, then climb to the upper level where the unique weapon is located.  

## Coolant Dispenser  
- Locate near a corpse in the mining‑accident zone of the mine north‑east of Gorgon.  

## Special Delivery  
- Retrieve the weapon from the corpse of **Charles the Accountant** in the Marauder’s Bastion.  

## Perfect Match  
- In the Lobby of the Reactive Incubation Office, either:  
  - Hack the terminal (35 hack) to unlock the display case, **or**  
  - Pick one of the western doors (80 lockpicking).  
- Pass through the opened door, go through the wall opening, and take the weapon from the stand.  

## Burst  
- Find the weapon on the bar south of the 4th floor of the Creative Incubation Office.  

## Luck  
- Search the cul‑de‑sac west of the Chemistry lab entrance.  

## Airstrike  
- Purchase from **Surely Carl** at his shop in the Gorgon Ruins.  

## Contraband  
- At the Control Post, use the southern terminal to read messages and discover an access code.  
- Print an access card using the same terminal with the password **“çasuffitlesconneriescarter.”**  
- Travel to the north‑east shack, unlock the door with the printed card, and collect the weapon from the left‑hand tablet.  

## Dart Pistol  
- After obtaining the Control Post access card (see Contraband), enter the north‑west shack and retrieve the dealer’s reserve access card.  
- Go to the Gorgon Ruins, enter the destroyed cabin in the north, and use the reserve card to open the chest containing the weapon.  

## Medal of Honor  
- Receive as a reward for completing the quest **“Love Expected, Death in Sight.”**  

## The B.E.N.  
- Find in the secret hideout of the junior super‑scientists during the quest **“On the Trail of a Hero.”**  

## A Very Dangerous Game  
- Open the locked safe (150 lockpicking) in the Musgrave collection.  
- Accessible during the quest **“Almost Safe.”**  

## Discharge  
- Retrieve from the corpse of **Dr. Marion Blakeslee** encountered in the quest **“Mechanical Pseudapple.”**  

## Tossball Grip (Renowned Player)  
- During the quest **“Mercenaries vs Mercenaries,”** board the Flibustier ship.  
- Get the crew access card from the toilets on the second level.  
- Unlock the nearby cabin and claim the unique weapon.  

## Storm Cannon  
- In the quest **“Catapulted into Orbit,”** go to the workshop’s second level on the station and collect the weapon.  

## Employee Perks  
- Given by **Dr. Jasper Low** during **“Catapulted into Orbit.”**  
- Can also be taken from his corpse or stolen with 90 lockpicking skill.  

## Agony  
- Locate in Test Chamber 3 of the VAH building.  
- Accessible via the quest **“At Spacer’s Choice, We…”**  

## Shock and Awe  
- Found in the supervisor’s safe of the VAH building.  
- Accessible via the same quest **“At Spacer’s Choice, We…”**  

## Regal Water  
- During the quest **“Perilous Experiment”:**  
  1. Go to the 3rd level of the Chemistry lab and retrieve Jasper’s locker key from the north locker.  
  2. Proceed to Jasper’s office on the 2nd level and unlock the east door with the key.  
  3. Descend the stairs and take the weapon from the tablet.  

## Sword Wall  
- Earn as a reward for completing the quest **“Investigative Journalism”** and turning in the recordings to Roscoe.  

## Gravedigger  
- Behind a locked door (60 lockpicking) on the 1st level of the Synthesis & Manufacturing Center.  
- Accessible during the quest **“The Bureaucracy Seal.”**  

## Prototype Pistol  
- Found on **Olivia Ambrose’s** desk during the quest **“The Bureaucracy Seal.”**  

## Balance  
- Kill **Olivia Ambrose** during the quest **“Ambrose Parades.”**  
- Loot the weapon from her corpse, or throw her into the reactor to find the weapon on the command console.  

## Crusher  
- Open the locked safe (100 lockpicking) in Olivia’s personal lab.  
- Accessible during **“Ambrose Parades”** if you meet Minnie.  

## Shovel  
- Search the locked trash can (80 lockpicking) in the discreet cave.  
- Accessible via the quest **“Ambrose Parades.”**
