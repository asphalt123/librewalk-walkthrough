## Side Quest: “On the Trail of a Hero”

**Acquisition**  
- Terminal of the Secret Society of Subordinate Super‑Scientists (located at the entrance of a hidden hideout east of Sprat’s Cabin).

**Rewards**  
- **2250 Octo**  
- Unique weapon **Le B.E.N**

## Quest Activation  
1. Go to the entrance of the hidden hideout (cave east of Sprat’s Cabin).  
2. Interact with the terminal to start the side quest.

## Find the Four Clues  

### Clue 1  
- From the hideout, cross the southern bridge.  
- Follow the path to Maurice’s research site.  
- Retrieve the **comic book** there – this is the first clue.

### Clue 2  
- Travel to the mine north‑east of the hideout.  
- Reach the end of the gallery to Virgil’s research site.  
- Collect the second clue.

### Clue 3  
- Use the southern bridge again and follow the path to the Gorgon Ruins.  
- Pass through the habitation complex and head north to Brigitte’s research site.  
- Obtain the third clue.

### Clue 4  
- Take the elevator at the far south of the area to the dump.  
- Follow the upper‑level path to Eustace’s research site.  
- Pick up the final **comic book**.

## Complete the Quest  

1. Return to the hidden hideout and interact with the terminal again.  
2. Enter the following password answers:  

   - **Answer 1:** *Sissak Sassafrass*  
   - **Answer 2:** *The skull of the Alpha King’s mother*  
   - **Answer 3:** *By deceiving Archibald with his vile mind‑control ray*  
   - **Answer 4:** *With a lightning strike from the sky*  

3. If you have **150 or more Hack** skill, you can activate the **“Super‑Villain Protocol”** to skip the quiz and gain a small experience bonus.  
4. After the password is accepted, the hideout door unlocks.

## Claim the Rewards  

- Enter the unlocked hideout.  
- Collect **2250 Octo** from the locker.  
- Pick up the unique weapon **Le B.E.N** from the table.
