
## Meet Clarence Quäsiment  

1. Activate the quest, then return to the **Impostor** and travel to **Byzantium**.  
2. Head west to the **Musgrave Collection**.  
3. In the lobby, talk to **Nina**, then enter the main hall.  
4. Follow the **objective marker** to locate **Clarence**.  
5. Question Clarence about the events on Gorgon and persuade him to help cancel the emergency lock.  
6. Agree to help him retrieve the **contest trophy** to unlock new objectives.  
7. (Optional) Return **Clarence’s eye** (obtained in the previous quest) during dialogue to slightly improve your reputation with the **Council**.  

## Steal the Contest Trophy  

1. After the conversation with Clarence, go to **Tilda**.  
   - If you have **55 Persuasion**, persuade her to give you the **Technician Access Card**. (The card saves time later but is not essential.)  
2. Descend the stairs and talk to **Milburn**.  
   - Persuade him to treat his dog with more respect (requires **35 Inspiration** or **55 Persuasion**) to earn a small reputation boost with the Council.  
3. You will now have custody of the small **Anubis** dog, which appears in your cabin on the **Impostor**.  
4. Take the elevator down to the building’s basement.  
5. In the basement:  
   - If you have the **Technician Access Card**, open the door on the right.  
   - Otherwise, go left, locate the **technician’s corpse**, enter the exhibition room, and use the elevator to return to the upper floor.  
6. On the upper floor, retrieve the **contest trophy** and unlock the side door.  
7. Return to **Clarence** and give him the trophy to receive the information you need.
