
## Quest Start
1. Go to the **Sprat Cabin** and head to the first floor.  
2. Talk to **Roscoe Clanton**, listen briefly, and accept his offer to start the side quest.

## Find the Four Phonographs

### 1. BIC (Biotechnical Institute Complex)
- Unlock the door using the **preserved eye** obtained from the quest *“Those Who Fled Gorgon.”*  
- Go to the second floor.  
- Retrieve the phonograph located in a piece of furniture in the projection room.

### 2. Administrative Apartments
- Enter the cabin on the first floor.  
- Collect **Dr. Ambrose’s Interview – Part 2**.

### 3. VAH Building (after completing “Mechanical Pseudapple”)
- Complete the quest *“Mechanical Pseudapple”* to obtain the **VAH building pass**.  
- Inside, go to the third floor.  
- Retrieve the phonograph hidden behind a conduit.

### 4. Chemistry Lab (after completing “Catapulted into Orbit”)
- Finish the quest *“Catapulted into Orbit”* to gain access to the **Chemistry Lab**.  
- Inside, go to the third floor.  
- Find the phonograph concealed in the western closet.

### 5. Dump (South of Gorgon)
- Leave the Chemistry Lab and travel to the dump area south of Gorgon.  
- Enter the guard’s cabin.  
- Retrieve the phonograph from the locker on the right.

## Return the Recordings

### Option A – Give Recordings to Georgie’s Contact
- **Reward:** 2250 octos + **Operation Armor** (found in the Scraps).  
- Locate the robot **NTVUR** near the Sprat Cabin.  
- Activate it using the code **“Ere Glaciaire.”**  
- Read the message, then agree to hand over the recordings.  
- You will learn Georgie’s true identity, receive a small reputation boost with **Aunt Cleo**, and obtain the Operation Armor.

### Option B – Give Recordings to Roscoe
- **Reward:** 1250 octos + unique weapon **Wall of Swords**.  
- Meet Roscoe at the **Sprat Cabin**. He will give you the octos and the Wall of Swords.  
- If you have **≥80 Persuasion**, you can also convince him to give an additional **750 octos**.
