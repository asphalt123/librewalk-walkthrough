
## By Instinct

1. Activate the quest.  
2. Travel to the **Distillation Station**.  
   - Fight through the hosts to reach the side door of the building.  
3. Inside the station, interact with the **intercom** on the side to learn the truth about **Helen**.  
4. At the end of the dialogue, make a choice:

   - **Work with Administrator Ludovico**  
     1. Unlock the quest **“Helen Halcyon's Infernal Explosive Machine.”**  
     2. This path ultimately leads to condemning Eridan.  

   - **Side with Helen Halcyon**  
     1. Activate the quest **“Factory Escape.”**  
     2. Continue with missions aimed at saving the colony from parasites.
