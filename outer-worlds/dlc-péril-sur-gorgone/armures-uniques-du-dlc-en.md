
## Night Light  
- **Location:** On the ground opposite Virgile’s research site in the mine northeast of Gorgon.  

## Chrono‑Adaptation Suit  
- **Obtained:** Reward for completing the quest **“Lami Lama.”**  

## Electrician’s Gear  
- **Location:** Inside a locker on the second level of the Precursor auxiliary hangar.  
- **Unlocks:** After starting the quest **“Mechanical Pseudapple.”**  

## Mono‑Lens of Cellular Stability  
- **Given by:** **Kelley** during the quest **“Catapulted into Orbit”** if you agree to place her on the Impostor.  

## Operation Helmet (Wasteland)  
- **Location:** Inside a locker in the caretaker’s hut at the Gorgon dump.  

## Operation Armor (Wasteland)  
- **Reward:** Completing the quest **“Investigative Journalism”** and handing the recordings to **NVTUR**.  

## Unconditional Fan  
- **Location:** On the third level of the Synthesis and Manufacturing Center.  
- **Accessible after:** Starting the quest **“Seal of Bureaucracy.”**  

## Fear Suit  
- **Location:** In a trash can near the Adrénachronine synthesizer on the fifth level of the Synthesis and Manufacturing Center.  
- **Accessible after:** Starting the quest **“Seal of Bureaucracy.”**  

## Prototype Hibernation Gear  
- **Items:** **Prototype Hibernation Helmet rev 3. 19f** and **Prototype Hibernation Suit rev 4. 7d**  
- **Location:** On the second level of the basement of the Synthesis and Manufacturing Center.  
- **Accessible after:** Starting the quest **“Ambrose Parées.”**
