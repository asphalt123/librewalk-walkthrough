
## Mission Steps

1. Travel back to **Gorgon** and head to the entrance of the **VAH building**.  
2. Use **Marion’s pass** to unlock the front door.  
3. Inside, go up the stairs and take the side tunnel. Continue forward until you encounter the first test subjects.  
4. Eliminate these enemies and proceed to the **office area**.  
5. *Optional shortcut*:  
   - If you obtained **Marion’s security code** during the “Mechanical Pseudapple” quest, activate it on the southern **terminal** to open a shortcut.  
   - If you do not have the code, you must pass through the two test chambers that are filled with subjects.  
6. In either case, reach the south corridor and enter the shaft that leads to the lower level.  
7. Once on the lower level, go through the door on your left.  
8. Locate and use the **elevator** a short distance ahead; ride it to the top floor.  
9. Enter the **supervisor’s office**.  
10. Activate the unlock signal from the **terminal** to finish the quest.
