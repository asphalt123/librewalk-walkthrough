## Main Quest: “Those Who Fled Gorgon”

### Meet Wilhelmina Ambrose
1. After unlocking the DLC and activating the quest, go to the Impostor’s cargo hold.  
2. Retrieve the **Phonograph** from the package.  
3. After the opening cutscene, pick up the **Phonograph** from the table.  
4. Travel to **Ambrose Manor** on Gorgon.  
5. Enter the building, go upstairs, and speak with **Wilhelmina Ambrose** in the lounge.  
6. Exhaust all dialogue to learn about **Chance Montoya** and the experiments on Gorgon.  
7. Return to the Impostor and travel to Gorgon’s landing zone.  
8. Head to the **Sprat’s Cabin** (the local bar) and enter.

### Follow the Trail to Sprat’s Cabin
1. Talk to **Hoffman** and let him inspect you.  
2. Use the elevator to go down to the lower level.  
3. Listen to the conversation between **Freddie** and **Trixie**.  
4. Interact with either **Freddie** or **Trixie** to add your input.  
5. Accept Lex’s request to intervene and begin listening to Freddie.  

### Choices at Sprat’s Cabin (Multiple Outcomes)

#### Option 1 – Side with Freddie, Trixie, or Neither  
- Listen to Freddie’s testimony, then Trixie’s.  
- Grant the wreck rights to either Freddie, Trixie, or refuse both.  
- **Reward:** **6 Algae Brownies** + **2000 credits** (from Lex).  

#### Option 2 – Accept Freddie’s Bribe  
- After Freddie’s first story, say “You’re not very convincing right now, Freddie.”  
- Accept his bribe.  
- Grant exploitation rights to Freddie.  
- **Reward:** **6 Algae Brownies** + **4000 credits** (including Lex’s bonus).  

### After the Optional Sequence
1. Talk to **Lex** again and ask about **Montoya** to update your objectives.  
2. Go to the first floor, enter the first room on the right, and meet **Leonora**.  
3. Accept Leonora’s proposal to start the side mission “Lost Love, Death in Sight.”  
4. Ask Leonora about **Chance Montoya** for new information.  
5. Return to the hallway and enter the opposite room to speak with **Roscoe**.  
6. Agree to help Roscoe to unlock the side quest “Investigative Journalism,” then ask him about Chance.  
7. Ascend to the top floor and enter **Montoya’s bedroom**.  
8. Read the note on the desk, use the discovered code to unlock the safe, and retrieve the **Eye** inside.

### Visit the Creative Incubation Bureau
1. Leave Sprat’s Cabin and head southeast to the **Creative Incubation Bureau** entrance.  
2. Approach the door and use the **Eye** on the scanner to unlock access.  
3. Inside, cross the lobby and take the stairs to the northeast to reach the second floor.  
4. Navigate through offices, eliminating marauders as needed.  
5. If you have **35 Hacking** skill, hack a nearby computer to reprogram the security turrets on the third floor.  
6. Continue upward to the fourth floor.  
7. Enter the **Administration Office** and use the terminal to attempt access to the synthesis and manufacturing center.  
8. After the first failed attempt, try again to force access; a strange individual will contact you.  

### Locate Laboratory Personnel
1. After the conversation, return to the fourth‑floor stairs and exit the building.  
2. Traverse the area to the **Gorgon Ruins**.  
3. Enter the **Alpha Transmission Tower** and use the terminal to locate **Clarence Quasiment**.  
4. Completing this step ends the main quest.
