
## 1. Complete the prerequisite quest  
1. Finish all objectives of the quest **Radio Libre Monarque**.  

## 2. Report to Phineas  
1. Go to Phineas’s laboratory and submit your report.  

## 3. Return to the Impostor and travel to the Colonies  
1. Board the starship **Impostor**.  
2. Choose any destination within the Colonies and travel there.  

## 4. Receive the courier call and start the new quest  
1. Upon arrival, you will receive a call from a courier informing you that a package has been left in the cargo hold.  
2. The quest **Those Who Fled Gorgon** will activate.  
3. Open the package to begin the new adventure.
