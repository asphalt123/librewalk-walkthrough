
## Quest Activation  
1. After the quest triggers, go to the **CSM office**.  
2. Unlock the door using the terminal.  
3. Enter the office and meet **PAM**, the multifunctional automaton.  

## Activate PAM’s Combat Functions (Optional)  
You must later convince PAM that you work for **Spacer’s Choice** (45 + points in **Deception**, **Inspiration**, or **Intimidation**). Choose one of the following methods to reactivate PAM’s combat abilities:  

- **Parvati** in your party: talk to her and let her bypass PAM’s security protocols.  
- **SAM** in your party: ask SAM to connect to PAM and recharge its batteries.  
- Retrieve the **derogation code** located in the east lounge and input it to override PAM’s limitations.  
- Find a **spare battery** in the west office, pick it up, and install it on PAM.  

## Retrieve Olivia’s Journal  
1. Once PAM is reactivated, follow it through the building.  
2. Assist PAM in eliminating the marauder forces occupying the area.  
3. Continue with PAM to the **third floor** and take the elevator down.  
4. At the top level, cross the area and enter **Olivia’s office**.  
5. Pick up the **journal** placed near the terminal to complete the objective.  
6. Exit Olivia’s office through the small opening on the left.  

## Return to Complete the Quest  
1. Follow the on‑screen marker to the nearest elevator.  
2. Ride the elevator back to the ground floor.  
3. Leave the building and return to **the Impostor** to finish the quest.
