
## Quest: Helen and the Slugs

1. **Travel to the helmsman's station** and enter the navigation bridge museum.  
2. **Fight through SLUG soldiers** inside the museum.  
3. **Talk to PHAETON** to update your objectives.  
4. **Proceed to the lower levels** to reactivate the reactor:  
   - Use the **south elevator**.  
   - If you have already completed the quest **“The Monster of PHAETON,”** you may instead take the **north elevator**.  
5. In the **main maintenance room**, eliminate all SLUG personnel and **kill Sedrick Kincannon**.  
6. **Pull the indicated lever**, then return upstairs to locate **PHAETON**.  
7. At the computer, you will encounter **Helen Halcyon**:  
   - Talk to her briefly.  
   - **Eliminate Helen Halcyon**.  
8. **Loot** the following unique items from her corpse:  
   - **The Needle**  
   - **Outfit of the Day (Option 2)**  
9. With the items in your inventory, **activate the gas dispersion procedure via PHAETON** to complete the quest.
