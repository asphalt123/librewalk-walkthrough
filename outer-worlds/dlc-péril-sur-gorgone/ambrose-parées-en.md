
## Side 1 – Support Minnie, Eliminate Olivia  

1. Enter the **CSM building** and fight your way through the pirates.  
2. If **PAM** is still active from the *Seal of Bureaucracy* quest, reactivate it and request assistance.  
3. Head north, then take the stairs down to the basement.  
4. Clear each room of the pirates.  
5. Ride the elevator to the final chamber where **Olivia Ambrose** is located.  
6. Talk briefly with Olivia (no dialogue can convince her) and then kill her.  
7. Activate the synthesizer at the nearby console.  
8. Use the marked elevator to return to the surface.  
9. Go to **Minnie** at the Ambrose Manor, claim your reward, and finish the DLC.  

## Side 2 – Eliminate Minnie for Olivia  

1. Go to **Ambrose Manor** and destroy the robots in the entrance hall.  
2. Go upstairs and use Olivia’s office terminal to unlock the secret elevator.  
3. Return to the ground floor and take the newly unlocked elevator.  
4. Neutralize the test subjects in the first room.  
5. Clear the hallway robots, then approach the back door and start dialogue with **Minnie**.  
6. End the conversation and fight **Minnie**.  
7. After the fight, enter the back room and activate the **AC protocol** from the terminal to destroy the Gorgon project.  
8. After activating the protocol, the **CSM building** on Gorgon becomes inaccessible.  
9. Follow the marked ladder, cross the cavern, and meet **Olivia** in the manor’s lounge to claim your reward and finish the DLC.  

## Convince Minnie to Drop Her Weapons (150 Persuasion)  

1. During the conversation with **Minnie**, propose an alliance with **Olivia** and point out that Minnie cannot compromise.  
2. Choose the dialogue option that requires **100 Persuasion** (or **100 Science**) and then the option that requires **150 Persuasion**.  
3. Minnie realizes her mistake, agrees to let you destroy the **CSM complex** without resistance.  
4. Return to **Ambrose Manor** and find **Olivia** to celebrate the double victory: Minnie survives and the lab is destroyed.  

## Negotiate an Agreement Between Olivia and Minnie (150 Persuasion)  

1. Go to **Ambrose Manor** and make your way to **Minnie**.  
2. Calm the situation and suggest an alliance with **Olivia**.  
3. Select the dialogue options requiring **65 Persuasion** (or **65 Inspiration**).  
4. Follow up with the options requiring **100** and then **150 Persuasion** to convince **Minnie** to side with her mother.  
5. Leave the manor and head to the **CSM basement** on Gorgon.  
6. Confront **Olivia**, tell her to shut up, and repeat Minnie’s line about “coming for tea” to prove your good faith.  
7. Return to **Ambrose Manor** and witness the emotional reunion between mother and daughter.  

## Alternative Outcomes  

- If you negotiate a truce between **Olivia** and **Minnie** but still destroy the **CSM** lab from the terminal, **Minnie** will attack you; you must kill her.  
- If you act as the worst rogue, you can kill both **Minnie** and **Olivia** at the manor and the **CSM** without destroying the lab. The mission ends with no reward.
