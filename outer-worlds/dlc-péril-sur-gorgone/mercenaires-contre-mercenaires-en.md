
## Prerequisites  
- Complete **“Mechanical Apple”** or **“Catapulted into Orbit”**.

## Situation  
After finishing one of those quests, Flibustier pirates will attack you while traveling in the Halcyon system. **ADA** will brief you and present two options.

## 1. Accept ADA’s Proposal and Flee  

1. Choose ADA’s disengagement option.  
2. The pirates retreat, ending the quest instantly.  
3. Receive the experience reward. *(You will not encounter these pirates again and will miss a unique weapon.)*  

## 2. Deal with the Pirates Personally  

### Preparation  
1. Tell **ADA** you want to handle the pirates yourself.  
2. Board the enemy vessel.  

You can either **negotiate** with the captain or **eliminate** everyone on board.  

### 2A. Negotiate with the Pirates  
*(Requirements: **95 Intimidation + Strength** *or* **95 Persuasion + Charm**, and a pistol)*  

1. Use the door intercom to contact **Coach Stilley**.  
2. Pass the appropriate skill check (intimidation or persuasion) to arrange a face‑to‑face meeting.  
3. Go through the unlocked door, ascend to the upper deck, and enter the captain’s cabin.  
4. Choose one of the following dialogue actions:  
   - **Intimidate** the captain.  
   - **Bribe** the captain.  
   - **Lie** to the captain (you can also intimidate after lying to obtain **700 Octo**).  
5. After negotiations, return to the lower deck.  
6. Unlock the cockpit door with the key given by **Stilley**.  
7. Retrieve the **tracker** from the terminal.  
8. Return to **ADA** to complete the quest.  

### 2B. Kill All Pirates  

1. If you cannot arrange a meeting, go through the eastern door and open fire.  
2. After the initial combat, loot a **galley access card** from a corpse and go upstairs.  
3. Continue fighting and enter the captain’s cabin.  
4. You have another chance to negotiate; if you prefer not to, kill the captain.  
5. Take the captain’s **access card**.  
6. Retrieve the **tracker** from the cockpit.  
7. Return to **ADA** to finish the quest.  

## 3. Alternative Outcomes  

- You may arrange a meeting with **Stilley** early and then kill him inside his cabin.  
- You can still negotiate with the coach even after eliminating most of his crew.
