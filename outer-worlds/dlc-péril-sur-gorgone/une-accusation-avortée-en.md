
## Prerequisites
- Complete **at least two** of the following side missions before the quest triggers:  
  - *The Prince of Tossball*  
  - *The Disturber*  
  - *The Queen of Productivity*  
  - *Lord of the Chimeras*  

> Starting this quest before finishing **all four** missions will cause the remaining missions to fail.

## Steps
1. **Activate the quest** – it becomes available automatically after meeting the prerequisite above.  
2. Go to **your bedroom** in the ship/compound.  
3. Approach **Ludovico** and select the dialogue option: *“I’m ready to tell you who killed Helen.”*  
4. When prompted to name the **assassin**, you have **no concrete evidence**, so you may choose **any character** (including **Ludovico**) as the culprit.  
5. Confirm your choice to **complete the accusation** and finish the quest.  

## Outcome
- The quest ends immediately after you name a suspect, regardless of who you pick.  
- You can proceed to the next main storyline mission.
