
## Acquisition
**Leonora Diallo**

## Rewards
**750 octos** + **Medal of Honor** (unique weapon)

## Steps
1. Go to **Sprat Cabin**, head to the first floor and talk to **Leonora** to start the side mission.  
2. Leave the cabin and travel to the **Gorgon Ruins**.  
3. Eliminate the **Marauders** in the area.  
4. Locate **Jerome’s journal** behind the bar south of the dwellings and read it to update your objective.  
5. Ascend to the second level of the dwellings and enter the apartment at the far end.  
6. Inside the apartment, pick up **Jerome’s flask** from the left‑hand bed.  
7. Return to **Leonora**, complete the dialogue, and receive your reward, including the **Medal of Honor** weapon.  
   - During the final conversation you may choose to reveal the truth to Leonora or hide Jerome’s fate; the choice does not affect the quest outcome.
