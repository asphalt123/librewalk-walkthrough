
## Prerequisite
- Must have placed **Adelaide** as the leader of Edgewater during the mission “And the Electricity Was”.

## Steps
1. Travel to **Emerald Valley**.  
2. Head toward the **geothermal plant**.  
3. Enter the plant building and talk to **Hogarth**. Convince him to let you access the plant.  
4. Once inside, cross the area and use the indicated **terminal** to activate the robot elimination protocol.  
5. Go to **Edgewater** and destroy all remaining **robots** roaming the streets.  
6. Return to **Sophia Akande** to complete the quest.
