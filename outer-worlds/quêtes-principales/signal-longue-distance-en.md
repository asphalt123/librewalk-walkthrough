
## Main Path
1. Go to **Phineas' laboratory**.  
2. Reach the **main room**.  
3. Interact with the **terminal** on the right side and **place the bug**.  
4. Return to **Sophia**.  
5. Talk to **Perceval** to complete the quest and receive your reward.  

- After completing the quest, **Perceval** will sell various items, including **two unique weapons**.

## Alternative Path (Working with Welles)
1. Speak with **Welles** in his laboratory.  
2. Use the **terminal** to **place a corrupted tracking signal**.
