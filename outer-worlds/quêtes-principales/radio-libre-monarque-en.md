
## Starting the Quest
1. After obtaining the **Navigation Key to Stellar Bay**, travel to **Monarque** and land in the city.  
2. Go to the **Yacht Club** and talk to **Nyoka**.  
   - Ask about the hermit.  
   - Accept the **“Fetish Pills”** quest.  
   - **Nyoka** will join your crew.

## Reaching the Summit of Devil’s Peak
You must travel across the wildlands of Monarque to reach the summit. Choose one of two routes:

### 1. Ascend via the Trail
1. Follow the path that leads upward, fighting any hostile creatures you encounter.  
2. Arrive at the **E3 Barracks** and speak with the mercenaries for the **“Demonic Mandibles”** quest.

### 2. Ascend via the Caves
1. Enter the caves south of the mountain.  
2. Pass under the arch directly ahead and follow the tunnel to the next level.  
3. Continue forward until you encounter a giant **Regalian Mantis**.  
4. Defeat the mantis to trigger the **“Demonic Mandibles”** quest, then talk to **Berthold**.  
5. Exit the caves on the southern side and head back to the **E3 Barracks** to find **Berthold** again.

## After the Summit
1. Talk to **Berthold** (regardless of the chosen route) to secure his assistance in the upcoming battle.  
2. Climb back up the trail and meet **Joy**. Talk to her, then initiate the assault on the marauders.  

## Assault on the Marauders
1. Defeat all bandits outside the building.  
2. Go upstairs and enter the building.  
3. In the first room, neutralize the enemies, then proceed north.  

## Confronting the Hermit
1. Open the door at the far end of the room and use the intercom to speak with the hermit.  
2. Turn around and take the elevator on the right side of the previous room.  
3. On the upper floor, reach the central chamber and clear out the brigands with help from the **E3** mercenaries.  
4. After clearing the area, deal with the remaining enemies on the top level.  
5. Return to the intercom; the hermit will open the main door.  

## Interacting with Hiram
1. Talk to **Hiram** to learn about his connection to **Wells**.  
2. Complete the following side quests to restore communications on Monarque:  
   - **“His Name is BOLT”** (MSI)  
   - **“Hidden Errors”** (MSI)  
   - **“The Transporter”** (Iconoclasts)  
   - **“The Price of the Press”** (Iconoclasts)  
3. Return to **Hiram** for new objectives.  

## Reactivating Communications and Finishing the Main Quest
1. Go to the back room, exit through the door, and reach the rooftop.  
2. Approach the console and reactivate the communications array.  
3. Leave the area and meet **Wells** in his laboratory to complete the main quest.

## Alternative Options
- You may travel to **Monarque** before acquiring the **Navigation Key to Stellar Bay**, but you will need to cross half the planet to reach the city.  
- During the dialogue with **Nyoka**, you can decline her help and attempt to reach the hermit on your own.
