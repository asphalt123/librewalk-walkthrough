
## Talk to Udom
1. Go to the Precursor and locate the local Council office.  
2. Speak with **Udom** and tell him you have information about Wells.  
3. Accept his request to help activate the new mission.  

## Acquire the Council Seal
1. Leave the Council office and head to the Motel.  
2. Talk to **Gladys**; she will offer to sell you the **Council Seal** for **8,000 Octos**.  
3. Purchase the **Council Seal**.  

## Earn 8,000 Octos
1. Take on various side jobs around the Precursor to earn money.  
2. Continue completing jobs until you have at least **8,000 Octos**.  

## Finish the Quest
1. Return to **Gladys** (if you haven’t already) and buy the **Council Seal**.  
2. Go back to **Udom** and give him the **Council Seal**.  
3. Receive the **Byzance navigation key**, which unlocks the next main quest.
