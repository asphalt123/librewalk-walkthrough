
## Starting the Quest
1. Activate the quest from **Sophia**.  
2. Travel to **Stellar Bay**.  
3. Go to the local restaurant and talk to **Everett**.  
4. Interrogate Everett to learn about **Devil's Peak**.  
5. Visit **Nyoka**.  

## Main Objective (follow the “Radio Libre Monarque” steps)
- Proceed as you would in the **Radio Libre Monarque** quest to reach and capture the **Devil's Peak relay station**.  
- Restore communications on **Monarque**.  

## Completing the Mission
1. Return to **Hiram** and persuade him to side with the **Council**.  
2. Go back to **Sophia** to finish the quest and claim your reward.
