
## Obtain the Quest and Head to Byzance Exploration Office
1. Receive the quest from **Sophia Akande**.  
2. Leave the area and travel to the **Byzance Exploration Office**.  
3. Talk to the **receptionist**.

## Acquire the Key to Lockwood’s Office
- If you have enough **Intelligence** or **Persuasion**, convince the receptionist to give you the **key to Lockwood’s office**.  
- If not, either:
  - Steal the key from the receptionist, **or**  
  - Search the closet near the elevator for the receptionist’s corpse to unlock new dialogue options.

## Enter Lockwood’s Office and Gather Information
1. Take the elevator to the floor with Lockwood’s office.  
2. Unlock the office using the **key**.  
3. Retrieve the **maps**.  
4. Use the terminal to read messages that reveal the **cartographer’s location**.

## Locate the Cartographer in the Maintenance Tunnels
1. Exit the building.  
2. Go to the **maintenance tunnels**.  
3. Reach **Lockwood’s position** (the area indicated by the terminal).

## Decide How to Deal with the Mercenary (Lockwood)
Choose one of the following approaches:

- **Let the mercenary kill the target**  
  - Loot **2000 Octos** from Lockwood’s corpse.  
  - Receive only half of the quest reward from Sophia.

- **Persuade Sherman to listen to Lockwood (requires 65 Persuasion)**  
  - Convince the mercenary to accept Lockwood’s proposal.  
  - Receive **1250 Octos** from Lockwood, but only a small reward from Sophia.

- **Kill the mercenary and claim the full bounty**  
  - Eliminate all enemies.  
  - Return to **Sophia** to collect the full payout.

## Alternative Way to Find Lockwood’s Location
1. Talk to **Mr. Billingsy** in his bar.  
2. Offer him **4700 Octos**.  
3. He will reveal Lockwood’s location.
