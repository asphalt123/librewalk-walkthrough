
## 1. Travel to Tartare Prison
1. Obtain the **Navigation Key** in Welles’ laboratory.  
2. Use it to set a course for Tartare Prison and dock.  

## 2. Obtain a Security Badge (optional but recommended)
1. When a corporate guard contacts you, use a high **Lie**, **Intimidation**, or **Persuasion** skill (78+).  
2. Convince the guard to give you a **Security Agent Badge**.  
3. This badge lets you bypass several unnecessary fights.

## 3. Enter the Prison
1. Exit the **Impostor** ship and proceed to the docks.  
2. Pass through the large door at the far end.  
3. Take the side elevator up to the next level.  

## 4. Reach the Hangar and the Fosse
1. Go down the stairs and enter the hangar.  
2. Stay on the left side of the area and climb the ladder at the far end.  
3. Open the large door you reach and head toward **The Pit** (the Fosse) at the end of the corridor.  
4. Turn right, cross the walkway, and locate the next elevator a short distance ahead.  

## 5. Ascend and Find the Quest Marker
1. Take the elevator to the upper floor.  
2. Turn right again, go through the large door, then head left down the corridor until you see the quest marker.  

## 6. Confront President Rockwell
1. Use the terminal to unlock the door near the marker.  
2. Enter and speak with **President Rockwell**.  
   - If you have high dialogue skills, try to convince him to side with you.  
   - If you fail or prefer to skip, simply leave the room.  

### Bug Workaround (if the game crashes when you open the door)
- Kill Rockwell from the corridor, **or**  
- Go to the far side of the area near the barricades, jump onto the concrete block, carefully walk along the railing, and re‑enter through the left door to reach Rockwell safely.

## 7. Proceed to the Upper Level
1. After dealing with Rockwell, head to the quest marker and take the central elevator to the prison’s summit.  

## 8. Deal with Sophia Akande
- **Option A – Convince Sophia to Stand Down**  
  1. Use high **Lie**, **Persuasion**, or **Intimidation** skills to persuade Sophia to drop her weapons.  
  2. If successful, take the right‑hand corridor directly to **Welles** and finish the mission.  

- **Option B – Fight Sophia and the Automaton**  
  1. If persuasion fails or you choose combat, go through the left door and confront the automaton **R.A.M.**  
  2. Use **electric weapons** for maximum damage.  
  3. After defeating R.A.M., loot the **Access Card** from its remains.  
  4. Use the Access Card to unlock the door at the far end of the area.  
  5. Ascend one floor, locate Sophia, and eliminate her.  
  6. With Sophia down, free **Welles**.

## 9. Alternate Outcomes
- **Working with Sophia**: President Rockwell will already be dead; you’ll find his corpse in the small filming room.  
- **Working with Sophia**: You must negotiate with **Phineas Welles** at the quest’s end. Use appropriate dialogue skills to convince him to surrender peacefully.
