
## Start the Quest
1. Receive the quest from **Wells**.  
2. Go to your cabin and pick up the **Holographic Veil** from your desk.  
3. Use your ship’s navigation system to set a course for the **Precursor**.

## Arrive at the Precursor
1. Dock at the **Precursor**.  
2. Go to the control station and talk to **Leonard** to unlock access to the rest of the ship.  
3. Proceed to the motel area.

## Meet Gladys
1. Enter the left‑most room at the back of the motel to encounter **Gladys**.

## Acquire the Navigation Key
- **Option 1:** Purchase the **Navigation Key** from Gladys for 10,000 Octos.  
- **Option 2:** If you lack funds, complete the side quest **“Distress Signal”** for Gladys.

## Lift the Ordinance
1. After deciding how to obtain the key, leave the motel.  
2. Travel to the local council headquarters.  
3. Speak with **Udom**; he will lift the ordinance restricting your ship.  
4. Your ship is now free to travel through space again.

## Retrieve the Navigation Key
1. Return to the **Precursor** when you are able.  
2. Speak with **Gladys** again and obtain the **Navigation Key** for Stella Bay.

## Finish the Quest
1. Return to the **Impostor**.  
2. Talk to **Wells** to complete the main quest.
