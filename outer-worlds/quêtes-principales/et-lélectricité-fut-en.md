
## Meet Max (Optional)  
1. After finishing the conversation with **Reed** during the quest **“A Stranger in a Strange Land”**, talk to **Parvati** to receive an optional objective.  
2. Leave the area and head toward the temple.  
3. Enter the temple and speak with **Max**.  

## Deliver Reed’s Message  
1. Leave **Edgewater** and travel north to the **Deserters’ Camp**.  
2. Speak with **Grace Romero** at the camp entrance.  
3. Enter the greenhouse.  
4. Talk to **Adelaide** to receive new quest objectives and ask her about agriculture, the deserters, and the plague for useful information.  

## Interrogate Adelaide about Gold Teeth (Optional)  
1. In the greenhouse, go to the far‑right room and take the **gold teeth** from the table on the left.  
2. If you previously questioned townspeople about **Eugène** during the quest **“To Our (Too Many!) Dear Deceased”**, use that knowledge to steer the conversation with Adelaide toward Eugène and her work.  
3. Learn that Adelaide is using the bodies of the townsfolk as fertilizer. This information helps later if you decide to redirect power to Edgewater.  

## Find the Power‑Redirection Switches  
1. Leave the Deserters’ Camp and travel to the **Power Plant**.  
2. Enter the building through the side breach.  
3. Clear or avoid the security robots and make your way inside.  
4. From the entry point, open the door ahead, then turn right.  
5. Interact with the terminal at the end of the hallway and attempt to reroute power to receive new objectives.  
6. Return to the entrance, go down the right ramp, and use **Reed’s access code** on the security console to unlock doors.  
7. Enter the next room, kill the robot on the lower level, then proceed to the right‑hand room and eliminate two more robots.  
8. Activate the **first redirection switch** located on the left side of the ensuing combat area.  
9. Continue forward to the circular room at the far end and descend to the lower level.  
10. Open the left door on this lower level and keep moving forward.  
11. In the next room, avoid steam bursts, climb the ramp, and activate the **second redirection switch**.  
12. Pass through the door opposite the switch, then locate and activate the **third and final redirection switch** on the upper floor.  
13. Return to the plant’s control room. You can now choose to divert power either to the **Botanical Lab** or to **Edgewater**.  

## Redirect Power to the Botanical Lab  
*Consequences: Edgewater is left without electricity; residents not in the Deserters’ Camp will die of the plague or be forced to leave the planet.*  

1. After selecting the botanical lab option, speak with **Adelaide** again, then travel back to **Edgewater**.  
2. Upon arrival, you will find the town in darkness—no power to houses or shops.  
3. Go to the **Canning Facility**, descend to the generator room in the basement, and locate **Reed**.  
4. If you have a very high **Intimidation** skill, you can persuade Reed to stand down without bloodshed. Otherwise, you must kill all of Reed’s soldiers before you can take the **electric regulator**.  

## Redirect Power to Edgewater  
1. After choosing to send power to Edgewater, report back to **Adelaide**. Two new options become available.  

### Convince the Workers to Return to Their Jobs  
*Consequences: Life resumes in Edgewater, but the population continues to suffer from malnutrition‑related diseases. Adelaide remains alone in the Botanical Lab.*  

1. After talking to Adelaide, approach the deserters and speak with **Thomas** or **Grace**.  
2. If you have already completed the quests **“Frantic Engineers”** and **“Kind Spirits”**, you can easily persuade them to go back to Edgewater.  
3. Inform Adelaide of the decision, then collect the **electric regulator**.  
4. Return to **Reed** and report the events. During this dialogue you can also slip a favor note for Thomas.  

### Convince Reed to Quit His Post  
*Consequences: Adelaide takes charge of Edgewater and works on agricultural techniques to eradicate the plague. Reed resigns and leaves Edgewater permanently.*  

1. During the conversation with Adelaide, ask her to let you handle Reed, which gives you the objective to convince him to quit.  
2. Travel to Edgewater and locate Reed’s office.  
3. Speak with Reed and steer the conversation toward the plague and the poor diet of Edgewater’s citizens.  
4. Reveal the information you learned about Adelaide’s use of bodies as fertilizer, prompting Reed to see the logic of the plan.  
5. After the dialogue, return to Adelaide, announce the outcome, retrieve the **electric regulator** from the lab, and head back to your ship.  

## Alternative Outcome  
*If you prefer not to convince Reed to quit:*  

1. Instead of persuading him, you may simply eliminate **Reed**.  
2. Be aware that killing Reed will cause **Parvati** to leave your party early unless you have sufficient **Persuasion** skill.
