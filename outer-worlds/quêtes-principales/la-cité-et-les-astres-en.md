
## 1. Reach the Byzance cargo landing area and talk to the Welles contact
- Go to the Byzance cargo landing area.  
- Speak with the **Welles** contact.

## 2. Enter Prime Minister Clarke’s house
- Approach the main door of Clarke’s house and talk to the guard.  
- After the dialogue, proceed to the **Halcyon Parcel Service** building to retrieve the minister’s package.

## 3. Obtain the Minister’s package *(choose one method)*
- **Persuade the employees (Intimidation or Persuasion 41)**  
  - Talk to **Olive** at the HPS reception and persuade her to give you the package.  

- **Steal the package (Lockpicking 60)**  
  - Go to the maintenance‑tunnel entrance in Byzance.  
  - Enter the HPS building through the service door.  
  - Wait for the guard to leave, pick the door opposite you, then take the package from the shelf.  

- After acquiring the package by either method, return to the guard outside Clarke’s house, show him the package, and he will let you inside.

## 4. Meet Clarke and activate “Rendez‑vous with Gaia”
- Go upstairs in Clarke’s house and talk to him for new objectives.  
- Activate the mission **Rendez‑vous with Gaia**.

## 5. Reach Halcyon Headquarters (west of Byzance) *(choose one method)*
- **If you have completed “Debt Reconnaissance” for Udom**  
  - Go directly to the Council building as part of the quest **The Masters of the World**; all guards will let you pass.  
  - Enter the minister’s office on the upper floor.  

- **Infiltrate through maintenance tunnels (Lockpicking 60)**  
  - Enter the city’s maintenance tunnels.  
  - Unlock the western door, take the elevator to the area behind the guard checkpoint.  

- **Bribe the guards**  
  - Approach guard **Stallworth** at the Halcyon HQ control post and give him **2000 Octos**.  

- Once inside, unlock the office door with the key given by Clarke, go to his terminal, and read the priority message to update objectives.

## 6. Go to the Ministry of Morality and Precision
- Exit the HQ, return to the street, head northwest, and enter the **Ministry of Morality and Precision** building.  
- You need to infiltrate the laboratory inside.

## 7. Gain access to the laboratory *(choose one method)*
- **Convince Caroline to accompany you (Science 70)**  
  - Go upstairs to the break room, talk to **Caroline**, and use Science to persuade her to go with you to the lab entrance.  

- **Gather information on Caroline’s colleague (Hacking 43)**  
  - Enter the Human Resources office, hack the terminal, read Caroline’s messages about her colleague.  
  - Talk to the researcher about **Theodore** to get her to let you into the lab.  

- **Intimidate or lie to the lab guard (Intimidation 70 or Lie 100)**  
  - Talk to the guard at the lab entrance and use Intimidation or Lie to get past him.  

- **Steal a UDL badge (Stealth 40)**  
  - If you have Stealth, take a badge from the soldier near the ground‑floor stairs, then enter the side room and proceed to the lab.  

- After gaining entry by any method, take the elevator down to the lab level.  
- In the locker room on the right, obtain a **UDL badge** if you do not already have one.

## 8. Reach the Hibernation Lab *(choose one method)*
- **If you have completed “Laboratory Cupid”**  
  - Go to the animal‑testing lab, talk to **Giles**, and receive the **hibernation‑lab access card**.  

- **Otherwise, pick the lock (Lockpicking 65)**  
  - Go to the lab entrance and pick the lock.  

- Follow the right‑hand path toward the central lab, turn left, take the elevator to the upper floor, and cross the northern walkway to reach the hibernation lab.

## 9. Transfer the chemical product
- At the terminal beside the hibernation lab, transfer **26 %** of the product into the capsule.  
  - (Transferring 100 % will kill all test subjects.)  

- Retrieve the capsule and enter the small southeast room.  

- Unlock the door, take the elevator back to the Ministry entrance.  

- Exit the building and head toward **Phineas’s laboratory** to finish the quest.

## 10. Alternative method to enter Clarke’s house
- Go to the Byzance bar and buy vodka for guard **Mayfield**.  
- When he lies down, steal his access card.  
- Enter Clarke’s house through the side alley using the stolen card.
