
## Activate Mission and Reach the Hope
1. Activate the mission.  
2. Travel to the **Hope**.  
3. Proceed along the docks and insert **ADA** into the ship’s auxiliary power feed.

## Choose Your Approach
- **Force** (convince the guards)  
- **Infiltration**  
- **Dialogue** (not detailed)

## Convince the Guards (Persuade)
1. Speak with **Quincy** at the ship entrance and persuade him to let you pass (requires 50 **Deception** + 50 **Science/Engineering** or 80 **Deception**).  
2. Enter the left room and pick up a **UDL badge** from a shelf.  
3. Go to the western door, enter the left room beyond it, and take the **Bridge Access Card** from the desk.  
4. Use the nearby terminal to reroute communications to the **Impostor**.  
5. Return to the door you just opened, use the **Bridge Access Card** to open it, then take the elevator to the upper level.

## Infiltration
1. Climb the guard‑post ladder and head northeast.  
2. Continue northeast, then descend the ramp behind the western door.  
3. In the restricted area, quickly move to the left room and collect a **UDL badge** from a shelf.  
4. Proceed to the western door, enter the left room beyond it, and retrieve the **Bridge Access Card** from the desk.  
5. Use the terminal to reroute communications to the **Impostor**.  
6. Return, open the opposite door with the **Bridge Access Card**, and take the elevator to the upper level.

## Bridge and Launch
1. On the upper deck, move swiftly through the area to avoid unnecessary fights.  
2. Approach the bridge terminal.  
3. Insert **ADA** into the system and authorize the ship to engage **FTL (faster‑than‑light) travel**.  
4. Retrace your steps back to the **Impostor**.

## Travel to Welles’ Lab
1. Travel to **Welles’ laboratory**.  
2. In the main hall, eliminate all corporate guards.  
3. Enter Welles’ quarters and take the **Access Card** from the corpse of the porkyste.  
4. Use this card on the terminal behind you to unlock the secret compartment.

## Acquire Phorce Weapon and Navigation Key
1. Cross the corridor and enter Welles’ bedroom.  
2. Retrieve the unique **Phorce** weapon from **Phinsur’s desk**.  
3. Take the **Navigation Key** from the secret compartment.
