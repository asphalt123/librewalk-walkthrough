
## 1. Intro  
1. After the opening cutscene, head down the slope and follow the guide until you spot enemy patrols.  
2. Hide in the tall grass and make your way to the nearby cave.

## 2. Inside the Cave – Pelham  
1. Enter the cave and approach the marker.  
2. Talk to **Pelham**.  
   - If you have the **Medicine** skill, use it to heal him. He will reward you with **Pelham’s Sentinel Blade**.  

## 3. Explosives and Marauders  
1. After the conversation, shoot the barrels to explode them, then exit the cave.  
2. Outside, a group of **marauders** will be waiting.  
   - Approach quietly and detonate any explosive barrels to start the fight on your terms.  

## 4. Clearing Additional Enemies  
1. Continue stealthily to eliminate a second group of enemies further ahead.  
2. Proceed along the path until you meet **Lieutenant Mercer**.  

## 5. Dialogue with Lieutenant Mercer  
1. Talk to **Lieutenant Mercer**.  
   - If you have **Persuasion**, convince her to lead the assault on the enemies around the ship.  
   - If you choose not to persuade, defeat the marauders yourself.  

## 6. Ship – Pilot Cabin and ADA  
1. Enter the ship and go to the pilot’s cabin to meet **ADA**.  
2. After the dialogue, leave the ship. **Mercer** will confront you and ask for identification.  
   - Choose to **lie** or tell the truth; either option lets you proceed.  
   - Killing **Mercer** is possible but will damage your reputation with **Spacer’s Choice**.  

## 7. Travel to Edgewater  
1. Head toward the town of **Edgewater**.  
2. Talk to **Silas** and ask about the **electric regulator**.  

## 8. Edgewater – Conservatory and Reed  
1. Enter the town and locate the **conservatory**.  
2. Use the elevator to reach **Reed’s** office.  
3. Talk to **Reed**, then exit the area.  

## 9. Parvati and the Regulator  
1. Speak with **Parvati** after leaving Reed’s office.  
2. She will task you with retrieving the **electric regulator** for your ship.  
3. Acquire the regulator (it is obtained during the “And the Electricity Was” quest line).  

## 10. Return to the Ship with Parvati  
1. Return to your ship and talk to **Parvati** again.  
2. She will ask to join you on future voyages – **accept** this invitation, or she will leave your party permanently.  

## 11. Install the Regulator  
1. Re‑enter the ship and speak with **ADA**.  
2. Install the **regulator** in the engine room.  

## 12. Finish the Quest  
1. Return to your navigator (Parvati).  
2. Leave the planet to complete the quest.
