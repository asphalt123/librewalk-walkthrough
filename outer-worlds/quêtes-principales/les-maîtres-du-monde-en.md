
## Quest Steps
1. After settling Udom’s debt in the previous quest, travel to **Byzance**.  
2. Locate the office of **Adjunct Akande**.  
3. Enter the corridor, speak with the guard, then proceed to the nearby elevator.  
4. Ride the elevator to the upper level.  
5. Talk to **Perceval**, then speak with **Akande** to complete the main mission.
