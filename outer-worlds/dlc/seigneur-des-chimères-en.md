
## Prerequisites
- Ensure the quest **Lord of the Chimera** is active (normally after completing “Prince of Tossball”). You can also skip that step by going directly to **Sedrick** at the port.

## Port Area
1. Go to the port after gaining access.  
2. Approach the group of **SLUG** agents.  
   - Choose to **eliminate**, **intimidate**, or **deceive** them.  
3. Enter the **captain’s office** located next door.

## Captain’s Office
1. Decide how to deal with the secretary:  
   - Bribe or persuade her to let you see the boss, **or**  
   - Ignore her cries and head upstairs (if you’re not a normal human).  
2. Interrogate **Sedrick** about **Helen**.  
3. Accept his request to help with the lost merchandise.

## Investigation Inside the Office
1. Examine **Sedrick’s weapons** using the **amplifier**.  
2. Inspect the **book** placed on the lounge table.  
3. Go to the **east restroom** and pick up the **receiver** from the floor.

## SLUG Warehouse
1. Travel to the **SLUG warehouse**.  
2. Go upstairs and interrogate the **team leader** for new objectives.  
3. Leave the warehouse and head toward the **delivery zone**.

## Delivery Zone – Data Retrieval
1. Examine the **automaton** to obtain its **data logs**.  
   - **Option A:** Retrieve the information yourself, then fight the security **robots** that appear.  
   - **Option B:** Let **Parvati** retrieve the data for you, avoiding combat.

## Final Steps
1. Return to **Sedrick** and relay the retrieved information to complete the objective.  
2. Report to **Ludovico** to finish the quest.
