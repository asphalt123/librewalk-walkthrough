
## Main Quest

1. **Talk to Grace Romero** near the entrance of the **Botanical Lab** to activate the mission and obtain information about **Zoé**.  
2. Proceed to the camp and **enter Zoé’s room**. Examine all three notes: the one on the desk, the one on the floor, and the one on the nearby furniture.  
3. **Leave the building** and **talk to Stefan Garcia**. Use **Persuasion** or **Lie** to convince him to give you the **Masked Vendor cassette**.  
4. Travel east to the small raider camp. Complete the enemies for the quest **“A Handful of Fingerprints”** and then **talk to Zoé**.  
5. During the dialogue, **give Zoé the Masked Vendor cassette**. If you do not have it, tell her that **Stefan** wants to watch the new episode with her.  
6. **Return to Grace Romero** to finish the mission and receive your reward: **Drill Pistol**.

## Alternative Outcomes

- If you have already met **Zoé** during **“A Handful of Fingerprints”**, you can skip searching her room.  
- If **Zoé** is already dead, **take the ring from her corpse** and give it to **Grace Romero**.
