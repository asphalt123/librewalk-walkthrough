
## Initial Setup
1. After completing the **Radio Libre Monarque** quest, leave the Devil’s Peak relay station.  
2. Travel to the crash site to the north‑west.  
3. Enter the wrecked ship and use the terminal to retrieve the **command key**.  
4. Open the door behind you and climb the ladder.  
5. At the upper level, go through the door, place the **map** in the left console, then pull the right lever to release the **module**.

## Give the Module to **Sanjar**
1. Travel to **Stellar Bay**.  
2. Deliver the **module** to **Sanjar**.  
3. Exit the town and fight your way through the **Iconoclast** troops.  
4. Continue until you reach the old church.  
5. Inside the church, eliminate **Graham** and **Zora**.  
6. Return to **Sanjar**, complete the quest, and receive the unique weapon **Saisisseur de Sauthon MSI**.

## Give the Module to **Graham**
1. Go to **Amber Heights** and enter **Graham’s** office.  
2. During the dialogue, stay loyal to **Graham** and then confront the **Iconoclast** leader on the upper floor.  
3. Hand the **module** to **Graham**, then leave the camp.  
4. Follow the **Iconoclast** troops along the road to **Stellar Bay**, then veer west toward the ruins.  
5. Pursue **Sanjar** in the ruins and eliminate him to complete the objective.  
6. Return to **Graham**, finish the quest, and receive the unique weapon **Le petit mot**.

## Negotiate a Truce Between **MSI** and the **Iconoclasts**
1. Visit **Sanjar** and discuss the possibility of a truce between the two factions.  
2. Leave the town and travel to the Cascadia ruins.  
3. Enter the abandoned Rizzo factory through the rear door.  
4. Inside, go to the left‑most back office and use the terminal to obtain **Zora’s** evaluations.  
5. Return to **Sanjar** and give him the cartridge; he will accept the truce proposal.  
6. Travel to **Amber Heights** and talk with **Zora**. Agree to support her against **Graham**, then go upstairs to confront the **Iconoclast** leader.  
7. Defeat **Graham**.  
8. Speak with **Zora** again and use dialogue options to persuade her to accept a truce with **MSI**.  
9. Go to the abandoned church in the Stellar Bay ruins to oversee the negotiations.  
10. Complete the mission and receive the unique weapon **Douce Voixen**.

## Additional Notes
- You may choose to ally with **Zora** and give the **module** to her instead of **Graham**; the quest flow remains the same, and if you cannot reconcile the factions, **Zora** will lead the **Iconoclasts**.  
- To convince **Sanjar** to accept the truce, you must have previously accessed his evaluations on the **ARMEE** terminal during the “Hidden Errors” quest.
