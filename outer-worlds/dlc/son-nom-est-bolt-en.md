
## Quest Overview
- **Acquisition**: Sanjar  
- **Reward**: 2188 Octos + **MSI Elite Helmet**  
- **Associated quests**: “Hidden Errors”, “Free Radio Monarch”

## Steps
1. Go to **Stellar Bay**, talk to Sanjar and accept his proposal to start the mission.  
2. Leave the town and head to the ruins northwest.  
3. Search the corpse near the bridge exit and retrieve the **ARMY building key**.  
4. Proceed to the indicated building and unlock the door.  
5. Inside, use the ground‑floor terminal to view administrative evaluations and delete the data to complete the optional objective. This grants an additional **750 Octos** from Sanjar.  
6. Go upstairs, move to the balcony, talk to **Huxley**, then unlock the storage room door using the **access card**.  
7. Retrieve the **BOLT‑52 data cartridge** from the left‑hand cabinet.  
8. Return to Sanjar to finish the mission.  

## Optional Bonus
- After freeing Huxley, you can request a reward and convince him to promote you to the Iconoclasts, earning a small reputation bonus with that faction.
