
## Starting the Quest
- Go to **Sprat's Cabin**, ascend to the 1st floor, talk to **Roscoe**, and accept his proposal to start the quest.

## Find the Four Phonographs

1. **Phonograph 1 – BIC**
   - Travel to the **BIC**.
   - Unlock the door using the **Conserved Eye** obtained from the quest *Those Who Fled Gorgon*.
   - Inside, go to the 2nd floor and retrieve the phonograph from a cabinet in the projection room.

2. **Phonograph 2 – Administrative Apartments**
   - Head to the **Administrative Apartments**.
   - On the 1st floor, enter the cabin and retrieve **Dr. Ambrose’s interview (Part 2)**.

3. **Phonograph 3 – VAH Building**
   - Complete the quest *Mechanical Pseudapple* to obtain the pass for the **VAH Building**.
   - Inside, go to the 3rd floor and retrieve the phonograph hidden behind a duct.

4. **Phonograph 4 – Chemistry Lab**
   - Complete the quest *Catapulted Into Orbit* to gain access to the **Chemistry Lab**.
   - Inside, go to the 3rd level and retrieve the phonograph from the west closet.

## Final Phonograph – Junkyard
- Leave the Chemistry Lab and travel to the **Junkyard** area south of Gorgon.
- Enter the **Guard’s Cabin** and retrieve the phonograph from the right locker.

## Turn In the Recordings

You have two possible recipients.

### 1. Turn In to Georgie’s Contact  
**Reward:** 2250 octo + **Operation Armor** (found in the wasteland)

- Locate the robot **NTVUR** near Sprat's Cabin.
- Activate it using the code **“Ere Glaciaire.”**
- Read the message and hand over the recordings to discover Georgie’s true identity.
- Gain a small reputation boost with **Aunt Cleo** and receive the Operation Armor.
- Afterwards, you may speak with Roscoe and either reveal the truth or keep it secret; the quest ends either way.

### 2. Turn In to Roscoe  
**Reward:** 1250 octo + **Sword Wall** (unique weapon)

- Return to **Roscoe** at Sprat's Cabin and give him the recordings.
- He will hand over the octo reward and the **Sword Wall**.
- If you have at least **80 Persuasion**, you can persuade him to give an additional **750 octo**.
