
## 1. Travel to the Prison
1. Obtain the **navigation key** from Welles' laboratory.  
2. Use it to set a course for Tartarus Prison and dock.  

## 2. Gain Entry Without Unnecessary Fights
1. A corporate guard will approach you. If you have **Lie (78)**, **Intimidation**, or **Persuasion**, convince him to give you a **security agent badge**.  
2. Exit the **Impostor**, proceed to the docks, and pass through the large door at the far end.  
3. Take the side **elevator** to the upper level.  

## 3. Navigate the Upper Level
1. Go down the stairs and enter the **hangar**.  
2. Stay on the left side of the area and climb the ladder at the opposite end.  
3. Open the large door you reach, then head to the **Fosse** section at the far end of the corridor.  
4. Turn right, cross the walkway, and locate the **elevator** a short distance ahead.  

## 4. Reach the President’s Office
1. On the next upper floor, go right again and pass through the large door.  
2. Turn left and follow the corridor until you reach the quest marker.  
3. Use the **terminal** to unlock the door ahead.  
4. Enter and speak with **President Rockwell**.  
   - If you can persuade him (high dialogue skills), convince him to side with you.  
   - If persuasion fails, simply leave the room.  

### Bug Workaround (if the game crashes entering Rockwell’s office)
1. Kill Rockwell from the corridor, **or** go to the far side of the area near the barricades.  
2. Jump onto the concrete block, carefully walk along the railing, and cross to the other side.  
3. Enter the left-hand door to reach Rockwell’s location.  

## 5. Ascend to the Prison’s Summit
1. After dealing with Rockwell, head to the quest marker and take the **central elevator** to the top of the prison.  

## 6. Confront Sophia Akande
1. At the summit, you will meet **Sophia Akande**.  

### Option A – Persuade Sophia to Stand Down
- With high **Lie**, **Persuasion**, and **Intimidation**, convince Sophia to surrender.  
- If successful, take the right-hand corridor directly to **Welles** and end the mission.  

### Option B – Fight Sophia and the Automaton
1. If persuasion fails or you choose combat, go through the left-hand door.  
2. Fight the automaton **R.A.M.** – use **electric weapons** for maximum damage.  
3. After defeating R.A.M., collect the **access card** from its remains.  
4. Use the access card to unlock the far door.  
5. Return to the upper floor, eliminate **Sophia**, and then free **Welles**.  

## 7. Alternative Outcomes
- **Working with Sophia:** President Rockwell will be dead when you arrive; you’ll find his corpse in the small filming room.  
- **Working with Sophia:** At the quest’s end, you must negotiate with **Phinéas Welles**. Persuade him to surrender peacefully if you have appropriate dialogue skills.
