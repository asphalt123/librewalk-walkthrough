
## Starting the Quest
1. Receive the quest from **Sophia Akande**.  
2. Leave the current area and head to the Byzance Exploration Office.

## Getting the Lockwood Office Key
1. Speak with the **receptionist**.  
2. If you have enough **Intelligence** or **Persuasion**, convince the receptionist to give you the **Lockwood Office Key**.  
3. If not, either:
   - Steal the key from the receptionist, **or**
   - Search the closet near the elevator for the receptionist’s corpse to unlock new dialogue options that grant the key.

## Retrieving the Maps
1. Take the elevator to the floor with **Lockwood’s office**.  
2. Unlock the office using the **Lockwood Office Key**.  
3. Collect the **maps** inside.  
4. Use the terminal to read messages and learn the cartographer’s location.

## Finding Lockwood’s Location
1. Exit the building.  
2. Go to the **maintenance tunnels**.  
3. Proceed to **Lockwood’s position**.

## Choices for Completing the Mission
- **Let the mercenary kill the target**  
  - Loot **2000 Octos** from Lockwood’s corpse.  
  - Receive only half of Sophia’s reward.

- **Persuade Sherman to listen to Lockwood (65 Persuasion)**  
  - Sherman will hand you **1250 Octos**.  
  - Receive a small reward from Sophia.

- **Kill the mercenary and claim the full bounty**  
  - Eliminate everyone.  
  - Return to **Sophia** to collect the full payout.

## Alternative Way to Locate Lockwood
1. Interrogate **Mr. Billingsy** in his bar.  
2. Pay him **4700 Octos** for the information on Lockwood’s whereabouts.
