
## Acquisition
- **Félix**

## Reward
- 1500 Octos  
- **Harlow’s Emblem** (unlocks the **Independent** skill for Félix)

## Steps

1. While exploring the Halcyon system, repeatedly visit **Scylla** until Félix mentions his old friend **Harlow**.  
2. Travel to the remote rock where Harlow’s base is located and land on the base’s landing pad.  
3. Talk to **Voelker**, then enter the building and meet **Harlow**. Accept his request to pursue **Trask**.  
4. Go to the **Precursor** and speak with **Rosana** at the local bar. Show her **Harlow’s Emblem** to unlock information about her ex‑husband.  
5. Head to the **Edgewater** region.  
   - Move north, eliminate the primates blocking the path, and reach Trask’s hidden camp in the hills.  

### Choice A – Kill Trask
6. Eliminate **Trask** and loot **Trask’s Ring** from his corpse.  
7. Return to **Harlow** and report the success to complete the objective.

### Choice B – Interrogate Trask and Gather Evidence
6. During the dialogue with **Trask**, request proof of Harlow’s betrayal to trigger the optional evidence objective.  
7. Ask Trask to hand over his **Ring** so you can fake his death.  
8. Return to Harlow’s base, enter the small western corridor, and retrieve the **documents** hidden in Trask’s stash.  
9. Bring the **evidence** to **Harlow** and let him justify himself to Félix.  
10. Harlow will reveal his true intentions, attack, and you must kill him and all his men.  
11. After the battle, go back to the **Impostor** and speak with **Félix** to finish the quest and earn the **Independent** skill for your companion.

## Alternate Outcomes
- Showing the evidence to **Voelker** has no effect on the bandits.  
- If you show the evidence to **Harlow**, you can only avoid conflict by claiming you were deceived by Trask; accusing him without evidence makes him hostile.
