
## Acquisition
- **Parvati**

## Reward
- XP

## Associated Quest
- “Passport for Adventure”

## Steps
1. During your first visit to the **Precursor**, find **Parvati** at the ship’s airlock. Accept her request, add her to your crew, then leave the **Impostor**.  
2. Go to the engine room at the far end of the ship, talk to **Junlei Tennyson**, and introduce **Parvati**.  
3. While the conversation takes place, stay back and quietly encourage your companion.  
4. Return to the **Impostor** and talk to **Parvati** again. Agree to take her for a drink on the **Precursor**.  
5. Travel to the **Precursor** and head to the local bistro.  
6. Talk to **Parvati**, buy her a drink (**wine**), then ask about her feelings and try to lift her spirits to convince her to make the first move.  
7. Go back to the **Impostor**, talk to **Junlei** again to complete the quest.
