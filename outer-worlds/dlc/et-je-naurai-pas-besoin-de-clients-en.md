
## Acquisition
- Talk to **Isabella Essary** and **Lawrence** near the legal hunter store in the Wasteland to start the quest.

## Rewards
- **750 octos** upon completion.

## Prerequisite
- **Complete “A Fleeting Taste of Purp”** (the preceding quest).

## Steps
1. Go to the Wasteland area.
2. Speak with **Isabella** and **Lawrence** near the legal hunter store to receive the quest.
3. Follow the quest markers to each dead client.
4. Loot **access cards** from each dead client.
5. When you reach the client at the far north‑east of the zone, read his **journal** for additional information.

## Completion Options
- **Give the access cards to Isabella and Lawrence** – the quest ends and you receive **750 octos**.  
- **Trap the two employees** (requires reading the dead client’s journal):
  1. Lie to **Isabella** and **Lawrence** to lure them into a deadly trap.  
  2. After the dialogue, teleport anywhere on the map to trigger the employees’ departure.  
  3. Travel to the north‑east of the zone and locate the bodies of the two employees.  
  4. This choice provides no extra reward beyond the story effect of avenging the dead clients.
