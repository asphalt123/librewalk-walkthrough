
## Starting the Quest  
1. Go to the northern street of Byzance and talk to **Hortense** to activate the side quest.  

## Main Objectives  
1. Proceed to the **cargo landing area**.  
2. Follow the signs marked **“Retirees”** to the southwest.  
3. Use the **terminal** in the far room to unlock the door.  
4. Go through the large door into the **retirees' reception center**.  
5. Move down the corridor and open the next door with the **terminal**.  
6. Take the elevator at the far end to the lower level.  
7. In the lower corridor, destroy all attacking **robots**.  
8. After the fight, use the **terminal** at the back of the room to unlock the hatch.  
9. Open the hatch to exit the area.  
10. Return to **Hortense** and report.  

## Choices and Rewards  
- **Tell the truth** to Hortense: receive **1,125 Octos** and a reputation penalty with the Council.  
- **Lie** to Hortense (40% lie): send her to her death, receive **1,125 Octos** and a reputation bonus with the Council.  

## Alternative Outcome  
- During the final dialogue with **Hortense**, you can use **persuasion** or **intimidation** to gain an additional **2,188 Octos**.
