
## Acquisition
**Célia**

## Reward
**375 Octos**

## Steps
1. Join **Célia** in Stellar Bay and talk to her about her money problems to receive the mission.
2. Exit to the street and locate **Sebastian**, who is nearby.
3. In dialogue with Sebastian, choose the persuasion option you prefer to convince him to spend time with Célia.
4. Return to **Célia** and collect your reward.
