
## Quest Details
- **Acquisition**: Silas  
- **Reward**: **300 Octos**  
- **Associated Quests**: “Et l’électricité fut”, “Un long lendemain”, “A bat les robots”

## Step‑by‑Step Guide

1. **Talk to Silas** at the Edgewater entrance and accept the mission.  
2. Locate the four debtors and collect their royalties:  

   - **Phyllis Granger** – In the canning factory:  
     1. Speak with Phyllis and ask for her royalty.  
     2. Ask her about **Eugène** to obtain information needed later for “Et l’électricité fut”.  

   - **Conrad (the barber)** – At the barber shop near the Edgewater entrance:  
     1. Talk to Conrad; he cannot pay yet. End the conversation.  
     2. Check the note near the corpse in the morgue for clues about **Eugène’s gold teeth**.  
     3. Resume dialogue with Conrad, convince him to pawn the gold teeth for his royalty.  
     4. Ask Conrad about **Eugène’s death** for additional hints.  

   - **Abernathy** – Go to the marked location on your map and collect his royalty. This also activates the quest “Un long lendemain”.  

   - **Miller** – Leave the town, head to the landing strip, and speak with Miller. Collect his royalty and receive the quest “A bat les robots”.  

3. Return to **Silas**, report the completed collections, and finish the quest.

## Eugène and the Gold Teeth

1. After gathering information from **Phyllis** and **Conrad**, return to Phyllis and question her again about **Eugène**.  
2. Speak to **Silas** about **Eugène**; discover that the corpse has vanished.  
3. Go to the Botanical Lab, explore the back of the greenhouse, and retrieve the **gold teeth** from the furniture.  
4. Use the gold teeth and the accumulated intel to uncover the secret of Adelaide’s fertilizer, which resolves the community issue at the end of “Et l’électricité fut”.  
5. The **gold teeth** can be sold for additional profit: **300 Octos** from Silas and **500 Octos** from Phyllis Granger.
