
## Steps

1. **Travel to Gorgon** and head to the entrance of the VAH building.  
2. Use **Marion’s pass** to unlock the front door.  
3. Inside, go up the stairs and take the side tunnel.  
4. Continue forward until you encounter the first group of test subjects.  
5. Eliminate these enemies and push onward to the office area.  
6. If you convinced Marion to give you her **security code** during the “Mechanical Pseudapple” quest, use it on the southern terminal to open a shortcut.  
   - If you don’t have the code, you must fight through the two test chambers infested with test subjects.  
7. In either case, reach the southern hallway and enter the conduit leading to the lower level.  
8. Once on the lower level, go through the door on your left.  
9. Take the **elevator** a short distance further down.  
10. When you reach the final floor, enter the **supervisor’s office**.  
11. Access the terminal there and **send the unlock signal** to complete the quest.
