
## Getting the Mission  
- Talk to **Lilya Hagen** to receive the quest (reward is variable; associated with the quest **“Clive the Butcher.”**).  

## Travel to Byzance and Reach the Lab  
1. Head to **Byzance** (the lower area).  
2. Use the terminal to activate the elevator.  
3. Ride the elevator down to the basement laboratory.  

## Decision Point: **Dr. Eva Chartrand**  
Choose one of the following approaches.  

### Option 1 – Kill **Dr. Eva Chartrand**  
1. Eliminate all enemies in the laboratory.  
2. Return to **Lilya Hagen** on the **Precursor** ship.  
3. Receive **2,188 Octos** and the **Vice President’s SubLight Armor**.  
4. *(Optional)* If you have completed **“Clive the Butcher”** for **Catherine** on **Monarch**, go to your cabin and collect the unique weapon **Negotiator** from the desk.  

### Option 2 – Spare **Dr. Eva Chartrand**  
1. Spare the scientist.  
2. Convince her to continue her research for **Welles** *or* the **Council** (choosing the Council grants a small reputation boost with that faction).  
3. Approach the guard and say the line **“Sophia needs me alive.”** to have him leave you alone.  
4. Exit the area.  
5. Report back to **Lilya Hagen**. She will be dissatisfied and will not give a reward.
