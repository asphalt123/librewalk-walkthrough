
## Quest Start  
1. After the quest triggers, head **south**.  
2. Follow the waypoint **west**.  

## Defuse the Bomb  
1. Enter the next room and skirt the steam jets on the **right** side.  
2. Pass through the back door.  
3. On the opposite side, locate the **fuel canisters** on the left shelf.  
4. Use the **anomaly amplifier** to examine the fuels and **defuse the bomb**.  

## After Defusing  
1. Talk to **Ludovico**.  
2. Exit the area.  

## Report to Administrator  
1. Return to your **suite**.  
2. Access the **terminal** and speak with the **administrator** to complete the quest.
