
## Optional: Meet Max  

1. After finishing the conversation with **Reed** in the quest “A Stranger in a Strange Land”, talk to **Parvati** to receive an optional objective.  
2. Leave the area and head toward the temple.  
3. Locate **Max** inside the temple and speak with him.

## Deliver Reed’s Message  

1. Exit **Edgewater** and travel north to the **Deserters’ Camp**.  
2. Speak to **Grace Romero** and enter the greenhouse.  
3. Talk to **Adélie** to receive new quest objectives.  
4. Ask her about her farming techniques, the deserters, and the plague to gather useful information.

## Optional: Question Adélie About Gold Teeth  

1. While in the greenhouse, go to the far‑right room and pick up the **gold teeth** on the left‑hand table.  
2. If you previously interrogated the townsfolk about **Eugène** during “To Our (Too Many!) Dear Deceased”, use that knowledge to steer the conversation with Adélie toward Eugène and her work in the greenhouse.  
3. Reveal that she uses the corpses of the villagers as fertilizer.  
4. This information will help later when you decide to redirect power toward Edgewater.

## Find the Power‑Redirection Switches  

1. Leave the Deserters’ Camp and head to the **Power Plant**. Enter the building through the side breach.  
2. Clear or avoid the security robots and make your way to the plant’s interior.  
3. From the entry point, open the door directly ahead, then turn right.  
4. Interact with the terminal at the end of the corridor and attempt to reroute the power to receive new objectives.  
5. Return to the entrance, go down the right ramp, and use **Reed’s access code** at the security post terminal to unlock doors.  
6. Enter the next room, defeat the robot on the lower floor, then go to the right‑hand room and eliminate two more robots.  
7. Activate the **first redirection switch** on the left.  
8. Proceed to the circular room at the far end and descend to the lower level. Open the left door and continue forward.  
9. In the following room, avoid steam jets, climb the ramp, and activate the **second redirection switch**.  
10. Pass through the door opposite you, locate the **third and final switch** on the upper floor, and activate it.  
11. Return to the plant’s control room. You can now choose to redirect the power either to the **Botanical Lab** or back to **Edgewater**.

## Redirect Power to the Botanical Lab  

*Consequences: Edgewater is left without electricity; residents not in the deserters’ camp may die from the plague or be forced to leave the planet.*  

1. After selecting the botanical lab option, speak with **Adélie** again, then travel back to **Edgewater**.  
2. Upon arrival, you’ll find the town in darkness—houses and shops have no power.  
3. Go to the **Canning Plant**, locate the generator room in the basement, and find **Reed**.  
4. If you have a very high **Intimidation** skill, you can persuade Reed to stand down without bloodshed.  
5. If not, defeat all of Reed’s soldiers and seize the **electric regulator**.

## Redirect Power to Edgewater  

1. Choose the Edgewater option and report back to **Adélie**. Two new paths become available.  

### Convince the Workers to Return to Their Jobs  

*Consequences: Life resumes in Edgewater, but the population will continue to suffer from poor nutrition‑related diseases. Adélie remains alone in the botanical lab.*  

1. Approach the deserters and talk to **Thomas** or **Grace**.  
2. If you have already completed the quests “Frantic Engineers” and “Benevolent Spirits”, you can easily convince them to return to Edgewater.  
3. Inform **Adélie** of the decision and collect the **electric regulator**.  
4. Return to **Reed**, report the events, and optionally slip a supportive comment about Thomas.  

### Convince Reed to Quit His Post  

*Consequences: Adélie takes charge of Edgewater and works on agricultural methods to eradicate the plague. Reed resigns and leaves the planet permanently.*  

1. While speaking with **Adélie**, ask her to let you handle **Reed** for a new objective.  
2. Travel to Edgewater and locate Reed’s office.  
3. Initiate dialogue and persuade him to abandon his post.  
4. Guide the conversation toward the plague and link it to the residents’ diet.  
5. Reveal the information that Adélie uses corpses as fertilizer, prompting Reed to realize the scheme’s cleverness.  
6. After the conversation, return to **Adélie**, tell her the outcome, retrieve the **electric regulator** from the lab, and head back to your ship.

## Alternative: Eliminate Reed  

1. Instead of persuading Reed, you may choose to kill him.  
2. Be aware that this action will cause **Parvati** to leave your party early unless you have sufficiently high **Persuasion** skills.
