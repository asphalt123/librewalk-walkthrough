
## Quest Overview
- **Acquisition**: **Talamadge Kerr**  
- **Reward**: **625 Octos**

## Steps

1. Go to the apartments south of town. A panicked worker will approach you. Talk to him, then enter the building and go to the first bedroom on the right to start the quest.  
2. Inspect the **document** on the table and search the **bag** in the back of the room.  
3. Head to **Left Field** and talk to **Nell** about Isaac to get more information.  
4. Follow the marker to the alley and approach the thugs. You have several options:  
   - **Kill the thugs** – the quest can finish normally, but you will receive a small reputation penalty with SubLight.  
   - **Intimidate the thugs** (requires 55 Intimidation) – persuade **Elijah** and his men to leave.  
   - **Find the murder weapon** – go to the docks near the cannery north of town, search the crate and retrieve a **broken Tossball cross**. Then return to **Elijah**, tell him you have found the weapon, and he will agree to leave town without causing trouble.  
5. After resolving the situation, speak with **Sanjar** to complete the objective and receive your reward.
