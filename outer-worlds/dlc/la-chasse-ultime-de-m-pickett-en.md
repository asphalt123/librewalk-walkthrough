
## Acquisition  
- Talk to **Sebastian Adams** in A Stellar Bay and ask about **Franklin Pickett** to receive the quest.  

## Steps  

1. **Leave A Stellar Bay** after the dialogue.  
2. Follow the mission marker located **south of Amber Heights**.  
3. **Eliminate the Royal Mantis** that guards the area.  
4. **Search the Mantis’s corpse** and pick up **Pickett’s Journal**.  
5. **Return to Sebastian Adams** in A Stellar Bay and hand over the journal to complete the quest.  

## Reward  
- **375 Octo**.
