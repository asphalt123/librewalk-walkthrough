
## Activate Mission
1. Go to your quarters on the **Impostor**.  
2. Use the terminal to read Hawthorne’s reports, which unlocks the side mission.

## Shrinking Ray
1. Travel to the Phineas laboratory.  
2. Reach the main room.  
3. Pick up the **Shrinking Ray** from the tablet near the window.

## Prismatic Hammer
1. Go to the **Precursor** and head to the dormitories north of the main docks.  
2. Jump onto the crates at the entrance, climb onto the bunks, and move toward the opening opposite.  
3. Eliminate all bandits on the other side.  
4. Search the bandit leader’s corpse for the **repair hangar access card**.  
5. Use the card to open the nearby door and open the crate to obtain the **Prismatic Hammer**.

## Mandibular Restructurer
1. On the **Precursor**, speak with Gladys and purchase the Hephaestus mining company archives from the “Quest Items” category.  
2. Return to your ship and read the document on your cabin terminal to update your objectives.  
3. Travel to the Scylla mining station.  
4. Head east to the abandoned outpost and search the indicated building.  
5. Open the crate inside to retrieve the **Mandibular Restructurer**.

## Sticky Weapon
1. Go to **Monarch** and visit the Fallbrook community.  
2. Talk to Duncan and purchase the UDL info block from the “Quest Item” category.  
3. With the info block, go to the UDL Lab on Monarch (part of the “Hidden Errors” quest).  
4. Enter the lab, go upstairs, and take the **access card** from the table in the central room.  
5. Return to the ground floor, insert the card into the terminal, and either hack the system (55 hacking) or answer the questions correctly (favoring the corporation president).  
6. Retrieve the **Sticky Weapon** from its compartment.

## Mental Control Beam
1. In Fallbrook, buy the SubLight info block from Duncan to update your objectives.  
2. Travel to Cascadia and enter Rizzo’s secret laboratory (part of the “Cosmocrime Syndicate” quest).  
3. Traverse the area to the final room of the complex.  
4. Transfer the Alta‑Vitae to the **Impostor**.  
5. From that position, jump to the platform east of you and climb the ladder.  
6. Jump to the next ladder, then open the crate to obtain the **Mental Control Beam**.
