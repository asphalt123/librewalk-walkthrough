
## Quest Overview
- **Acquisition:** Variable  
- **Reward:** XP + access to the **“Cosmic Crime Syndicate”** quest line  
- **Associated Quest:** **“Passport to Adventure”**

## Steps

1. **Talk to Lee Flores or Gladys** on the **Precursor** to learn about **SubLight** and trigger the quest.  
2. **Proceed to the SubLight headquarters** and take the elevator to the upper floor.  
3. **Introduce yourself to Tobias** at the top of the elevator.  
4. **Speak with Lilya** and ask her for work.  
5. **Obtain the Stellar Bay navigation key** from Gladys; she will only hire you after you have it.  

After completing these steps, the **“Cosmic Crime Syndicate”** faction mission becomes available.
