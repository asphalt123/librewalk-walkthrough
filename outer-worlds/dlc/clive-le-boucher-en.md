
## Acquisition  
- **Catherine Malin** (quest giver)

## Reward  
- 2188 **Octos**  
- **SubLight Contractor Helmet**

## Associated Quest  
- “Dance with the Porkystes”

## Getting the Mission  
- Go to **Catherine** in Fallbrook and ask for work to receive this side mission.  
- Exhaust all conversation topics to gather useful information.

## Entering the Porglier Factory  

### Main Entrance  
1. Speak with **Duncan** and persuade him to give you a **Porglier Factory badge**.  
   - If persuasion fails, attempt to steal a badge from his locker.  
2. Approach the guards at the main entrance and use any persuasion, intimidation, or lie skill (20 points each) to convince them to let you in.  
3. Open the door and run across the courtyard into the factory.  
4. Inside, go up the stairs, open the office door, then descend to the porkystes breeding area.

### Sewer Entry (Lockpicking 35)  
1. If you have lockpicking, go to the sewer access behind the eastern waterfall.  
2. Pick the door, walk onto the walkway, and climb the ladder to the upper level.  
3. Move left, pass the terminal on the right, and continue without being seen.  
4. Retrieve the **Porglier Factory badge** from the far locker for freer movement.

## Meeting Clive  
1. From the breeding area, go through the south‑west door and climb the stairs on the opposite side.  
2. Use the terminal at the top to call the elevator to **Clive’s office**.  
3. Return, go through the left door, cross the canning area, and reach the north‑west elevator.  
4. Talk to **Clive** briefly and tell him you need time to think before moving freely in the factory.

## Options After Meeting Clive  

### Negotiate a Truce (55 Persuasion or 55 Medicine)  
1. During dialogue, explain your purpose and propose an agreement between **Clive** and **Catherine**.  
2. If you have high **Medicine**, convince him of the proposal’s relevance.  
3. Otherwise, return to the breeding area, read the info block on the shelf to learn about Clive’s methods, then use **Persuasion** to convince the butcher to cooperate with **Catherine**.  
4. Return to **Catherine** and persuade (or lie to) her to follow the plan; requires high Persuasion or Lie.

### Sabotage the Factory  

- **Alter Porkystes’ Diet** (55 Medicine)  
  1. Go to the porkystes feeding stations.  
  2. Use the central terminals to change the vitamin dosage in their food.  

- **Disrupt Canning System** (55 Engineering)  
  1. Reach the canning line, climb the ladder in the south‑west corner to the terminal.  
  2. Use Engineering to increase the yield and halt production.  

- **Falsify Financial Reports** (Remarkable Intelligence)  
  1. Enter the office at the factory entrance.  
  2. Use the back‑room terminal to tamper with the factory’s financial data.  

### Assassinate Clive  
1. Follow the path to **Clive’s office** and kill him.  
2. Enter the left corridor from his office and take the elevator to exit the factory.  
3. Outside, use the terminal to unlock the back door and leave the area.

## Additional Tips  
- Before tackling Clive’s case, speak to **Catherine** about the butcher; she will reward you with 500 **Octos**.  
- In the left corridor of Clive’s office, collect the three canned food items and consume them.  
- Afterwards, talk to the butcher about the tasting; he will give you 625 **Octos**.
