
## Travel to the Specialized Pharmacy Unit on Olympus  
1. From the **Impostor**, set a course for the pharmacy unit located on Olympus.  
2. If you have already completed the “Mechanical Pseudapple” quest, you will be ambushed by Flibustiers upon arrival, triggering the “Mercenaries vs. Mercenaries” side quest. Resolve that encounter first, then land on Olympus.  

## Convince Kelley to Open the Door  
1. Inside the station, interact with the intercom near the right‑hand door.  
2. Persuade the woman locked in the closet (Kelley) to open the door. Required stats: **55 Persuasion** *or* **90 Deception + 40 Medicine** *or* **90 Intimidation**.  
3. If successful, talk to **Kelley** and ask about **Dr. Jasper**.  
4. Agree to let her board the **Impostor**; she will reward you with the unique **Mono‑Lens of Cellular Stability Armor**.  
5. Optionally, extort an additional **500 Octos** by charging her for the rescue.  

## Cross the Station and Reach Kelley (if persuasion fails)  
1. Go through the north door and destroy the security drones.  
2. Take the elevator to the upper level.  
3. Eliminate the new group of robots and pass through the large south‑west door.  
4. Head south‑east, descend the ladder, and meet Kelley.  

## Interrogate Dr. Low  
1. From Kelley’s position, climb the ladder and clear all robots.  
2. Enter the south‑west room and take the **habitat station map** from the desk.  
3. Unlock the door to meet **Jasper Low**.  
4. Talk to Jasper; he will share information only after you reactivate the station’s generator turbine.  

## Reactivate the Turbine  
1. Open the indicated door using Jasper’s **access card**.  
2. Ride the elevator to the top of the tower.  
3. Use the terminal to attempt a turbine restart.  
4. Fight robots en route to the two turbine generators and pull both levers.  
5. Return to the terminal and restart the turbine permanently.  

## Return to Jasper  
1. Speak with **Jasper** again; he will give you the **Chemistry Lab password** and reward you with the unique weapon **Employee Perks**.  
2. If you have **55 Intimidation** *or* **90 Persuasion**, you can press for additional information, gaining the **Chemistry Lab security pass**, which speeds up the “Perilous Experiment” quest.  

## Alternative Outcomes  

- **Kill Dr. Low**: If you prefer not to repair the turbine, eliminate Jasper Low. Loot his corpse for the **Chemistry Lab password** and the **Employee Perks** weapon.  

- **Steal the Password**: If you want a non‑violent approach, use **90 Lockpicking** to steal the password from Jasper without harming him.
