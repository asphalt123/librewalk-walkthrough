
## Acquisition
- Activate the quest by accessing the terminal near Dr. Blossom’s corpse in the DD&RR labs during the “A fleeting taste of Purple” quest.

## Update Objectives
- Leave the labs and go to the Nursery Warehouse.  
- Use the terminal’s inventory register to update your objectives.

## Gather Vodka Information
- Speak with Sidney at the Bazaar to learn about vodka stock locations.  
- Vodka bottles will now appear on the map.

## Vodka Locations
- **Vodka Spectre Orange** – Purchase from Sidney.  
- **Vodka Spectre Red** – Purchase from Sidney or the Grand Colonial bartender; also found at the ruined picnic area.  
- **Vodka Spectre Black** – Purchase from the Grand Colonial bartender; also found at the ruined picnic area.  
- **Vodka Spectre Blue** – Purchase from the Grand Colonial bartender or locate on a table in the eastern part of the vegetal maze.  
- **Vodka Spectre Indigo** – Purchase from the Grand Colonial bartender or find in a cupboard in the gardener’s hut.  
- **Vodka Spectre Violet** – Purchase from the Grand Colonial bartender or find in Dr. Blossom’s office in the DD&RR labs.  
- **Vodka Spectre Yellow** – Find in the control room of the Hive.  
- **Vodka Spectre Green** – Find in a crate at the Nursery Warehouse.

## Complete the Collection
- **Collect all 8 Spectre Vodkas**.

## Retrieve the Weapon
- Return to the DD&RR labs with the 8 bottles.  
- Use the terminal to open the compartment and obtain the unique weapon **Gatling Spectre**.

## Reward
- **Gatling Spectre** (unique weapon)
