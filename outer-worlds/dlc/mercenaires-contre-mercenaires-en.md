
## 1. Accept ADA’s Proposal and Flee

1. When attacked by the **Flibustiers**, listen to **ADA**’s offer to disengage.
2. Choose to follow **ADA**’s plan to escape the combat.
3. The quest ends immediately and you receive the experience reward.  
   *Note: You will never encounter these pirates again and you miss out on a unique weapon.*

## 2. Deal with the Flibustiers Personally

### a. Initiate Direct Action

1. Tell **ADA** you will handle the situation yourself.
2. Board the pirate ship.

### b. Negotiate with the Captain (requires **95 Intimidation** + **Strength** or **95 Persuasion** + **Charm**)

1. Use the intercom on the door to contact **Coach Stilley**.  
2. Apply the appropriate skill (Intimidation, Persuasion, or a combination with Strength/Charm) to convince him to meet you in person.  
3. Go through the door unlocked by a **Flibustier** and ascend to the upper deck.  
4. Enter the captain’s cabin.  
5. Choose one of the following options:  
   - **Intimidate** the captain.  
   - **Persuade** the captain.  
   - **Lie** to the captain (you can also intimidate after lying to obtain **700 octo**).  
   - **Bribe** the captain.  
6. After the negotiation, return to the lower level.  
7. Unlock the cockpit door with the **key** given by **Stilley**.  
8. Retrieve the **tracker** from the terminal.  
9. Return to **ADA** to complete the quest.

### c. Kill All Pirates

1. If you cannot convince the captain to meet you, go east through the door and start shooting.  
2. After the initial fight, loot the **access card** to the galley from a dead pirate and go upstairs.  
3. Continue clearing enemies on the upper deck and enter the captain’s cabin again.  
4. You now have another chance to negotiate; if you prefer combat, kill the captain and take his **access card**.  
5. Use the captain’s **access card** to reach the cockpit and retrieve the **tracker**.  
6. Return to **ADA** to finish the quest.

## 3. Alternative Outcomes

- You may arrange a meeting with **Stilley** at the start of the mission and still choose to kill him once you’re in his cabin.  
- Conversely, you can negotiate with the captain even after eliminating most of his crew.
