
## Main Quest

1. Examine the crime scene.  
2. Go to reception and talk to **concierge Halston** to obtain access to your room.  
3. Take the elevator to the **luxury suite**.  
4. In the suite, enter the right‑hand room and read the terminal messages to start **“The Queen of Productivity”** quest.  
5. Use the living‑room terminal to contact **administrator Ludovico**, who grants access to the **Purple Orchards**.  

## Find Clues in the Purple Orchards

1. Leave the hotel and head southwest to the bridge; cross it and the next bridge to reach the **Purple Orchards**.  
2. Talk to **Sidney** and ask about **Helen** for new information.  
3. Examine the footprints on the road, then continue forward and speak with the nearby gardener.  
4. Follow the footprints west to the greenhouse.  
5. Cross the greenhouse and keep following the footprints south.  
6. Near the generator, turn southwest and proceed to the ruined picnic area.  
7. Analyze the Tossball bat and pick up the **DD&RR access card** found on its cover.  
8. Head to the nearby **DD&RR laboratories**.

## Investigate the DD&RR Labs

1. Enter the back corridor of the labs and use the intercom to speak with **Dr. Blossom**. Accept her proposal to receive new objectives.  
2. Take the elevator behind you to the lower level.  

### Non‑Violent Approach (Release Anesthetic Gas)

1. Advance down the corridor and turn right.  
2. Hide behind crates, enter the left‑hand room, then exit through the right‑hand door a short distance ahead.  
3. Quickly cross the corridor and enter the next room.  
4. Continue forward, pass through the back door, and enter the workshop directly ahead.  
5. Enter the subsequent room and walk along the right wall.  
6. At the far end, go through the hole in the wall and use the terminal to release the anesthetic gas throughout the labs.  
7. Return upstairs to find **Dr. Blossom** (her body will be there). Instead of interrogating her corpse, check her terminal for recent event logs.  

8. Use the **Gatling terminal** to activate the quest **“At the End of the Specter”**, then examine the corpses on the floor.  
9. Inspect the ventilation duct at the north end of the corridor, then exit the laboratories.

## Optional: Deal with the Killer Wasps

1. Upon returning to the orchards, you’ll notice swarms of lethal wasps. This is optional for main‑story progression.  
2. If you choose to handle it, travel to the hive south of the orchards and eliminate all wasp swarms.  
3. Enter the shed, use the terminal to stop the pollination cycle.  
4. Analyze the multifunction tool on the ground for additional clues.

## Finish the Quest

1. After completing the orchard investigation, return to your **Grand Colonial suite**.  
2. Speak with **administrator Ludovico** to conclude the **“Purple Taste”** quest and unlock the next objective.
