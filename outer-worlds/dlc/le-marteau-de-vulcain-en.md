
## Steps

1. Go to the Roseway laboratory, talk to **Orson Shaw**, and accept his quest.  
2. Leave the laboratory and head east to the storage building.  
3. Enter the right‑hand door of the storage building and eliminate the bandits inside.  
4. Proceed to the southeast room and examine the note near the armory door.  
5. Travel to the old Tatie‑Biotique laboratory to obtain the armory access code. Choose one of the following methods:  
   - **Hack the reception computer** (requires 20 hacking). Hacking this terminal reveals the armory code immediately.  
   - If you lack sufficient hacking skill, go to the upper floor of the old lab, read the computer in the north office, then the two computers in the open‑space area. Combine the messages to reconstruct the code.  
6. Return to the storage building and unlock the armory door using the obtained code.  
7. Inside the armory, take the **Acidic Macerator** from the shelf (part of the “Cleaning Unit” quest) and go down the stairs a short distance.  
8. On the lower level, destroy the robot and collect the **Force Pistol schematics**.  
9. Return to **Orson Shaw**, give him the documents, and complete the quest. You will receive the experimental pistol **Ultimatum** as a reward.  
10. After completing other quests, talk to **ADA** on the Impostor to receive a message from **Orson Shaw**.  
11. Go back to **Orson Shaw** at the Roseway laboratory to receive the unique weapon **Ulti‑Nature**.

## Optional Alternative

- Keep the schematics instead of handing them over and later sell them to **Gladys** during the “Distress Signal” mission for a large sum of money. Choosing this path will cause the quest to fail, and you will not obtain the **Ulti‑Nature** weapon.
