
## Quest Acceptance
1. After completing **“Ship, Sweet Ship”**, go to the Engine Room and speak to **Edna Ingmire** to accept the quest.  
2. Return to the **Impostor** and set course for relay station **PC‑23**.

## At Relay Station PC‑23
1. Enter the first door.  
2. Turn left and lure the robot you see in the background.  
3. Defeat the robot, then destroy the mines.  
4. Jump over the trap to reach the next room.  
5. Go through the door on the right.

## Upper Floor
1. Eliminate the robots in the left corner.  
2. Take the stairs to the upper level.  
3. Enter the office at the top.  
4. Inspect the corpse at the far end.

## Retrieve Items
1. Take the **access code** from the corpse.  
2. Pick up the unique weapon **Silencieux**.

## Activate Relay
1. Use the **access code** to unlock the terminal.  
2. Reactivate the relay.  
3. Leave the station and return to **Edna** on the **Precursor**.

## Rewards
- **1500 Octos**  
- **Silencieux** (unique weapon)
