
## Acquisition  
- Talk to **Velma** at the cannery in Stellar Bay to receive the quest.  
- Continue questioning her to learn about the hidden **Octos** stash.

## Options to Resolve the Strike  

### 1. Lie to **Caleb** *(Lie 55)*  
1. Go to the **Yacht Club**.  
2. Talk to **Caleb** on the upper floor.  
3. Lie about **Velma** so he agrees to resume work immediately.  

### 2. Steal **Caleb**'s Savings *(Lockpicking 50)*  
1. If you learned about **Caleb**'s Octos reserve from **Velma**, head to the restaurant.  
2. Go upstairs.  
3. Jump over the balcony onto the left building.  
4. Pick the lock on the safe to retrieve the **Octos**.  
5. Return to **Caleb** and tell him his savings are gone; he will then agree to resume work.  

### 3. Convince **Velma** to Raise Employee Wages *(Persuasion 55)*  
1. Talk to **Velma**.  
2. If you have already completed the quest **“The Hidden People,”** persuade her that it is in her best interest to end the strike quickly.  

### 4. Blackmail **Velma** *(Lie 35 or Hacking 55)*  
1. Talk to **Caleb** and ask about rumors of corruption involving **Velma**.  
2. Return to **Velma**:  
   - If you have **Lie 35**, claim you have proof of fund embezzlement.  
   - Otherwise, go to her office upstairs, hack her computer (**Hacking 55**), and review the data for incriminating information.  
3. Use the obtained leverage to convince **Velma** to increase wages.  

## Reward  
- **625 Octos**  

## Associated Quest  
- **“The Hidden People”**
