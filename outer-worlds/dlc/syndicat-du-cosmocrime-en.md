
## Steps

1. **Accept Lilya Hagen’s quest** immediately after finishing “Stellar Harvester”.
2. **Travel to Stellar Bay**  
   - Go to the docks behind the cannery.  
   - Retrieve the bloody message from the corpse hidden behind the crates.
3. **Proceed to Fallbrook (south‑east)**  
   - Speak to **Catherine** and give her the message.
4. **Head to Cascadia (west)**  
   - Follow the left side of the perimeter wall to enter the town.  
   - Eliminate the bandits roaming the streets.
5. **Enter the marked building**  
   - Use the terminal and the password found on the bloody message to open the ground‑floor door.  
   - Take the elevator down to **Rizzo’s secret lab**.
6. **Lab entrance**  
   - Turn right, then enter the first left room.  
   - Either convince the security robots (60 Lie) or destroy them.  
   - Go through the south door and use the terminal to contact **ADA**.  
   - Read the messages about the ventilation system.  
   - Unlock the left door and continue through the corridor to the next lab section.
7. **Neutralize the Mantisaur**  
   - Advance to the end of the corridor and turn right.  
   - Destroy the mines on the left and jump over the electric arcs.  
   - Enter the left room and neutralize the robots.  
   - Proceed to the back corridor, go north, and move carefully to avoid steam jets.  
   - At the far end, open the door and interact with the terminal on the right.  
   - Choose one of the following methods to deal with the Mantisaur:  
     - **If Ellie is present**: have the surgeon adjust CO₂ levels to kill all Mantisaurs.  
     - **If Ellie is absent**: use the terminal to modify CO₂ yourself (60 Science or 100 Hacking).
8. **Align the supply system**  
   - Unlock the north door, continue down the corridor, and enter the first right room.  
   - Climb the ladder at the back and use the terminal to align the supply system.  
   - Return down, enter the next room, inspect the body in the tube, then go to the back‑right office.  
   - Read the terminal messages, head to the stairs at the back, reach the marker, and use the terminal to decide how to siphon the **Alta‑Vitae**.
9. **Transfer the Alta‑Vitae** (choose one option):  
   - **Do it yourself** – 40 Engineering or 60 Hacking.  
   - **With Parvati** – she will handle the transfer using her engineering skills.  
   - **With Max** – rely on his hacking abilities for a safe transfer.
10. **Retrieve the Control Ray** (for “Weapons of the Great Void”)  
    - Go to the east corridor, jump onto the platform, climb the ladder, then jump onto the next platform.  
    - Ascend to the upper level and pick up the **Control Ray**.
11. **Finish the quest**  
    - Turn around, take the elevator, go up, open the barricaded door, and return to the **Impostor**.  
    - Report back to **Lilya Hagen** to complete the mission.

## Alternative Transfer (if lacking skills)

- Overload the system regulators to force the Alta‑Vitae onto the Impostor.  
- Note: This method prevents safe navigation of Rizzo’s lab afterward (you will take damage).
