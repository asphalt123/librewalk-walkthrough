
## Acquisition
- Talk to **Samuel Huttes** at the entrance of the vegetal maze in the Purple Orchard and offer your services to start the mission.

## Walkthrough
1. After the dialogue, take the left path to meet **Salina Navarre**.  
2. Return to **Samuel**, then go right to meet **Emily Zimmerman**.  
3. After speaking with Emily, go through the nearby passage and head west.  
4. At the next intersection, continue south.  
5. At the following crossroads, turn south again and eliminate the robots.  
6. Continue west to a small orchard occupied by drones; clear the area if needed.  
7. From there, follow the southern trail until you reach a mausoleum‑like structure.  
8. From the mausoleum, head northeast to arrive at the center of the maze.  
9. In the center:  
   - Inspect the bottle on the ground.  
   - Retrieve the **certificate of completion** from the display.  
   - Pick up the unique weapon **Captain Robin's Courage** from the bench on your left.  
10. Exit the maze. You now have several options for concluding the quest.

## Ending Options

### 1. Leave the vodka with Carter
- Head northwest toward the greenhouse, find **Carter**, and confirm he is holding the original vodka.  
- Agree to let him keep the bottle. This completes the quest and grants **1,250 Octo**.

### 2. Convince Carter to give you the bottle
- Use high **Lie**, **Persuasion**, or **Intimidation** skills to persuade Carter to hand over the vodka.  
- Return to any of the NPCs (**Samuel**, **Salina**, or **Emily**) and give them the bottle for **1,250 Octo**.

### 3. Double payout
- First, accept to let Carter keep the original bottle and collect the initial **1,250 Octo**.  
- Then steal the bottle back from Carter (or loot it from his corpse) and give it to **Samuel**, **Salina**, or **Emily** for an additional **1,250 Octo**.

## Alternative Completion
- If you steal the vodka from Carter before entering the maze, you can finish the quest and receive the reward without ever stepping inside the labyrinth.
