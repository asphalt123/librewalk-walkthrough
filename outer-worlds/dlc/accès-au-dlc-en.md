
## Prerequisite Quest
1. Complete all objectives of the quest **“Radio Libre Monarque.”**

## Report to Phineas
2. Go to **Phineas**’s laboratory and deliver your report.

## Travel to a Colony
3. Return to the **Impostor** starship.  
4. Use the navigation console to travel to any destination in the **Colonies**.

## Receive the Courier Call
5. Upon arrival, you will receive a call from a courier stating that a package has been deposited in the cargo hold.

## Activate New Quest
6. The quest **“Those Who Fled Gorgon”** will activate.  
7. Open the package in the cargo hold to begin the new adventure.
