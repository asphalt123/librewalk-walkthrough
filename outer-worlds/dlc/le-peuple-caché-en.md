
## Acquisition  
**Velma**  

## Reward  
625 Octos + **Table Knife**  

## Associated Quest  
*The Misfortunes of Grimm*  

## Walkthrough  

1. Go to the Stellar Bay cannery and talk to **Velma** about **Braxton** to start the mission.  
2. Head to the apartment complex, go upstairs, and talk to **Laura** for additional information.  
3. Leave the complex and travel west to the ruins. Enter the **Mather House**.  
4. Inside, speak with the family head **Edgar**. You now have two possible approaches:  

   **Option A – Attack the Mather family**  
   - Ask **Edgar** to open the door, then attack.  
   - Kill all family members.  
   - Retrieve the **Key** from **Edgar**'s corpse.  
   - Go to the top floor and unlock the bedroom door with the **Key** to find **Braxton**.  

   **Option B – Search the house (Lockpicking 35)**  
   - After the conversation with **Edgar**, go to the second floor.  
   - Pick the lock on the left side of the bedroom (**Lockpicking 35**).  
   - Search **Braxton**'s corpse, obtain his **Badge** and the unique weapon **Table Knife**.  

5. After securing the **Badge** (and optionally the **Table Knife**), you can either:  
   - Escape through the first‑floor door, **or**  
   - Return to the ground floor to deal with the cannibals.  

6. Return to **Velma**, report the outcome, and receive your reward.
