
## Starting the Quest
1. Return to the hotel reception hall.  
2. Speak to **Agent Keene** and request the suspect list.  
3. After the dialogue, go to **concierge Halston** at the hotel entrance.  
4. Persuade or bribe Halston to gain access to the **VIP floor**.  

## VIP Floor
1. Take the elevator to the **VIP floor**.  
2. Enter **Bertie's room**.  
3. Use the **anomaly amplifier** to examine the document in the trash.  
4. Use the **anomaly amplifier** to examine the footprints in the dressing room.  
5. Leave the hotel.  

## Timonerie
1. Travel to the **Timonerie**.  
2. Enter the nearby **weather station**.  
3. Inside, inspect the mascot corpse with the **anomaly amplifier** to update objectives.  
4. Loot the **Armored Tossball Suit** from the corpse.  
5. Loot the **Rangers mascot head** from the floor (both are unique items).  

## Port Area Access
1. Complete the quest **“A fleeting taste of Purple.”**  
2. Locate the **terminal** near the bridge connecting the hotel to the port.  
3. Contact **Sedrick** at the terminal to travel to the port area.  

## Port Area
1. Deal with the bandits as part of the quest **“Lord of Chimera.”**  
2. Proceed to **Agent Gray’s mess hall** and locate **Bertie**.  
3. Interrogate the **Tossball player**.  
4. Talk to **Orick**, who is nearby, to confirm the player's alibi.  
5. Return to your room and file a report to **Ludovico**.  
6. *(Optional)* Arrest a suspect – this action has no effect on the quest outcome.
