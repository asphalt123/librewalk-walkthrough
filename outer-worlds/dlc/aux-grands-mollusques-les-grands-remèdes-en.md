
## Step 1 – Leave Helen and go to the Piraeus Port
1. After the dialogue with **Helen**, exit the area.  
2. Head toward the **Piraeus Port**.  
3. Fight through the degenerated hosts and enter the **SLUG warehouse**.  

## Step 2 – Talk to Sedrick and obtain Sprat Essence
1. Inside the warehouse, speak with **Sedrick** and explain the situation so he joins your cause.  
2. Proceed to the team leader’s office and take the **Essence of Sprat**.  

## Step 3 – Retrieve Parasite Egg Pods
1. Leave the port area and travel north to the entrance of the **Hole Caverns**, located north of the Wild Wastes.  
2. Inside the caverns, go southwest and search the small chest for **Parasite Egg Pods**.  

## Step 4 – Craft the Antiparasitic
1. Return to your hotel suite.  
2. Use the **centrifuge** to combine the **Essence of Sprat** and the **Parasite Egg Pods**, creating the **antiparasitic**.  

## Step 5 – Deploy the Antiparasitic at the Helmsman Museum
1. Leave the hotel and head to the **Helmsman Museum**.  
2. Go to the upper floor, find **PHAETON**, and release the **antiparasitic** to complete the quest.
