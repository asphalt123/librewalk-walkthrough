
## Acquisition
- **Lillie Vargas**

## Rewards
- **1250 octos**

## Walkthrough
1. Go to the bridge that connects the hotel to the orchards and talk to **Lillie Vargas** to start the mission.  
2. After the opening sequence, head to the bridge that connects the hotel to the wasteland.  
3. Eliminate all enemies there, then speak with the guard. You can be modest for a small reputation boost or persuade him to give you **200 octos**.  
4. Approach the nearby building and pick the lock, or climb the side ladder and enter through the roof.  
5. Inside, read the journal on the table to update your objectives.  
6. Follow the marker to the botanical operations area and locate a new document.  
7. Return to the surface and travel to the islet between the orchard and the hotel.  
8. Fight your way to the building marked by the marker and retrieve the **phonograph** from the bed. Listen to the recording.  
9. Go back to the hotel and descend the stairs to the employee quarters.  
10. Follow the marker to **room A2**. Examine the bottom of the door with the anomaly amplifier, then search the slab to obtain the **access card**.  
11. Open the room, kill the **parasite**, then examine **Evans'** corpse to activate new objectives.  
12. Return upstairs and head to the bridge leading to the helm.  
13. Interrogate **Amelia** to learn the truth about **Evans'** death.  
14. After the dialogue, meet **Lillie Vargas** at the landing pad and tell her the truth about **Evans'** fate. (You may lie about Amelia's intentions; the quest ends the same way and the reward is unchanged.)

## Alternative Paths
- You can go directly to **Vargas** after finding **Evans'** body and finish the quest without interrogating **Amelia**.  
- After the dialogue with **Amelia**, you can choose to attack and kill her. This only changes the outcome that **Vargas** will replace two employees instead of one.
