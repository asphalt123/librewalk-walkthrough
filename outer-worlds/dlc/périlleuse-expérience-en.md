
## Starting the Quest
1. Go to the entrance of the **Chemistry Lab**.
2. Use **Jasper’s recording** to unlock the building’s front door.
3. Enter the lab and head east toward the cavern, following the walkways.

## If You Have Jasper’s Security Pass (from “Catapulted into Orbit”)
1. Proceed directly to the locked door on the east side of the cavern.
2. Open the door with **Jasper’s security pass**.
3. Carefully navigate the trapped tunnel ahead.
4. Continue forward to reach the quest **objective** a short distance beyond the tunnel.

## If You Do Not Have the Security Pass
1. Descend to the lower level of the lab.
2. Move through the southern tunnel.
3. Fight your way past the **contaminated primates** to reach **Jasper’s office**.

## Completing the Quest
1. Interact with the **terminal** in Jasper’s office and force the unlock of the synthesis/manufacturing building.
2. Exit the building through the door located north of your current position.
3. The quest is now complete.
