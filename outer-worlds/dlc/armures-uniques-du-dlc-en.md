
## Armor List

- **Night Light** – On the ground opposite Virgile’s research site in the mine north‑east of Gorgon.  
- **Chrono‑Adaptation Suit** – Reward for completing the quest **“Lami Lama.”**  
- **Electrician Gear** – Inside a locker on the second level of the Precursor auxiliary hangar. Accessible after starting the quest **“Mechanical Pseudapple.”**  
- **Mono‑Lens of Cellular Stability** – Given by Kelley during the quest **“Catapulted into Orbit”** if you agree to place her on the Impostor.  
- **Operation Helmet (Wasteland)** – Inside a locker in the caretaker’s cabin at the Gorgon dump.  
- **Operation Armor (Wasteland)** – Reward for the quest **“Investigative Journalism”** if you turn the recordings over to NVTUR.  
- **Fanatic** – Found on the third level of the Synthesis and Manufacturing Center. Accessible after beginning the quest **“The Bureaucracy Seal.”**  
- **Fear Suit** – Located in a trash can near the Adrenachronine synthesizer on the fifth level of the Synthesis and Manufacturing Center. Accessible after starting the quest **“The Bureaucracy Seal.”**  
- **Prototype Hibernation Helmet rev 3. 19f** and **Prototype Hibernation Suit rev 4. 7d** – Both found on the second level of the basement of the Synthesis and Manufacturing Center. Accessible after initiating the quest **“Ambrose Parées.”**
