
## Quest Start
1. Enter the Roseway secret laboratory.
2. Talk to **Lillian** and accept her request to retrieve the missing **cigarette case**.

## Locate the Cigarette Case
1. From the entrance, take the path heading **East** toward the objective area.
2. When you reach the **Raptidon enclosure**, climb the hill on your right.
3. Carefully descend the hill to the walkway overlooking the Raptidons.
4. Use the **terminal** on the walkway to activate the electric barrier.
5. Eliminate the Raptidons (the barrier makes the fight easy).
6. After the fight, go down into the enclosure and pick up **Lillian’s cigarette case** near the crates.
7. Return to **Lillian** to decide what to do with the case.

## Dialogue Choices and Rewards
- **Keep the case** (requires 30 Lie)  
  *No immediate reward; you retain the **cigarette case**, which can later be sold for a good amount of Octos.*

- **Ask Lillian to talk to her boss** (requires 30 Persuasion)  
  *No reward; this choice does not affect the current quest.*

- **Return the case to Lillian**  
  *Lillian thanks you and gives you **625 Octos** plus the unique weapon **Anarchist Recipe Book**.*
