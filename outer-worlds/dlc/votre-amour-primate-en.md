
## Acquisition  
- Obtain the quest from **Cyrus Wahlgren**.  

## Rewards  
- Variable (depends on the choices you make).  

## Prerequisite  
- Complete “A Fleeting Taste of Purple”.  

## Main Steps  

1. **Travel to the Wild Ruins**  
   - Head to the hermit’s cabin in the northwest.  
   - Speak with **Cyrus** and offer your services.  

2. **Choose Your Approach**  

   - **Option A – Eliminate the Hunters**  
     1. Follow the marker to the hunters’ location.  
     2. Defeat all hunters.  
     3. Return to **Cyrus**.  
        - Receive **1500 octo** and the unique weapon **Pen**.  

   - **Option B – Help Victoria**  
     1. Talk to **Victoria** at the hunters’ camp instead of fighting.  
     2. Accept her request to retrieve the **property title**.  
     3. Go back to the hermit and convince him to hand over the document using **Persuasion**, **Lie**, or **Intimidation**.  
     4. If successful, return to the hunters to complete the quest.  
        - Receive a modest (slightly rotten) reward.  

## Alternative Path (If You Fail to Obtain the Title)  

1. You are forced to kill **Cyrus** and **Caliban**.  
2. Loot **Caliban’s Heart** from Caliban’s corpse.  
3. Return to the **Impostor** and place the still‑beating heart on your desk for admiration.
