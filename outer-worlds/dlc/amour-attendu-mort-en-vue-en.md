## “Love Awaited, Death in Sight”

## Acquisition  
**Leonora Diallo**

## Rewards  
**750 octos** + **Medal of Honor** (unique weapon)

## Steps  

1. At the Sprat Shack, go to the **first floor** and talk to **Leonora** to start the side mission.  
2. Leave the shack and head toward the **Gorgon Ruins**.  
3. Inside the ruins:  
   - Eliminate the **Marauders**.  
   - Check **Jerome’s journal** behind the bar south of the dwellings to update your objective.  
4. Go up to the **second level** of the dwellings and enter the apartment at the far end. Retrieve **Jerome’s flask** from the left‑hand bed.  
5. Return to **Leonora** with the flask to complete the quest and receive your reward, including the unique weapon **Medal of Honor**.  
6. During the final dialogue you may either reveal the truth to **Leonora** or hide Jerome’s fate; this choice does **not** affect the quest’s outcome.
