
## Quest Overview  
- **Acquisition**: Talk to **Mildred Lenox** to start the quest.  
- **Condition**: Must have completed “A Fleeting Taste of Purple”.  
- **Rewards**: Variable (see steps).  

## Main Path  

1. **Activate the mission**  
   - Locate the Legal Hunter store in the Wilds. Speak with **Mildred Lenox** and offer your services.  
   - After the dialogue, travel west to find the robot **Betty**.  

2. **Protect Betty – Eliminate the canines**  
   - Defeat the canine enemies that appear to keep **Betty** safe.  
   - Return to **Mildred** after the fight. She will reward you with **625 octas** and the unique weapon **The Attractor**.  

3. **Examine Betty** *(optional continuation)*  
   - Approach **Betty** quietly and initiate dialogue.  
   - Choose one of the following to access her maintenance hatch:  
     - Use your personal skills to pry it open, **or**  
     - Call **Parvati** for assistance.  
   - After completing the examination, go back to **Mildred** and persuade her to reveal the truth.  
   - The quest ends, granting the same reward as in step 2 **plus 10 weapon parts**.  

4. **Destroy Betty** *(alternative outcome)*  
   - If you decide to destroy the robot, do so.  
   - Return to **Mildred** and inform her of the robot’s destruction.  
   - The quest ends **without any reward**.  

## Alternative Completion  

- If you manage to bring **Betty** back alive and unharmed, you can intimidate **Mildred** for an additional **750 octas**.
