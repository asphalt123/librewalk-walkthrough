
## Prerequisite
- Complete **“A fleeting taste of purple”** quest.

## Steps

1. After the quest triggers, return to the hotel lobby and speak with **Dr. Goodnight** in his lab.  
2. Talk to the reception hostess to obtain **VIP floor access**, then take the elevator to the upper floor.  
3. Enter **Spencer's** room and interrogate the actor.  
4. Return to the lobby and speak with **Chef Anwar** in the kitchen.  
5. Leave the hotel and head toward the bridge leading to the dock. Talk to **Sedrick** at the terminal to gain entry to the area.  
6. At the dock, enter the law‑enforcement office and bribe (or hack) the **assistant sommelier** to retrieve the security footage.  
7. Go to the back office and inspect the corpse of **Maria Keene**.  
8. Travel to the island located between the ruins and the hotel:  
   - Eliminate the enemies there.  
   - Search **Owen's** corpse to update your objectives.  
9. Return to the hotel’s VIP floor and force **Spencer** to confess his crime using the gathered evidence.  
10. After the dialogue, go to your own room and report to **Ludovico** to finish the quest.  
    - You may choose to imprison **Spencer**; this decision does not affect the rest of the story.
