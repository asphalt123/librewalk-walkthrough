
## Side with Minnie and eliminate Olivia
- Enter the **CSM building** and fight through the pirate ranks.  
- If you re‑activated **PAM** during “The Seal of Bureaucracy” and it is still functional, request its assistance.  
- Head north and take the stairs to the building’s basement.  
- Clear each room of pirates.  
- Reach the final room, take the elevator, and arrive at **Olivia Ambrose**’s location.  
- Talk briefly with her, then kill **Olivia** (she cannot be persuaded).  
- Reactivate the synthesizer from the nearby console.  
- Use the marked elevator to return to the surface.  
- Go to **Minnie** at the Ambrose Manor to collect your reward and finish the DLC.

## Eliminate Minnie for Olivia
- Go to the **Ambrose Manor** and kill the robots in the lobby.  
- Go upstairs and use **Olivia’s office terminal** to unlock the secret elevator.  
- Return to the ground floor and take that elevator.  
- Neutralize the test subjects in the first room, then continue onward.  
- Deal with the robots in the hallway, then approach the far door to trigger dialogue with **Minnie**.  
- End the conversation and fight **Minnie**.  
- After the fight, enter the back room and activate the **AC protocol** from the terminal to finish the Gorgon project.  
- Note: after this point you can no longer access the **CSM building** on Gorgon.  
- Take the marked ladder, cross the cavern, and return to **Olivia** in the manor’s lounge to collect your reward and finish the DLC.

## Persuade Minnie to lay down arms (150 persuasion)
- During conversation with **Minnie**, propose an alliance with **Olivia** and point out Minnie's inability to compromise.  
- Choose the dialogue option requiring **100 persuasion** (or 100 science), then the one requiring **150 persuasion**.  
- Minnie will realize her mistakes and agree to let you destroy the CSM complex without resistance.  
- Return to the Ambrose Manor to find **Olivia** and celebrate the double victory: Minnie's survival and the lab’s destruction.

## Negotiate an agreement between Olivia and Minnie (150 persuasion)
- Go to the **Ambrose Manor** and reach **Minnie**.  
- Calm the situation and propose an alliance with **Olivia**.  
- Select dialogue options requiring **65 persuasion** (or 65 inspiration).  
- Follow up with options requiring **100** and then **150 persuasion** to convince **Minnie** to side with her mother.  
- Leave the manor and go to the **CSM** basement on Gorgon to find **Olivia**.  
- Tell her to shut up, then repeat Minnie's line “come have tea” to convince her of your good faith.  
- Return to the manor and witness the emotional reunion between mother and daughter.

## Alternative outcomes
- If you negotiate a truce between **Olivia** and **Minnie** but still destroy the CSM lab from the terminal, **Minnie** will attack you and you must kill her.  
- If you choose to be the worst rogue, you can go to the manor and then the CSM building, kill both **Minnie** and **Olivia** without destroying the laboratory. The mission will be marked complete but you will receive no reward.
