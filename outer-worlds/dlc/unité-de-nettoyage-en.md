
## Acquisition
- **Reward:** 1125 Octos + **SAM** joins your crew  
- Associated quest: **“The Vulcan Hammer”**

## Steps

1. **Complete** the quest “A Stranger in a Strange Land” to launch the **Impostor**.
2. **Board the Impostor** and go to the ship’s deck.  
   - Examine the **SAM unit** in the locker.
3. **Talk to ADA** to receive new objectives.
4. **Return to your cabin**:  
   - Access the terminal, search for information on the robot, and read the three messages that appear.
5. **Travel to Roseway**.  
   - Locate **Orson Shaw** and speak with him as part of “The Vulcan Hammer” quest.
6. **Progress** the “The Vulcan Hammer” quest until the **armory door** unlocks.
7. **Enter the armory** and retrieve the **Acid Macerator** from the shelf.
8. **Return to the Impostor**.  
   - Insert the **Acid Macerator** into the cleaning unit.
9. **SAM reactivates** and automatically joins your crew.
