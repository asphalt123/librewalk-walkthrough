
## Activate PAM (optional)

1. After the quest starts, go to the **CSM office** and unlock the door using the **terminal**.  
2. Inside, meet **PAM**, the multifunction automaton.  
3. Choose one of the following methods to reactivate PAM's combat functions:  
   - If **Parvati** is in your party, have her bypass the robot's security.  
   - If **SAM** is in your party, ask him to connect to **PAM** and recharge its batteries.  
   - Retrieve the **exemption code** from the lounge to the east and use it to override PAM's limitations.  
   - Collect a **spare battery** from the west office and install it on **PAM**.  
4. Convince **PAM** that you are an employee of **Spacer’s Choice** – you need at least **45 points** in **Lie**, **Inspire**, or **Intimidate**.

## Retrieve Olivia's Journal

1. With **PAM** reactivated, follow her through the building and help eliminate the marauder forces.  
2. Continue with **PAM** to the **3rd floor** and take the **elevator**.  
3. At the top floor, cross the area and enter **Olivia's office**.  
4. Pick up the **journal** near the terminal to complete the objective.  
5. Exit the office through the small opening on the left.  
6. Follow the mission marker back to the **elevator**, descend to the ground floor, and leave the area.  
7. Return to the **Impostor** to finish the quest.
