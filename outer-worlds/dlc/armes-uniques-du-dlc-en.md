
## **Doctor's Orders**
1. At **Ambrose Manor**, retrieve the **kitchen access card** from the caretaker’s shed.  
2. Use the card to enter the **kitchen** on the ground floor.  
3. Inside, go through the tunnel, ascend to the second floor and enter **Olivia’s office** via the secret passage.  
4. Take the **bedroom access card** from the desk, unlock the bedroom door and exit into the hallway.  
5. Open the bedroom door at the end of the hallway and pick up the unique weapon from the left furniture.

## **Grilled**
- Found on the corpse of **Wilhelmina Ambrose**.

## **Deconstruction Tool**
- Purchased from **Khaleel** at his shop in the Gorgon landing area.

## **Overheat**
1. Start at **Roscoe’s position** on the first floor of the **Sprat Cabin**.  
2. Jump onto the conveyor belt, crouch, and progress to the upper level.  
3. Retrieve the unique weapon located there.

## **Coolant Dispenser**
- Located near a corpse in the **mining accident zone**, inside the mine northeast of Gorgon.

## **Special Delivery**
- On the corpse of **Charles the Accountant**, found in the **Marauder Bastion**.

## **Perfect Match**
1. In the **Reactive Incubation Office Lobby**, either:
   - Hack the display case (**35 hacking**) via the terminal, **or**
   - Pick one of the western doors (**80 lockpicking**).  
2. Pass through the chosen door, go through the wall gap, and collect the unique weapon from the display stand.

## **Burst**
- On the bar south of the 4th floor of the **Creative Incubation Office**.

## **Luck**
- In the dead‑end west of the entrance to the **Chemistry Lab**.

## **Airstrike**
- Purchased from **Certainly Carl** at his shop in the **Gorgon Ruins**.

## **Contraband**
1. At the **Control Post**, check the southern terminal and read messages to find an access code.  
2. Use the same terminal to print an **access card** with the password `çasuffitlesconneriescarter`.  
3. Go to the northeast shed, unlock the door with the card, and retrieve the unique weapon from the left tablet.

## **Dart Pistol**
1. After obtaining the **Control Post access card** (as above), enter the **northwest shed** and take the **reserve dealer access card**.  
2. Travel to the **Gorgon Ruins**, enter the destroyed cabin north of the area, and use the card to unlock the chest containing the unique weapon.

## **Medal of Honor**
- Reward for completing the quest **“Love Expected, Death in Sight.”**

## **The B.E.N**
- Found in the secret hideout of the junior super‑scientists during the quest **“On the Trail of a Hero.”**

## **One of the Most Dangerous Games**
- Located in the locked safe (**150 lockpicking**) of the **Musgrave Collection**. Accessible via the quest **“Almost Safe.”**

## **Discharge**
- On the corpse of **Dr. Marion Blakeslee**, encountered during the quest **“Mechanical Pseudapple.”**

## **Renowned Tossball Player’s Stock**
1. During the quest **“Mercenaries versus Mercenaries,”** board the **Flibustier ship**.  
2. Retrieve the **crew access card** from the bathroom on the second level.  
3. Unlock the nearby cabin to obtain the unique weapon.

## **Storm Cannon**
- During the quest **“Catapulted into Orbit,”** go to the workshop on the second level of the station and collect the unique weapon.

## **Employee Perks**
- Given by **Dr. Jasper Low** during the quest **“Catapulted into Orbit.”**  
- Can also be taken from his corpse or stolen with **90 lockpicking**.

## **Agony**
- Found in **Test Chamber 3** of the **VAH Building**. Accessible via the quest **“At Spacer’s Choice, We….”**

## **Shock and Awe**
- Inside the **supervisor’s safe** of the VAH. Accessible via the same quest.

## **Regal Water**
1. From the quest **“Perilous Experiment,”** go to the 3rd level of the **Chemistry Lab** and retrieve **Jasper’s locker key** from the north locker.  
2. Go to **Jasper’s office** on the 2nd level, unlock the east door with that key, descend the stairs, and pick up the unique weapon from the tablet.

## **Wall of Swords**
- Reward for the quest **“Investigative Journalism”** if you turn in the recordings to **Roscoe**.

## **Gravedigger**
- Behind a locked door (**60 lockpicking**) on the first level of the **Synthesis and Manufacturing Center**. Accessible via the quest **“The Bureaucracy Seal.”**

## **Pistol Prototype**
- Found on **Olivia Ambrose’s desk** during the quest **“The Bureaucracy Seal.”**

## **Balance**
- Found on **Olivia Ambrose’s corpse** if you kill her during the quest **“Ambrose Paraded.”**  
- If you throw her into the reactor, the unique weapon appears on the command console.

## **Shredder**
- Inside the locked safe (**100 lockpicking**) of **Olivia’s personal lab**. Accessible during **“Ambrose Paraded”** if you meet **Minnie**.

## **A Shovel**
- Located in a locked trash can (**80 lockpicking**) in the **discreet cave**. Accessible via the quest **“Ambrose Paraded.”**
