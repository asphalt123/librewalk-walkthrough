
## Start the Quest
1. Accept the mission from **Sophia**.  
2. Travel to **Stellar Bay**.

## Talk to Everett
1. Go to the local restaurant in **Stellar Bay**.  
2. Speak with **Everett** and ask about **Devil's Peak**.

## Visit Nyoka
1. Locate **Nyoka** and talk to her for additional information.

## Take Control of the Relay Station
1. Follow the objective to reach the **Relay Station** at **Devil's Peak**.  
2. Complete the required actions to restore communications on **Monarque**.

## Persuade Hiram
1. Return to **Hiram**.  
2. Convince him to side with the **Council**.

## Complete the Quest
1. Go back to **Sophia**.  
2. Turn in the quest and collect your reward.
