
## Acquisition  
- Obtain the quest from **Parvati** after completing her previous mission by speaking with your mechanic on the **Impostor**.

## Reward  
- **2500 Octos** + photo of Junlei and Parvati  

## Steps  

1. Travel to the **Precursor** with **Parvati**.  
2. Purchase perfume from **Gladys**.  
3. Give the perfume to **Parvati** to receive new objectives.  
4. Return to the **Impostor** and head to **Stellar Bay**.  
5. Speak with the head chef and request a **powder‑keg stew**.  
   - If you lack **55 Intimidation**, you must pay **3000 Octos** for the stew.  
6. After obtaining the stew, go to **Monarch** and travel to the abandoned town of **Cascadia**.  
7. Enter the town, neutralize the bandits, and proceed to the former **Rizzo’s store** in the west.  
8. Inside the store:  
   - Eliminate the remaining marauders.  
   - Go upstairs and collect the **Petit Heart Cakes** from the shelf behind the bar.  
9. Give the cakes to **Parvati** and leave the area.  
10. Travel to **Byzantium** and visit **Jolicoeur’s Mercery**.  
11. Talk to **Celeste** and purchase an **outfit** for **Parvati** for **6000 Octos**.  
    - With **65 Persuasion** or **Intimidation**, you can reduce the price to **3000 Octos**.  
12. Give the outfit to **Parvati** and return to your **ship**.  
13. Dock at the **Precursor** and speak with **Parvati** again to receive new objectives.  
14. In the kitchen, approach the two women, then exit onto the **docks**.  
15. Return to your **ship**, talk to **Parvati**, and complete the quest to receive your reward.
