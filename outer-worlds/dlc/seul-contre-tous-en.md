
## Quest Overview
- **Quest Giver:** Anton Crane  
- **Reward:** **1250 Octos**  
- **Associated Quests:** “Roseway à pleines dents”, “Le marteau de Vulcain”, “Unité de nettoyage”

## Steps

1. **Talk to Anton Crane** during the “Roseway à pleines dents” quest and accept the request to locate his apprentice.
2. Leave the conversation area and travel to the building located **south of your current position**.
3. Inside the building:
   - Approach the corpse on the floor and **pick up the Access Card**.
4. Proceed **right down the corridor** and go through the door at the far end.
5. In the next room:
   - **Eliminate the robots** present.
   - **Climb the ladder** to the upper level.
6. On the upper level:
   - Enter the offices and **kill all Raptidons**.
7. After the combat:
   - Use the **Access Card** to **unlock the back door**.
   - **Talk to Jameson** inside.
8. Return to **Anton Crane** to complete the mission and claim your **1250 Octos** reward.

## Alternative Outcome
- If you **delay too long** before rescuing Jameson on the upper floor, the Raptidons will kill him. In that case, **search his corpse** for any loot before reporting back to Anton.
