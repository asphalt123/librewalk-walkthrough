
## Acquisition
- Talk to **Nyoka** at the local bar in Stellar Bay. Ask her about the informant and accept the task to retrieve her dose of **stimulants**.

## Reward
- **1125 Octos**
- **Nyoka** joins your crew

## Associated Quest
- “Free Radio Monarch”

## Steps

1. **Go to the dispensary** and speak with **Abigaïl**. Choose one of the following methods to obtain the stimulants:

   - **Bribe Abigaïl** (55 Persuasion, 2000 Octos) – During the conversation, hide who you’re getting the meds for and offer her a bribe.
   - **Intimidate Abigaïl** (55 Intimidation) – Discuss Nyoka’s case, then intimidate her to receive a dose immediately. This is the fastest option but may anger her.
   - **Steal the reserve key from Abigaïl** (40 Stealth) – Sneak behind her, take the key to the dispensary’s reserve, then head upstairs to collect the meds.
   - **Pick the lock on the reserve** (30 Lockpicking) – Go upstairs, pick the reserve door lock, and take the meds.
   - **Hack Nyoka’s prescription** (55 Hacking) – Go upstairs, hack the terminal at the end of the corridor, edit Nyoka’s daily dose in her file, then return to Abigaïl to receive the meds.
   - **Retrieve the dispensary key from Doctor Williams** – Ask Abigaïl about Doctor Williams, leave Stellar Bay, travel to the southwest cemetery, take the key from Doctor Williams’s corpse, then return to the clinic and collect the meds from the reserve.

2. **Return to Nyoka** with the retrieved stimulants.

3. **Give the stimulants to Nyoka** and accept her offer to join your crew.
