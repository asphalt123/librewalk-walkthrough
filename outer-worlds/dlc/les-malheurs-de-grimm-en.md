
## Quest Overview
- **Quest Giver:** **Grimm**  
- **Reward:** **625 Octos**  
- **Associated Quest:** “The Hidden People”

## Steps

1. **Talk to Grimm** in Stellar Bay during your first visit and accept his request to start the mission.  
2. **Speak with Célia** and ask her about the poster.  
3. **Go to the local cannery** and talk to **Velma** to learn more about the situation.  
4. Choose one of the following methods to obtain the poster from Velma:

   - **Bribe Velma** – Pay **612 Octos** to receive the poster immediately.  
   - **Intimidate Velma** – Use **55 Intimidation** to force her to give you the poster.  
   - **Persuade Velma** – Complete the quest **“The Hidden People,”** then return to Velma and use **20 Persuasion** to convince her to hand over the poster.  
   - **Pick the lock** – Use **50 Lockpicking** to break into Velma’s office chest on the upper floor of the cannery and steal the poster.  
   - **Retrieve the poster through Nell** – Continue with the steps below.

5. **Get the poster via Nell**  
   a. Talk to **Velma** about **Nell** to receive new objectives.  
   b. Travel to **Left Field** and speak with the young woman (Nell).  

6. Choose one of Nell’s options:

   - **Lie to Nell** about the poster (requires **55 Deception**).  
   - **Help Nell find her cargo** of **Tossball jerseys**.

7. If you decide to help Nell:

   a. Leave Stellar Bay and head to the **southern marker**.  
   b. Eliminate the bandits there.  
   c. Search the container to recover the **Tossball jerseys**.  

8. Return to **Nell**, give her the recovered cargo to complete her objective.  
9. Go back to **Velma** and collect the **poster** from her.
