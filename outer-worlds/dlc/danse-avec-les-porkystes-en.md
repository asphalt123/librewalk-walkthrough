
## Acquisition
- Speak to **Clive** in the Porglier factory during the quest “Clive the Butcher”.

## Reward
- **11250 Octos**  
- **Clive’s Mace**

## Steps
1. When you meet **Clive**, agree to team up with him. This activates the side mission.  
2. Travel to **Fallbrook**.  
3. Locate and kill **Catherine**.  
4. The assassination triggers a battle with the **SubLight employees** in the city; defeat them.  
5. Return to **Clive** after the fight.  
6. Receive your reward and complete the mission.  

*Note: Your reputation with the company will drop significantly after this quest.*
