
## Acquisition
**Max the Vicar** (available in Edgewater)

## Reward
**Max** joins your crew

## Associated Quest
*“A Stranger in a Strange Land”*

## Steps

1. In **Edgewater**, go to the temple and talk to **Max the Vicar**.  
   - Ask about his beliefs and his occupation.  
   - He will request your help to locate a precious manuscript.

2. Leave the town and travel east to the **Collector’s House**.

3. Inside the house, enter the small room behind the counter and read the document on the table.

4. Head west to locate the entrance to a small cave.

5. Move through the tunnel. Near the collector’s hideout, fire at the mines to explode them.

6. Unlock the safe (Lockpicking level 5) to obtain **Mr. Bakonu’s journal**.

7. Return to **Max the Vicar** and give him the manuscript.

8. Accept his invitation to join the **Impostor** (your ship).

9. After completing the quest “A Stranger in a Strange Land,” you will find **Max** aboard your vessel.
