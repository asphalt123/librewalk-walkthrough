
## Quest Steps  

1. Travel to **Eridan** using the navigation console.  
2. Talk to the bellhop at the bottom of the elevator.  
3. Follow the mission marker to the entrance of the **Grand Colonial Hotel**.  
4. Inside the hotel, go to the main lounge to locate the crime scene.  
5. Speak with **Agent Keene**, then with **Dr. Goodnight** to receive the **Anomaly Amplifier**.  
6. Use the **Anomaly Amplifier** to analyze the footprints on the floor, completing the objective.  
7. Examine the strange **letter** written on the floor by Helen (useful for later).
