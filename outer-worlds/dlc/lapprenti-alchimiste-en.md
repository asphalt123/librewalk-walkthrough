
## Acquisition
- **Vaughn Cortes** (quest giver)

## Reward
- 1875 **Octos**

## Associated Quests
- “Distress Signal”
- “Roseway at Full Teeth”

## Steps

1. Go to the Roseway laboratory complex and enter the habitation area.  
2. Talk to **Vaughn Cortes** to receive the side mission.  
3. Travel south to **Tatie Cleo’s** secret laboratory.  
4. Enter the southern part of the complex and interact with the terminal to access the Raptidon musk preparation system.  
5. Choose one of the following options at the terminal:  

   - **Recalibrate the experiment** (cost: 50 **Science**)  
     - Requires the appropriate skills.  
     - Produces **2 samples of Raptidon Musk**.  
     - You can later use **45 Lie** to deceive **Vaughn**, keep one sample, and sell it to **Gladys** for a good profit.  

   - **Complete the experiment**  
     - Produces **1 sample of Raptidon Musk**.  
     - You may give it to **Vaughn** or sell it to **Gladys**.  

   - **Purge the sample**  
     - Produces **no sample**.  
     - The quest can still be finished, but you receive no reward and your reputation with **Tatie Cleo** decreases.  

6. **Optional:** If you obtained **2 samples**, you can give both to **Vaughn** for a small **experience bonus**.
