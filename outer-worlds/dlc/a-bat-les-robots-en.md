
## Acquisition
- Talk to **Ludwig Miller** at the Edgewater landing pad to start the quest.

## Reward
- **500 Octos**
- **Electric Charger Mod**
- **Electric Staff** (unique weapon)

## Quest Steps
1. Go to the Edgewater landing pad and talk to **Ludwig Miller** to activate the mission.  
   - If you have the appropriate persuasion skill, ask him for an advance of **75 Octos**.  
2. Proceed to the small power dump near the town.  
   - Approach the robot.  
   - Choose to **repair the drone** or **destroy the robot**.  
   - *Repairing the drone* lets you find it later in the power plant during the quest **“And There Was Electricity,”** where you can reactivate it to eliminate hostile robots.  
3. Return to **Ludwig Miller** and give your report.  
4. Travel to the town and head to the cantina.  
   - Enter the back hallway.  
   - Retrieve the small package hidden behind the toilets.  
5. Return to **Ludwig Miller** to receive a Mod and new objectives.  
6. Continue the quest **“And There Was Electricity”** to reach the power plant.  
   - At the entrance, go down the ramp.  
   - Continue to the lower‑level workshop.  
   - Enter the right‑hand room to find **Jeremy**.  
   - Reactivate **Jeremy** so he cleans out the building.  
7. Go back upstairs, head north, and climb the stairs.  
   - Search the security chief’s corpse and take his **access card**.  
8. Return to the power plant entrance.  
   - Unlock the door with the **security chief’s access card**.  
   - Retrieve the **logic circuit**.  
9. Bring the **logic circuit** back to **Ludwig Miller** to complete the quest.  
   - Receive the **Electric Staff** as a reward.
