
## Objective
**Meet Sophia Akande** and complete the main mission in Byzance.

## Steps
1. After settling Udom’s debt in the previous quest, travel to **Byzance**.  
2. Locate the office of **Adjutant Akande**.  
3. Enter the hallway and speak with the **guard**.  
4. Take the **elevator** a short distance away.  
5. On the upper floor, talk to **Perceval**.  
6. Speak with **Adjutant Akande** to finish the main mission.
