
## Starting the Quest
1. Travel to **Edgewater** and talk to **Martin Abernathy**.  
2. After the conversation, leave his house. **Esther Blaine** will approach you. Listen to her dialogue.  
3. Head north to the **community center**.

## Community Center – Clear the Entrance
1. Approach the building cautiously.  
2. Eliminate the marauders guarding the entrance.  
3. Enter the building and neutralize the nearest enemy.

## Obtaining an Access Card (or Key) for the First‑Aid Room  
Choose one of the following methods based on your skill levels:

- **Hacking (Skill 25)** – Use the terminal at the community‑center entrance to encode a new **access card**.  
- **Lockpicking (Skill 5)** – Enter the southwest ground‑floor room, pick the crate, and obtain a **key**.  
- **Brute Force (Skill 100)** – Go upstairs, kill all marauders, then search the corpses for a **key**.

## Retrieve the Medicine
1. Use the **access card** or **key** to open the **first‑aid room**.  
2. Collect the stash of **experimental medicines**.  
3. Return to **Edgewater**.

## Quest Completion Options
- **Give the medicines to Martin Abernathy** – Receive **500 Octos + 300 Octos** (requires Intimidation/Persuasion 5).  
- **Give the medicines to Esther Blaine** – Receive **700 Octos + 300 Octos** (requires Intimidation 5 or Charm).  
- **Give the medicines to Reyes** – Receive **500 Octos** and a reputation bonus with the Council.
