
## Acquisition
- Talk to **Virgil Henderson** at the small camp north of the wasteland after completing **“A fleeting taste of purple.”**

## Rewards
- **750 octo**

## Steps
1. Travel to the small camp north of the wasteland and speak with **Virgil Henderson** to receive the quest.  
2. Head to the entrance of the pierced caves.  
3. Inside the caves, locate and collect the three nearest **geological study** items.  
4. Take the elevator on the south side of the caves to reach the next level.  
5. Go through the door directly ahead, eliminate all enemies.  
6. Retrieve the final **geological study** from the floor.  
7. Return to **Virgil Henderson** to complete the quest.
