
## Starting the Quest  
1. Go to the hotel pool area and talk to **Dolph McAllister** to activate the quest.  
2. If you haven’t already, visit **Dr. Goodnight** and obtain the **Anomaly Amplifier**.  
3. Analyze the pool water and inspect the filter.  
4. Return to **Dolph**, then head north to the greenhouse located north of the hotel.  
5. Inside the greenhouse, read the employee’s journal on the desk to learn about the chemical mixer.  

## Option 1 – Craft a Pool Cleaner (Standard Completion)  

1. Use the machine’s levers to load chemicals **1‑5‑4** into the appropriate reservoirs.  
2. From the terminal, prepare the cleaning mixture.  
3. Return to the pool area and insert the newly crafted **pool cleaner** into the filter.  
4. Talk to **Dolph** to receive the **SLUG Warehouse key** as a reward.  
5. (Optional) Use Persuasion or Intimidation to gain an additional **1250 credits**.  

### Accessing the SLUG Warehouse (if desired)  
1. Enter the Botanical Operations building.  
2. Descend to the lower level.  
3. Open the warehouse door using the **SLUG Warehouse key**.  

## Option 2 – Sabotage the Pool (Alternative Completion)  

1. After reading the employee journal, go to the north balcony of the hotel and talk to **Donovan**. He will offer to pay you to sabotage the pool for **Spacer’s Choice**.  
2. Accept Donovan’s offer and return to the greenhouse.  
3. Mix any chemicals together in the mixer to create an **unknown compound**.  
4. Go back to the pool and insert the compound into the filter; the water will turn into acid.  
5. Speak with **Dolph** and claim you are working for **Rizzo**. Use Intimidation (if needed) to obtain the **SLUG Warehouse key** from him.  
6. Return to **Donovan** to finish the quest and receive **1250 credits** as payment.  

## Alternative Outcome (Failed Sabotage)  

- If you sabotage the pool **before** speaking with **Donovan**, the quest is considered failed.  
- You can still obtain the **SLUG Warehouse key** from **Dolph** if your Intimidation skill is high enough.  

## Rewards  

- **SLUG Warehouse key** (if you follow the standard or sabotage path)  
- **1250 credits** (optional via Persuasion/Intimidation or guaranteed via sabotage)  
- Variable additional rewards tied to the associated main quest “The Last Role of Helen Halcyon.”
