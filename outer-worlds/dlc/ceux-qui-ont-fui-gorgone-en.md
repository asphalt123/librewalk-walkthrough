## Main Quest: “Those Who Fled Gorgon”

### 1. Meet Wilhelmina Ambrose
1. After unlocking the DLC and activating the quest, go to the Impostor’s cargo hold and retrieve the **Phonograph** from the package.  
2. Complete the cutscene, pick up the **Phonograph** from the table, and travel to **Ambrose Manor** on Gorgon.  
3. Enter the building, go upstairs, and find **Wilhelmina Ambrose** in the lounge.  
4. Exhaust all dialogue options with her to learn about Chance Montoya and the experiments on Gorgon.  
5. Return to the Impostor and travel to the Gorgon landing pad.  
6. Proceed to the **Sprat’s Cabin** (the local bar) and enter.

### 2. Follow the Trail at Sprat’s Cabin
1. Talk to **Hoffman** and let him inspect you.  
2. Use the elevator to reach the lower level.  
3. Listen to the conversation between **Freddie** and **Trixie**.  
4. Initiate dialogue with either Freddie or Trixie and agree to intervene on Lex’s behalf.  
5. From this point you have multiple ways to resolve the dispute (see section 3).

### 3. Resolve the Freddie/Trixie Dispute  
You can choose one of the following outcomes, each granting **Brine Algae x6** plus a credit reward.

- **Side with Freddie or Trixie (or side with neither)** –  
  - Listen to both testimonies.  
  - Grant the wreck rights to the chosen party (or refuse both).  
  - Lex thanks you and gives a reward of **2000 credits** plus the **Brine Algae**.

- **Accept Freddie’s bribe** –  
  - After hearing Freddie’s first story, respond with “You’re not very convincing right now, Freddie.”  
  - Accept his bribe, granting him exploitation rights.  
  - Receive an additional **4000 credits** (total **2000 credits** from Lex + **2000 extra**).  

*After completing the optional dispute, talk to Lex again and ask about Montoya to update your objectives.*

### 4. Continue After Sprat’s Cabin
1. Go to the first floor, enter the first room on the right, and meet **Leonora**.  
2. Accept Leonora’s offer to start the side mission “Lost Love, Death in Sight.”  
3. Ask her about Chance Montoya for more information.  
4. Return to the hallway and enter the room opposite to speak with **Roscoe**.  
5. Agree to help Roscoe, unlocking the side quest “Investigative Journalism.”  
6. Interrogate Roscoe about Chance.  
7. Ascend to the top floor and enter **Montoya’s bedroom**.  
8. Read the desk note, then use the discovered code to open the safe.  
9. Retrieve the **Eye** found inside.

### 5. Visit the Creative Incubation Bureau
1. Leave Sprat’s Cabin and head southeast to the **Creative Incubation Bureau** entrance.  
2. Approach the door and use the **Eye** on the scanner to unlock access.  
3. Inside, cross the lobby and take the stairs northeast to the second floor.  
4. Navigate through the offices, eliminating any marauders.  
   - If you have **35 Hacking**, use a nearby computer to reprogram the security turrets on the third floor.  
5. Continue upward to the fourth floor.  
6. Enter the **Administration Office** and use the terminal to attempt unlocking the synthesis and manufacturing center.  
7. After the first failure, try again to force access; a strange individual will contact you.

### 6. Locate Lab Personnel
1. After the conversation, return to the fourth‑floor stairs and exit the building.  
2. Traverse the area to the **Gorgon Ruins**.  
3. Enter the **Alpha Transmission Tower** and use the terminal to locate **Clarence Quasiment**.  
4. Completing this interaction finishes the main quest.
