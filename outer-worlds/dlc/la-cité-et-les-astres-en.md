
## Start the Mission
1. Go to the **Byzance cargo landing area**.  
2. Talk to **Welles' contact**.

## Infiltrate Prime Minister Clarke's Residence
1. Speak with the guard at the main door of Clarke’s house.  
2. Enter the residence.

## Retrieve the Minister's Parcel
Choose one of the following methods:

- **Persuade/Intimidate** Olive at the **Halcyon Parcel Service (HPS)** reception (requires 41 Persuasion or Intimidation).  
- **Picklock** the service entrance (requires 60 Lockpicking):
  1. Go to the maintenance tunnel entrance of Byzance.  
  2. Enter HPS through the service door.  
  3. Wait for the guard to leave, then pick the interior door.  
  4. Take the parcel from the shelf.

5. Return to the guard outside the house, show him the parcel, and he will let you in.  
6. Go upstairs, talk to **Clarke** for new objectives and activate the **“Meet Gaia”** mission.

## Reach Halcyon Headquarters (west of Byzance)
If you have completed the **“Debt Recon”** mission for Udom on the Precursor:

1. Walk directly to the Council building.  
2. All guards will let you pass; enter the minister’s office upstairs.

Otherwise, pick one of the following approaches:

- **Sneak through maintenance tunnels** (60 Lockpicking):
  1. Enter the city’s maintenance tunnels.  
  2. Unlock the western door.  
  3. Take the elevator behind the guard checkpoint.

- **Bribe** guard Stallworth at the HQ control post with **2000 Octos**.

After gaining entry:

1. Unlock the office door with the key supplied by **Clarke**.  
2. Access the terminal and read the priority message to update your objectives.

## Proceed to the Ministry of Morality and Precision
1. Exit the building, head northwest, and enter the **Ministry** building.  
2. Infiltrate the laboratory.

### Ways to Enter the Lab
- **Convince Caroline** to accompany you (70 Science): talk to her in the break room and use Science skill.  
- **Hack** the HR terminal (43 Hacking) to read Caroline’s messages, then discuss Theodore with her to gain entry.  
- **Intimidate** or **Lie** to the guard at the lab entrance (70 Intimidation or 100 Lie).  
- **Steal** a **UDL badge** (40 Stealth) from the soldier near the ground‑floor stairs and use it to enter.

3. Take the elevator to the lab level.  
4. In the right locker room, obtain a **UDL badge** if you don’t already have one.

## Reach the Hibernation Lab
Choose based on prior progress:

- If you finished the **“Laboratory Cupid”** mission (saved all Sprats), talk to **Giles** in the animal‑testing lab for the hibernation‑lab access card.  
- Otherwise, **picklock** the lab door (65 Lockpicking) if you lack the card.

7. Leave the building and head to **Phineas' laboratory** to complete the quest.

## Alternative Way to Enter Clarke’s Residence
1. Go to the Byzance bar and buy vodka for guard **Mayfield**.  
2. When he’s drunk and lying down, steal his access card.  
3. Enter Clarke’s house through the side alley.
