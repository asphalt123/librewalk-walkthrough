
## Quest Details
- **Acquisition**: Max  
- **Reward**: 2625 **Octos** + **Incense Burner** (unlocks “In Harmony” skill for Max)  
- **Associated Quests**: “The Illustrated Manual”, “Who Goes There?”, “Ship, Sweet Ship”

## Steps

1. **Trigger the quest**  
   - After obtaining the manuscript in “The Illustrated Manual”, talk to Max.

2. **Reach the Precursor security post** and gain entry. Choose one of the following methods:  

   - **Bribe Lee Flores** – 675 **Octos**  
     - Talk to Lee and give him the bribe to be let in.  

   - **Persuade Lee** – 45 Persuasion  
     - Use Persuasion skill to convince Lee to let you enter.  
     - *If you lack the required Persuasion*, complete “Who Goes There?” and “Ship, Sweet Ship” to lower the requirement to 30, then to 15.  

   - **Pick the lock** – 30 Lockpicking  
     - Go to the Precursor hangar, reach the back door of the security post.  
     - Wait for the path to clear, lockpick the door, and enter.  
     - Retrieve **Mardette’s badge** from the shelf and equip the **Holographic Veil** for a disguise.  

   - **Use the badge on the crates**  
     - Take **Mardette’s badge** from the crates near the security post.  
     - Walk through the door; the disguise activates automatically.  

3. **Collect the terminal data**  
   - Enter the small back room, access the terminal, and take the data cartridge.  
   - Give the cartridge to Max to receive new objectives.

4. **Travel to Fallbrook**  
   - Go to the outpost on Monarch, locate the hermit’s house, and retrieve the document from the shelf.

5. **Talk to the Hermit**  
   - Head to the tunnel north of town and speak with the **Hermit**.  
   - Keep Max’s aggression in check during the dialogue so he doesn’t kill the hermit.

6. **Proceed to Scylla**  
   - Return to the Impostor, travel to Scylla, then move to the abandoned outpost.  
   - Enter the hermit’s residence.

7. **Trigger the vision sequence**  
   - Talk to the old woman, then enter the left‑hand meditation room.  
   - Interact with the **Incense Burner** to start the hallucination.

8. **Complete the vision**  
   - During the vision, converse with your companions and let the sequence run its course.  
   - When it ends, the quest finishes and you receive the **Incense Burner**, unlocking Max’s “In Harmony” skill.
