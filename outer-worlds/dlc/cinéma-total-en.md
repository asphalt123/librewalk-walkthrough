
## Starting the Mission
1. Go to Byzance and talk to **Maverick Johnson** waiting in front of the cargo hangar.  
2. Accept his proposal to activate the side mission.  
3. Head northwest and enter the **Odéon** building.  

## Completing the Scene
1. Inside the Odéon, take the elevator up to the soundstage.  
2. Play through the entire scene. Your dialogue choices and actions determine the reward.  

## Reward Outcomes
- **Play poorly** (e.g., “like a potato”): receive **625 Octons**.  
- **Use most skill dialogue options**: **Maverick** is very satisfied and gives **1,125 Octons**.  
- **Kill everyone**: **Maverick** is impressed, you receive **1,125 Octons** and earn a reputation bonus with the Council.
