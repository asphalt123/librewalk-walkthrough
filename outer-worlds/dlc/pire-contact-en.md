
## Starting the Quest
1. Go to the **infirmary** on the **Precursor**.  
2. Talk to **Ellie** near the entrance and agree to visit **Jessie** to activate the companion quest.  
3. Speak with **Dr. Mfuru** and try to persuade him to let you in. If he refuses:  
   - Go to the main infirmary, approach the back door and retrieve the **Mardete badge** from the tablet.  
   - Use the **Holographic Veil** (enabled by the badge) to enter the restricted area.  
4. Pass through the door and head quickly to the right corridor.  
5. Approach **Jessie's** room and use the intercom to have her open the door.  
6. Enter the small room, discuss Jessie's problems with the council, then leave promptly before your disguise fails.  
   - If you run out of time, try to slip behind the guard, or go through the operating room, unlock the right‑hand door and follow the tunnel to the motel.

## Dealing with the Council
1. Return to **Ellie**.  
2. Visit **Udom** at the Halcyon council.  
3. Resolve Jessie's issue by choosing one of the following:  
   - Pay **1890 Octos** to **Udom**.  
   - Use **Persuasion 20** to convince **Udom** to have Jessie sign a servitude contract.

## Completing the Quest
1. Return to **Jessie**; she will give you **1000 Octos** as a reward.  
2. Talk to **Ellie** again and accept her onto the **Impostor** ship. She will join your crew.  

**Reward:** **1000 Octos** + **Ellie** joins your crew.
