
## 1. Activate the Quest  

1. Go to your suite and check the messages on the bedroom terminal.  
2. Alternatively, talk to the baggage handler **Norville** and persuade him to give you information about the Prophetess.  

## 2. Complete the Prerequisite Quest  

1. Finish the mission **“A Fleeting Taste of Purple”**.  
2. After it is completed, head to the Wild Wastes area.  

## 3. Reach the Prophetess’ Bunker  

1. Cross the bridge that leads to the Wild Wastes.  
2. Take the elevator to the upper level.  
3. Move north‑east and enter the **Prophetess of Productivity** bunker.  

## 4. Gather Evidence  

1. Inside the bunker, go to the right‑hand room and speak with the Prophetess in her tent.  
2. After the initial conversation, you need to obtain information about her alibi.  
   - Analyze the herbs on the bench behind you using the **Anomaly Amplifier**.  
3. Talk to **Aliyah Mason**.  
   - Ask a few questions, then exit the conversation to trigger a crucial piece of information.  
4. Approach the nearby terminal and read **Ruth’s** message for a new clue.  
5. Leave the bunker and analyze the footprints on the road with the **Anomaly Amplifier**.  
6. Follow the trail and inspect the mound of earth you find.  

## 5. Confront the Prophetess  

1. Return to the Prophetess now that you have all the evidence.  
2. Confront her and present your findings.  
3. She will beg you to imprison her and may even offer a payment for doing so.  

## 6. Complete the Quest  

1. Choose whether to imprison her or let her go (the choice has no impact on the rest of the game).  
2. Return to your suite and report to **Ludovico** to finish the mission.
