## The Pteromanta Trap (Side Quest)

**Acquisition:** Thelbert Wolcott  
**Rewards:** 1500 Octo, **Magnum Force** (unique weapon)

### Steps
1. Go to the **Timonerie** area and enter the museum on the navigation bridge.  
2. Speak to **Thelbert Wolcott** and accept the quest.  
3. After the dialogue, go upstairs and use **Thelbert’s terminal** to update your objectives.  
4. Retrieve the **battery** and **cutlery** at the locations indicated inside the museum.  
5. Leave the museum and enter the **weather station**.  
   - Pick up the **cables** on the floor.  
6. Follow the nearby marker and collect the **scrap**.  
7. Before returning to Thelbert, go to the **engineering camp** west of your current position and take the **windmill**.  
8. Return to **Thelbert Wolcott**, turn in all collected items, and receive the **Magnum Force** weapon.
