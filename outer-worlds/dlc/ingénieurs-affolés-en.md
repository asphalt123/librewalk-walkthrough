
## Starting the Quest
- Go to the **Botanical Lab** and talk to **Thomas** near the workbench.  
- Offer your help to activate the quest.

## Retrieve **Volume 1**
- Travel to the **Community Center** north of **Edgewater**.  
- Go upstairs and pick up the document from a desk.

## Retrieve **Volume 2**
- Go to the **Edgewater Cannery** and enter the main hall.  
- Climb to the upper level and enter Parvati’s father’s old office.  
- Find the second volume on the lockers.

## Retrieve **Volume 3**
- Advance the “And the Electricity Was” quest until you reach the **Power Plant**.  
- Proceed to the central area of the building called **The Abyss**.  
- Descend the ladder chain to the lowest level.  
- Retrieve the document near **Higgins’ bunk**.

## Completion
- Return to **Thomas** and hand over the three volumes.  
- Receive **800 Octos** and the **Charged Surface Mod** as reward.
