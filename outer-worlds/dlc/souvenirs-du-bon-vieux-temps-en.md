
## Starting the Quest  
1. Go to the tavern in Amber Heights and talk to **Ash** to receive the quest.  

## Proceed to the Abandoned Refuge  
2. After the dialogue, follow the road southwest to the **Abandoned Refuge**.  

## Clear the Outpost  
3. Eliminate the bandits inside the refuge.  
4. Enter the outpost and examine the documents on the bench.  
5. Read the messages on the terminal.  

## Cross the River  
6. Head south from the outpost and cross the river using the bridge located there.  

## Deal with the Creatures  
7. Follow the quest marker to the area with corpses.  
8. Eliminate all the **creatures** surrounding the bodies.  

## Retrieve the Box  
9. Approach the charnel and pick up the **small box** from the human remains.  

## Return to Ash  
10. Return to **Ash** and inform him of his companions’ deaths.  
11. During the conversation, reveal that you know about his schemes; he will reward you with **875 Octos** instead of the standard **625 Octos**.
