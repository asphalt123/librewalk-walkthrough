
## Meet Clarence Quäsiment  
1. Activate the quest, then return to the **Impostor** and travel to **Byzance**.  
2. Head west of the town to the **Musgrave Collection**.  
3. In the lobby, talk to **Nina**, then enter the main hall.  
4. Follow the objective marker to meet **Clarence**.  
5. Question him about the events on Gorgon and persuade him to help cancel the emergency lock.  
6. Agree to help him retrieve the **Contest Trophy** to unlock new objectives.  
7. *(Optional)* Return his eye (obtained in the previous quest) to slightly improve your reputation with the **Council**.  

## Steal the Contest Trophy  
1. After the dialogue with Clarence, locate **Tilda**.  
   - If you have **55 Persuasion**, convince her to give you the **Technician Access Card** (useful but not essential).  
2. Descend the stairs and talk to **Milburn**.  
   - Persuade him to treat his dog with more respect (requires **35 Inspiration** or **55 Persuasion**) to earn a small reputation boost with the Council.  
   - You will now have the **Anubis** dog as a companion, which can be found in your cabin on the **Impostor**.  
3. Take the elevator to the building’s basement.  
   - If you have the **Technician Access Card**, open the door on the right.  
   - If not, go left, locate the dead technician, enter the exhibit room, and use the elevator to return to the upper floor.  
4. On the upper floor, retrieve the **Contest Trophy**, unlock the side door, then return to **Clarence**.  
5. Give Clarence the **statuette** and receive the information you need to progress.
