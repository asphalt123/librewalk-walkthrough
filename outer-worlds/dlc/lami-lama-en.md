
## Mission Activation
1. Go to the fossil **southeast of the Sprat Cabin**.  
2. Talk to **Raquel** and accept the request to retrieve the **package** for **Captain Babbage**.

## Steps
1. **Create a path** through the **Marauder camp** located east of your starting position.  
2. Follow the cleared route to **Lama Rock**.  
3. **Collect the package** from the altar on Lama Rock.  
4. Return to **Raquel** with the package.  

## Completion Options & Rewards
- **Give the package without negotiating**  
  - Reward: **750 octos** + **Chrono‑Adaptation suit**.  

- **Negotiate the hand‑over** (base reward + 3,250 octos)  
  1. During the dialogue with **Captain Babbage**, select the line: “*Because you think I’m just going to give you the package like that?*”  
  2. Then choose: “*I’ll take whatever you have to offer*” to receive the extra **3,250 octos**.  

- **Intimidate or lie to the captain** (requires 80 Intimidation or 80 Deception; base reward + 3,438 octos)  
  - After initiating negotiation, pick either the **Intimidate** or **Lie** option to gain an additional **3,438 octos**.
