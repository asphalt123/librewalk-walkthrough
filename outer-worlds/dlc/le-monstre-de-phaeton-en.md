
## Acquisition  
- Talk to **PHAETON** at the Navigation Bridge Museum to receive the mission.  

## Rewards  
- Variable (see end of quest).  

## Associated Quests  
- *“To the Great Mollusks, the Great Remedies”* or *“Helen and the Snails”*  

## Steps  

1. Follow the marker and take the **elevator** down to the lower level.  
2. Proceed down the corridor and enter the **west door**.  
3. Eliminate all enemies in the room.  
4. Retrieve the **access card** from the chair.  
5. With the card, go through the **south door** and continue down the corridor.  
6. Meet **Captain Mabel Thomson**.  
   - If you have the required conversation skills, convince her to assist you (this grants the reward).  
   - Without those skills, you will complete the quest but receive no reward.  
7. Head south and cross the **engine room** at the far end of the corridor.  
8. Enter the door on the opposite side, kill the **dog**, then use the **elevator** on the left.  
9. On the lower floor, follow the marker to the large room and defeat all **giant dogs**.  
10. After the fight, call the lift at the back of the room to reach the **control room**.  
11. Talk to **Mabel** in the control room. She will give you **1,250 Octo** and a **modified light assault rifle**.  
12. Use the terminal in the control room to **realign the gyroscopic axis**.  
13. Once the objective is confirmed, backtrack through the corridor behind you and take the **elevator** to return to **PHAETON**.
