
## Starting the Quest  
- Go to the chief horticulturist’s office and talk to **DeWitt Hathaway** to activate the quest.  

## Investigate the Sabotages  
- Travel through the area and investigate the reported sabotage points.  

### Examine the Console  
- Locate the command console and interact with it to complete the **Examine Console** objective.  

### Analyze the Chemical Barrel  
- Find the barrel on the ground and inspect it to determine the composition of the chemical.  

## Resolve the Automecs Issue  
You have two ways to complete this objective:  

1. **Destroy the Drones**  
   - Go to the southern part of the zone.  
   - Destroy all drones you encounter.  

2. **Hack or Use Access Card**  
   - If you have at least **55 Hacking** or have found the **Drone Terminal Access Card**, go to the **Automecs Warehouse**.  
   - Use the terminal to deactivate the drones.  
   - While there, open the **User Register** to obtain information that leads to the quest’s true conclusion.  

## (Optional) Interrogate the Intern  
- If you accessed the User Register, return to the **Nursery Warehouse**.  
- Talk to **Anna** to learn the real reason behind the sabotages.  

## Report Back to DeWitt  
- Return to **DeWitt Hathaway** and deliver your report.  
- At this point you will be presented with several possible endings.  

### Ending Options  

1. **Support the SLUG**  
   - Choose to side with the **SLUG**.  
   - Rewards: **2,250 octo** from **Clyde**, a small reputation boost with **SubLight Underground**, and a reputation penalty with **Rizzo**.  

2. **Support Rizzo**  
   - Choose to side with **Rizzo**.  
   - Rewards: **2,250 octo** from **DeWitt**, a small reputation boost with **Rizzo**, and a reputation penalty with **SubLight Underground**.  

3. **Reconcile the SLUG and DeWitt**  
   - Use **Persuasion** to satisfy both parties.  
   - Receive **2,250 octo** from each (**Clyde** and **DeWitt**) – total **4,500 octo**.  
   - If you use **Intimidation** instead, both sides accept the deal but you only receive a single payment of **2,250 octo**.  

## Dealing with the Intern After the Main Conflict  
- After resolving the SLUG‑Rizzo dispute, you can address Anna’s situation.  
  - To keep Anna employed, repeatedly select the first dialogue option in each conversation.  
  - To have Anna fired, convince **DeWitt** to dismiss her, removing her from the game.  
- In either case, **DeWitt** gives an additional **2,250 octo** for your services.  

## Alternate Path  
- If you never accessed the intern’s information on the Automecs terminal, you can still finish the quest, but you will miss the extra **2,250 octo** bonus from **DeWitt** for uncovering the sabotage truth.
