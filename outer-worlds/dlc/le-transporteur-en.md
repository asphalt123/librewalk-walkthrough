
## Steps

1. **Go to Amber Heights** and talk to **Graham**, leader of the **Iconoclasts**, to receive the quest.  
2. After the dialogue, leave the camp and head north to the **Bayside warehouse**.  
3. Approach **Niles** and initiate conversation. Choose one of the following approaches:  
   - Use **Lie** (55), **Persuasion** (55), or **Intimidation** (100) to convince Niles to leave the area.  
   - Eliminate all **SubLight** employees (this will significantly lower your reputation with that faction).  
4. Proceed to the **warehouse** and use the **intercom** to contact **Carlotta**.  
5. Enter the warehouse, meet **Carlotta**, and obtain the **cargo**.  
6. Collect either **provisions for Zora** or **ammo for Graham**.  
7. Return to **Amber Heights** and complete the quest.
