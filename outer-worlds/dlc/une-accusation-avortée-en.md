
## Prerequisites
- Complete **at least two** of the following missions:
  - **Prince of Tossball**
  - **The Disturber**
  - **Queen of Productivity**
  - **Lord of Chimera**
- Starting this quest before finishing all four will cause the remaining missions to fail.

## Steps
1. **Activate the quest** – it triggers automatically after meeting the prerequisite.  
2. **Go to your bedroom**.  
3. **Talk to Ludovico** and choose the dialogue option to announce you’re ready to reveal **Helen’s assassin**.  
4. **Select a suspect** – you may accuse any character, including **Ludovico** himself.  
5. **Confirm the accusation** to complete the quest and unlock the next main objective.
