
## Acquisition
- **Junlei Tennyson**

## Reward
- **3750 Octos**
- **Welding Goggles** (unique armor piece)

## Associated Quest
- “Who’s There?”

## Steps
1. Go to the Precursor's Engine Room and talk to **Junlei Tennyson**. Offer your services to receive the mission.  
2. Leave the room and take the elevator directly opposite to reach the Secondary Docks.  
3. If you have not completed the “Who’s There?” quest, you will encounter Captain **MacRedd**. Choose one of the following approaches:  
   - **Negotiate** (requires 40 Persuasion/Intimidation). Calm him, mention the part you are retrieving, then either:  
     - Bribe him for **602 Octos**, or  
     - Bluff him with 40 Deception to gain access to his hideout.  
   - **Attack** and defeat him in combat.  
4. In either case, proceed to the back of the room and climb the stairs on the north‑west side to the upper floor.  
5. At the top, collect the **Thermal Dissipator** from the back of the room.  
6. *(Optional)* Return to the main room, go down, and speak with soldier **Lettie Dixon**, who was sent by **Junlei**.  
7. Leave the area and return to **Junlei** for new objectives.  
8. After the dialogue, head to the west side of the Engine Room and take the door on your right.  
9. Use the elevator to go to the lower level and eliminate all hostile creatures in the vicinity.  
10. After the fight, climb the ladder at the far end and enter the first room on the right.  
11. Turn left, ascend the stairs, enter the next room, then descend the left ladder.  
12. At the bottom, use the terminal to repair the thermal dissipators.  
13. Return to **Junlei**, report the completed task, and receive your reward (**Welding Goggles**, **3750 Octos**).
