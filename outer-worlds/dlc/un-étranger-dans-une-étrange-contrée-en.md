
## 1. Arrival and Cave  
1. After the opening cutscene, go down the slope and follow the guide until you spot enemies.  
2. Hide in the tall grass and make your way to the **cave**.  
3. Inside, follow the marker and talk to **Pelham**.  
   - Use your **Medicine** skill to heal him; he will reward you with the **Sentinel Blade**.  
4. When the conversation ends, shoot the barrels to explode them, then exit the cave.  

## 2. Dealing with Marauders  
1. Outside the cave, a group of marauders is waiting.  
   - Approach quietly and detonate any explosives to start the fight on favorable terms.  
2. Move a little farther, stealth‑kill another enemy group, then continue onward.  

## 3. Lieutenant Mercer  
1. At the end of the path, meet **Lieutenant Mercer**.  
   - If you have **Persuasion**, convince her to lead the assault on the enemies around the ship.  
   - If not, defeat the marauders yourself.  

## 4. Ship Interior  
1. Enter the ship and go to the cockpit to meet **ADA**.  
2. After the dialogue, leave the ship; **Mercer** will stop you and demand identification.  
   - Choose to lie, tell the truth, or kill her (killing harms your standing with Spacer’s Choice).  

## 5. Travel to Edgewater  
1. Head toward the town of **Edgewater**.  
2. Talk to **Silas** and ask about the **Electrical Regulator**.  
3. Enter the town, go to the cannery, and take the elevator to **Reed’s** office.  

## 6. Acquiring the Regulator  
1. Speak with **Reed**, then exit and talk to **Parvati**.  
2. Retrieve the **Electrical Regulator** for your ship (refer to the “And the Electricity Was” quest details if needed).  
3. Return to your ship with the regulator and speak to **Parvati**.  
   - She will ask to join you on future voyages; **accept** this invitation, otherwise she will leave permanently.  

## 7. Installing the Regulator  
1. Re‑enter the ship and talk to **ADA** again.  
2. **Install the Electrical Regulator** in the Engine Room.  
3. Return to your navigator and **leave the planet** to complete the quest.
