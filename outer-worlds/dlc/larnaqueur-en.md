
## Steps

1. After finishing **“The Price of the Press”**, go to the infirmary, talk to Zora, and accept her request to start this mission.  
2. Travel west to the relay station and clear the surrounding enemies.  
3. Use the access card provided by Zora to unlock the building.  
4. Inside, loot the **Hemlock Eyepatch** armor from the corpse.  
5. Retrieve the **Data Block** from the crate on the left.  
6. Return to Zora and report your findings to complete the quest.
