
## Main Quest

1. Travel from **The Impostor** to the auxiliary maintenance hangar of the **Precursor**.  
2. If you have already completed **“Catapulted into Orbit,”** you will be intercepted by pirates from the **“Mercenaries vs. Mercenaries”** mission. Defeat the pirates.  
3. Disembark, proceed down the corridor, and talk to the guard.  
   - During the conversation, attempt to **bribe** or **lie** (Lie 85) to convince him to let you pass.  
4. After the dialogue, cross the area, open the far door, and go down the stairs.  
5. Clear the lab of the robots.  
6. Once the lab is cleared, head west down the corridor, destroy the drones, and take the elevator at the far end.  
7. On the final level, neutralize the security drones in the plantation and meet **Marion** in her lab.  
8. Retrieve the **VAH Pass**.  
9. Talk to Marion for advice, then persuade her (Persuasion 55) to give you additional information and obtain her **security code** (optional but saves time later).

## Alternative Paths

- **Kill everything** in the area, including Marion, instead of negotiating with the guards. You will still obtain the **VAH Pass**, but no extra information.  
- **Avoid combat and negotiation** by stealthily infiltrating the lab through the side passage on the left side of the main corridor.
