
## Quest Details
- **Acquisition:** Lilya Hagen  
- **Reward:** 2188 Octos  
- **Associated Quest:** “Syndicate of the Cosmocrime”

## Steps
1. **Talk to Lilya** after finishing “Syndicate of the Cosmocrime” to receive the quest.  
2. **Travel** to satellite **SRH‑1084** in your ship.  
3. **Enter the hangar** and head to the northwest corridor.  
   - Destroy the ground mines.  
   - Descend to the lower level.  
4. **Use the terminal** to restore power in the base and make contact with **UDL** troops.  
5. **Deal with the corporate troops** (choose one):  
   - **Negotiate:** Offer money, intimidate (**65 Intimidation**) or lie (**65 Persuasion**).  
   - **Combat:** Fight the UDL assault troops if you lack the required skills or prefer not to spend Octos.  
6. After either outcome, **go back upstairs** and head to the southeast crates.  
   - Enter the conduit and proceed to the room at the far right.  
7. In the office, **pick up the **blank map**** from the left‑side furniture and **use the terminal** to encode it.  
8. **Return** the way you came and pass through the east door.  
9. **Descend the stairs**, open the right‑hand door using the **access card**, then **use the terminal** to disable the adjacent room’s security system.  
10. **Return to the corridor**, enter the next room, go through the door and head west.  
11. **Cross the laboratory** and enter the office at the top of the stairs.  
12. **Use the terminal** to access security routines and **insert the **cartridge**** provided by Lilya to complete the objective.  
    - If you have **65 Hacking**, you can also unlock the nearby door and **retrieve the **Seeker Mask**** from the safe.  
13. **Return to your ship** and **report back to Lilya** to finish the mission.
