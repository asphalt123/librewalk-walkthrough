
## Acquisition
- Locate the secret society terminal **(Subordinate Super‑Scientists)**.

## Rewards
- **2250 octos**
- Unique weapon **Le B.E.N**

## Quest Steps

1. **Activate the quest**  
   - Go to the entrance of the secret hideout, located in the cave east of the Sprat Cabin. Interact with the terminal.

2. **Collect the four clues** scattered across Gorgon.

   - **Clue 1** – From the hideout, cross the southern bridge and follow the path to Maurice’s research site. Retrieve the comic book there.  
   - **Clue 2** – Head to the mine northeast of the hideout, then proceed to the far end of the tunnel to Virgile’s research site and pick up the second comic book.  
   - **Clue 3** – Use the southern bridge again, follow the trail to the Gorgon Ruins, move through the housing complex, then go north to Brigitte’s research site and claim the third comic book.  
   - **Clue 4** – Take the elevator at the southern edge of the area to reach the dump. Follow the upper‑level path to Eustache’s research site and obtain the final comic book.

3. **Return to the hideout** and interact with the terminal again.  
   - Enter the password to start the **“True Fans Quiz.”**  
   - *Optional:* If you have at least **150 Hack** skill, activate the **Super‑Villain Protocol** to skip the quiz and gain a small XP bonus.

4. **Answer the quiz** (required if you do not skip it):

   1. **Sissak Sassafrass**  
   2. **The skull of the mother of King Alpha**  
   3. **By deceiving Archibald with his vile mind‑control ray**  
   4. **With a lightning strike from the sky**

5. **Unlock the hideout door**, enter, and collect the rewards: the **2250 octos** from the locker and the unique weapon **Le B.E.N** from the table.
