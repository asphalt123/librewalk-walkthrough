
## Acquisition
- Obtain from **Graham** after completing “The Transporter” mission.

## Rewards
- **2188 Octos**
- **Apostle armor** (Iconoclast faction)
- Optional: **Medical kit** and **Euthanasia Kit** (unique weapon)

## Associated Quests
- “The Transporter”
- “Free Radio Monarch”

## Steps
1. Talk to **Graham** to receive the quest.  
2. Travel to the Terra‑1 Publishing building.  
3. Approach the **Van Noy** and start dialogue.  
4. Ask the **Van Noy** to return to Amber Heights and agree to help locate their doctor.  
5. Head to the main building, turn right, and eliminate the enemies.  
6. Search the doctor's corpse in the small building at the back to obtain the **medical kit** and the unique weapon **Euthanasia Kit**.  
7. Return to the **Van Noy** and give them the **medical kit** to complete the optional objective.  
8. Enter the main Terra‑1 Publishing building and clear the area.  
9. After the fight, go to the western room and go upstairs.  
10. Enter the second room on the left, locate the terminal, and use it to repair the press.  
11. Return to **Graham** to report the mission.  
12. Receive an additional **750 Octos** bonus for rescuing the **Van Noy**.

## Alternative Path
- During the conversation with the **Van Noy**, you can persuade, intimidate, or lie (60 persuasion) to convince them to assault the building. If successful, they will assist you during the zone cleanup.
