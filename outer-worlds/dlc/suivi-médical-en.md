
## Acquisition
- **Leonard Wheeler**

## Reward
- **1500 Octos** + various consumables

## Steps
1. Talk to **Leonard Wheeler**, the soldier in charge of the control post, and offer your services.  
2. Head to the infirmary and speak with **Wanda** to learn about the missing cargo.  
3. Return to the **Impostor** and set a course for **Scylla**.  
4. Upon arrival, take the elevator down to the ground level and proceed south.  
5. At the far end of the asteroid, encounter the bandits responsible for the delivery delay. Eliminate all of them.  
6. Speak with **Captain Irion**.  
7. Explain why you are there; he will give you **Irion's Silox Rifle**.  
8. Return to **Wanda** on the Precursor and give her your report to complete the quest.
