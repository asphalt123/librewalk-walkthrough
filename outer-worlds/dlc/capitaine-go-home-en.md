
## Objective  
- **Talk to ADA** on your ship to finish the DLC storyline.  

## Steps  
1. Board your ship.  
2. Find **ADA** (the ship’s AI) and start the conversation.  
3. Complete the dialogue to conclude the mission.  

## Alternate Dialogue (if you sided with **Helen** instead of **Ludovico** during the “By Instinct” quest)  
- After speaking with ADA, you can briefly talk to **Sedrick Kincannon** and **Helen Halcyon** before leaving the planet.
