
## Steps
1. Immediately after activating the quest, head **south**, then **west**.  
2. In the back room, avoid the steam jets and wait for **Helen** to open the next door.  
3. Continue down the corridor and cross the bridge at the far end.  
4. Pull the lever on the right.  
5. Go through the opposite door and check in with **Helen**.  
6. After the dialogue, use the nearby **ladder** to climb to the surface.  
7. Proceed to your hotel suite and talk to **Helen** to finish the mission.  

## Optional Reward
- If you have **40 or more Persuasion**, you can convince **Helen** to give you **the Switchblade** (her iconic pistol).
