
## 1. Arrive at Monarque  

1. After obtaining the **navigation key** for Stellar Bay, travel to Monarque and land in the town.  
2. Go to the **Yacht Club** and talk to **Nyoka**.  
3. Ask about the hermit and accept the **“Pilules fétiches”** quest – the hunter will join your crew.

## 2. Reach the Summit of Devil’s Peak  

You can either take the trail or go through the caves.

### Option A – Trail  

1. Follow the path straight to the summit, eliminating hostile creatures along the way.  
2. Proceed to the **E3 barracks** and speak with the mercenaries to start the **“Mandibules diaboliques”** quest.

### Option B – Caves  

1. Enter the caves south of the mountain.  
2. Pass under the arch opposite the entrance and follow the tunnel to the upper level.  
3. Continue forward until you encounter a giant regal mantis.  
4. **Kill the mantis** to trigger the **“Mandibules diaboliques”** quest, then talk to **Berthold**.  
5. Exit the caves on the south side and head back to the **E3 barracks** to find Berthold again.

## 3. After Reaching the Summit (Either Route)

1. Talk to **Berthold** after completing **“Mandibules diaboliques”** so he will assist you in the upcoming battle.  
2. Climb back up the trail and meet **Joy**. Talk to her, then initiate the assault on the marauders.

## 4. Assault on the Bandits  

1. Attack the bandits and eliminate all enemies outside the building.  
2. Go upstairs and enter the building.  
3. Inside, clear the first room of enemies, then head north.  
4. Go through the door at the back of the room and use the intercom to speak with the hermit.  
5. Turn around and take the elevator located to the right of the previous room.

## 5. Central Hall and Final Enemies  

1. On the upper floor, reach the central hall and clear the brigands with help from the **E3 mercenaries**.  
2. After that, defeat the remaining enemies on the top level.  
3. Use the intercom again so the hermit will open the door for you.  

## 6. Conversation with Hiram  

1. Talk to **Hiram** to learn about his connection to **Wells**.  
2. Complete the following side quests to restore communications on Monarque:  
   - **“His Name Is BOLT”** (MSI)  
   - **“Hidden Errors”** (MSI)  
   - **“The Transporter”** (Iconoclasts)  
   - **“The Price of the Press”** (Iconoclasts)  
3. Return to **Hiram** for new objectives once the quests are finished.

## 7. Reactivate Communications and Finish the Main Quest  

1. Go to the back room, exit through the door, and reach the roof.  
2. Approach the console and **reactivate the communications**.  
3. Leave the area and head to **Wells’s laboratory** to complete the main quest.

## 8. Alternative Paths  

- You may travel to Monarque before acquiring the **navigation key** for Stellar Bay, but you will need to cross half the planet to reach the town.  
- During the dialogue with **Nyoka**, you can refuse her assistance and attempt to reach the hermit on your own.
