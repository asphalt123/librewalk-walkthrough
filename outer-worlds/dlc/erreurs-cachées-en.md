
## Starting the Quest
1. Complete **“His name is BOLT”**.  
2. Accept **Sanjar**’s new proposal to start the “Hidden Errors” quest.  
3. Travel to **Fallbrook** and talk to **Catherine**.  

## Finding the UDL Laboratory

### Option A – Retrieve Catherine’s Courier
1. Accept the task to locate the missing SubLight employee.  
2. Go to the **destroyed bridge**.  
3. Inspect the automatic loader, then follow the blood trails to the concealed hatch hidden in the tall grass.  
4. Descend carefully, avoiding mines and electric arcs, until you reach **Arthur** at the end of the cavern.  
5. Talk to **Arthur** for new information, then leave the area.  
6. Return to **Catherine**, report the findings, and earn a reputation boost.  
7. Proceed to the **UDL laboratory** location.

### Option B – Interrogate Huxley
1. Use **100 Lie** or **100 Intimidation** to force **Catherine** to reveal her information.  
2. Meet with **Westley** and **Yvonne** to update your objectives.  
3. Travel to **Amber Heights** and speak with **Huxley** to learn the exact location of the UDL lab.

## Retrieving Lab Data
1. Arrive at the laboratory and watch the scientists’ death cutscene.  
2. Neutralize the bandits inside the main building.  
3. Enter the building’s **right‑hand room**, then go upstairs.  
4. Enter the **left control room** and use the terminal to **download the data**.  
5. While inside, pick up the **access card** from the desk.  
6. Use the access card to reach the **Goo Weapon** on the ground floor, completing the **“Weapons of the Great Void”** objective.  
7. Return to **Sanjar** to finish the quest.

## Alternative Paths
- You may skip the information‑gathering steps and head straight to the UDL laboratory.  
- During the dialogue with **Huxley**, you can bribe her or persuade her with skills; she will then give you the **Chastity hideout access card**. Use this card on the terminals around the lab to trigger the alarm or unlock the large north door.  
- If **Huxley** died during “His name is BOLT”, you cannot interrogate her for information.
