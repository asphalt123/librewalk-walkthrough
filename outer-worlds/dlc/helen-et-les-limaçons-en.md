
## Quest: “The PHAETON Monster”

## Steps

1. **Travel** to the helm and enter the navigation‑bridge museum.  
2. Fight through the SLUG soldiers inside and **talk to PHAETON** to update your objectives.  
3. Proceed to the lower levels to reactivate the reactor:  
   - Use the **south elevator**, **or** if you have already completed “The PHAETON Monster” quest, use the **north elevator**.  
4. In the main maintenance room, **neutralize the SLUG troops** and **eliminate Sedrick Kincannon**.  
5. Pull the indicated lever, then return upstairs to locate **PHAETON**.  
6. Upon reaching the computer, **Helen Halcyon** appears.  
   - Talk to her briefly, then **kill her**.  
7. **Loot** her corpse for the unique items **Needle** and **Outfit of the Day (Option 2)**.  
8. With the items in your inventory, **activate the gas‑dispersion procedure from PHAETON** to complete the quest.
