
## Starting the Quest
1. Activate the quest **Par l'instinct mû**.
2. Head toward the **Distillation Station**.
3. Fight your way through the hosts until you reach the **side door** of the building.

## Inside the Distillation Station
1. Enter the station through the side door.
2. Interact with the **intercom** on the wall to learn the truth about **Helen**.
3. After the dialogue, you will be presented with a choice.

## Decision Point
- **Choice A – Work with Ludovico**  
  *Select to accept a partnership with the administrator Ludovico.*

- **Choice B – Ally with Helen**  
  *Select to join forces with Helen Halcyon.*

## If You Choose to Work with Ludovico
1. The quest **Helen Halcyon's Infernal Explosive Machine** is unlocked.
2. Follow the new quest line, which ultimately leads to condemning Eridan.

## If You Choose to Ally with Helen
1. The quest **Factory Escape** is activated.
2. Continue with the subsequent missions that focus on saving the colony from parasites.
