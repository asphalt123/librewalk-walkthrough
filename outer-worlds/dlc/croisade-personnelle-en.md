
## Starting the Quest
1. During your first visit to **Byzantium**, talk to **Ellie** in front of the ship's airlock to trigger the quest.  
2. Enter the town and head west to **Ellie's house**.

## Meeting the Parents
1. Inside the building, go into the room on the right to meet **Ellie's parents**.  
2. Choose any dialogue options you prefer throughout the conversation.  
3. Exit to the street and talk to **Ellie** again to receive new objectives.

## Deliver the Mail
1. Follow the new quest marker to the **mailbox** and inspect it.

## Travel to Fallbrook
1. Travel to **Fallbrook**.  
2. Visit the **insurance company** there.

## Modify the Insurance Clause
1. Find a way to change **Ellie's insurance contract clause**.  
   - You can **bribe** the employee, **or** use your **Intimidation** skill.

## Finish the Quest
1. Return to **Ellie**, discuss the outcome, and complete the mission to claim your reward.

## Reward
- **1250 Octos**  
- **Framed insurance check**
