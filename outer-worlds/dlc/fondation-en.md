
## Prerequisite
- Complete the mission **“And the Electricity Was”** and place **Adelaide** in charge of **Edgewater**. This quest is only available after that outcome.

## Steps
1. Travel to the **Emerald Valley**.  
2. Head toward the **Geothermal Plant**.  
3. Enter the plant’s main building and speak with **Hogarth**. Convince him to allow you access to the interior.  
4. Once inside, cross the facility and locate the designated **terminal**. Use it to activate the robot‑elimination protocol.  
5. Proceed to **Edgewater**. Eliminate all remaining **robots** roaming the streets.  
6. Return to **Sophia Akande** to complete the mission.
