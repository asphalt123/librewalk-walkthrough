
## Starting the Quest  
- Locate **Commander Sanita** near the control station on the **Precursor** ship.  
- Talk to her and accept the quest.  
- After the dialogue, head to the rear of the ship.  
- Take the elevator down to the **Secondary Docks**.  

## Negotiating with MacRedd (40 Persuasion / 40 Intimidation)  
- Find **MacRedd** in his hideout.  
- Talk to him and try to calm him down.  
- Tell him you are here to collect the bounty on his head.  
- Use **Persuasion** (or **Intimidation**) to convince him to negotiate.  
- He will give you his **lighter**.  
- Return to **Commander Sanita** and report MacRedd’s “death”.  
- The quest ends and you receive the reward.  

## Killing MacRedd  
- Enter **MacRedd’s** lair.  
- Eliminate all enemies (any weapons work; the area will be on fire).  
- Loot **MacRedd’s** corpse for the **lighter** and the unique **flamethrower “Montag”**.  
- Scavenge the surrounding area for additional loot.  
- Return to **Commander Sanita** to complete the quest and claim the bounty.  

## Rewards  
- **1250 Octos**  
- **Detox x2**  

*Note: You need at least 40 Persuasion or 40 Intimidation to use the negotiation option.*
