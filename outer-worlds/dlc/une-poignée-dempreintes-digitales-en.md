
## Acquire the Quest  
- Go to the local police station in Edgewater.  
- Talk to **Agent Reyes** to receive the bounty quest.  
- Use the nearby terminal to view the wanted posters and learn about the three targets.  

## Hunt the Targets  

1. **Bertie Cotton**  
   - **Location:** West of Edgewater, in a small scavenger camp.  
   - Eliminate the target and loot **one of his fingers**.  

2. **Guillaume Antrim**  
   - **Location:** Northeast of Edgewater, among a group of bandits.  
   - Eliminate the target.  
   - While there, speak with **Zoé** to progress the side quest “Kind Spirits”.  

3. **Dr. Maybell**  
   - **Location:** Northwest of Edgewater, inside a hidden cave in the mountain.  
   - Wait at the cave entrance for the target and the marauders to exit, then eliminate them.  

## Complete the Quest  
- Return to **Agent Reyes** after all three targets are dead.  
- Collect the reward: **1500 Octo** and **Junior Adjunct Agent badge**.
