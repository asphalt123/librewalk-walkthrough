
## 1. Activate the quest  
1. During the “Passport for Adventure” quest, talk to **Gladys** and accept her request to investigate the distress signal.  
2. Go to the **Precursor** and listen to the recorded message. This triggers the “Roseway at Full Teeth” quest.

## 2. Travel to Roseway  
1. Head to the **Roseway landing zone**.  
2. Proceed to the **Roseway Laboratory**.

## 3. Talk to the key NPCs  
- Speak with **Anton Crane** (related to “Roseway at Full Teeth”).  
- Speak with **Orson Shaw** (related to “Vulcan’s Hammer”).  
- Speak with **Vaughn Cortes** (related to “The Apprentice Alchemist”).

## 4. Complete objectives for each associated quest  

### Roseway at Full Teeth  
- Retrieve the **Improved Toothpaste Formula** data.  

### The Apprentice Alchemist  
- Retrieve the **Raptidon Muscle** data.  

### Vulcan’s Hammer  
- Retrieve the **FORCE Weapon Prototype** data.  

*Note: If you decide to keep any of these items for the scavenger instead of selling them, you will be unable to finish the associated side quests.*

## 5. Sell the gathered information to Gladys  
- Sell the **Improved Toothpaste Formula** → **3,750 Octos**  
- Sell the **Raptidon Muscle** → **1,875 Octos**  
- Sell the **FORCE Weapon Prototype** → **1,875 Octos**

## 6. Report back to Gladys  
- Return to **Gladys** and deliver the report to complete the main quest.  
- Receive an additional **375 Octos** as a reward.

## 7. Optional: Keep the intel for the scavenger  
- You may choose not to sell any of the data.  
- Return to the scavenger and inform her that nothing valuable was found (no money received).  
- The rewards obtained from selling the Roseway intel (steps 5‑6) will still offset the loss.
