
## Getting Started
1. Activate the quest.
2. Head toward the **Distillation Station** and make your way to the main entrance.

## Inside the Distillation Station
1. Open the door marked by the quest marker.
2. Take the elevator down to the lower level.
3. On the lower level:
   - Go through the western door, then the southeast door.
   - Eliminate any enemies you encounter.
   - Ascend the stairs to the next floor.
4. On the upper floor:
   - Clear another group of enemies.
   - Use the elevator on the opposite side of the walkway to go up one more level.

## Reaching Administrator Ludovico
1. Continue north‑west.
2. Traverse the western corridor.
3. Open the back door to find **Administrator Ludovico**.

## Fight the Queen of Parasites
1. After the dialogue with Ludovico, the **Queen of Parasites** battle begins.
2. Defeat her by:
   - Using your most powerful weapons and abilities, **or**
   - Hacking the terminals in each corner of the room to activate the steam distribution systems.

## Loot
1. Search Ludovico’s body for:
   - **Ludovico’s Access Card**
   - Unique items **Salve Nova** and **Administrator’s Attire**.

## Quest Completion
1. Follow the quest marker to talk briefly with **Harwell**.
2. Use the indicated exit access to leave the area and finish the quest.
