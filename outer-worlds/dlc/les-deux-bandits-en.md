
## Acquisition  
- **Dash Whitlock**  

## Rewards  
- Variable (depends on your choices)  

## Condition  
- Complete **“A fleeting taste of Purpelle”**  

## Walkthrough  

1. After finishing **“A fleeting taste of Purpelle”**, travel to the **Pirée Spaceport** and locate **Dash Whitlock** in the **Grey Agent Cantina**.  
2. Talk to **Dash**, then head to the **Law Enforcement Headquarters**. Speak with **Cassidy** in the cells and give her **the password** so she reveals details about **the cargo** you need to retrieve.  
3. Acquire **the cargo**:  
   - **Option A** – Go to the **Delivery Zone**, talk to **Benton**, and either pay him or persuade him to hand over **the cargo**.  
   - **Option B** – Enter the delivery area through the southeast breach and collect **the cargo** from the marked container.  
4. Return to **Cassidy**, then proceed to **Joseph’s** shop.  
5. Give **Joseph** **the password**, choose your response, and convince him to take you to the backroom. Hand him **the package** and gain access to his illegal shop.  
6. Choose how to handle the payment:  
   - **Keep the full bounty** – In the final dialogue with **Dash**, keep **3,750 octo** for yourself. The mission is marked as a failure.  
   - **Give 1,000 octo** – Keep **2,750 octo** and finish the mission normally; the smugglers become hostile (no impact on the main story).  
   - **Give 2,000 octo** – Pay the agreed amount; the smuggler thanks you.  
   - **Report the smugglers** – Return to the **Law Enforcement Headquarters** and report them for a reward of **1,250 octo**. You may report them at any time, even before retrieving **the cargo**, but the mission will then count as a failure.  
7. **Optional:** Double your payment by keeping the money given by **Joseph** and then reporting it to the police.
