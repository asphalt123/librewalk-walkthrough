
## Acquisition
- **Quest Giver:** **Agnès Needham** (Stellar Bay)

## Reward
- **625 Octo**

## Main Steps

1. **Talk to Agnès** at the southeast exit of Stellar Bay.  
   - Accept her request to find her missing son to start the quest.
2. Leave Stellar Bay and travel south to the **Iconoclast Camp**.
3. Enter the small building on the north side of the camp to meet **Tucker**.

## Options for Completing the Quest

### 1. Kill Tucker
- If you cannot reach an agreement with Tucker, kill him.  
- Loot the **ring** from his corpse.  
- Return to **Agnès** to finish the quest (you will receive a large reputation penalty with the Iconoclasts).

### 2. Persuade Tucker (Persuasion 55)
- Use a Persuasion score of **55** or higher to convince Tucker to go back to his mother.  
- Travel with Tucker to Stellar Bay, speak to **Agnès**, and complete the quest for the reward.

### 3. Threaten Tucker’s Mother (Intimidation 35)
- During the initial conversation with Tucker, use an Intimidation score of **35** or higher to threaten his mother’s safety.  
- Force Tucker to return to Stellar Bay.  
- Afterward, you can:
  - Collect the reward from **Agnès** and leave Tucker under the threat of violence, **or**
  - Allow Tucker to leave and live his life.  
- Both choices affect your reputation but complete the quest.

### 4. Simulate Tucker’s Death
- Agree to Tucker’s proposal to retrieve his valuable **ring**.  
- Return to **Agnès** and lie about Tucker’s fate to finish the quest and claim the reward.  
- Alternatively, you can reveal the truth to Agnès, which will fail the plan entirely.
