
## Starting the Mission  
1. Activate the mission and travel to **Hope**.  
2. Proceed along the docks and insert **ADA** into the ship’s auxiliary power feed.  

## Choose Your Approach  
You can progress on Hope by using force, infiltration, or dialogue. The steps below cover both the persuasion and infiltration methods.

---

## Persuade the Guards (requires 50 Lie + 50 Science/Engineering or 80 Lie)  
1. Talk to **Quincy** at the ship’s entrance and persuade him to let you pass.  
2. Enter the left‑hand room and pick up a **UDL badge** from a shelf.  

---

## Infiltrate the Ship  
1. Climb the guard post ladder and head northeast.  
2. Continue in that direction, then go down the ramp behind the western door.  
3. In the lower area, enter the restricted zone and quickly move to the left‑hand room to find a **UDL badge** on a shelf.  

*Both approaches converge after this point.*

---

## Reach the Bridge Access Card  
1. Head to the western door.  
2. On the other side, enter the left‑hand room and take the **bridge access card** from the desk.  

---

## Redirect Communications to the Impostor  
1. Use the terminal a short distance away to reroute communications to the **Impostor**.  

---

## Access the Bridge  
1. Return the way you came and open the opposite door with the **bridge access card**.  
2. Take the elevator to the upper deck.  

---

## Activate the Impostor’s Flight  
1. Move quickly across the upper deck to avoid unnecessary fights.  
2. Approach the bridge terminal.  
3. Insert **ADA** into the system and authorize the activation of supraluminal flight.  

---

## Return to the Impostor and Travel to Welles’ Lab  
1. Retrace your steps back to the **Impostor**.  
2. Travel to **Welles’ laboratory**.  

---

## Clear Welles’ Laboratory  
1. Enter the main hall and eliminate all corporate guards.  
2. After the fight, go to Welles’ quarters and take the **access card** from the corpse of the porkyste.  
3. Use this card on the nearby terminal to unlock the secret compartment.  

---

## Retrieve the Unique Weapon and Navigation Key  
1. Walk down the corridor and enter Welles’ bedroom.  
2. Take the unique weapon **Phorce of Phinsur** from the desk.  
3. Collect the **navigation key** from the secret compartment.
