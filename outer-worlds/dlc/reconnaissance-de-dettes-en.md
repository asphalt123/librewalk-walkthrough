
## Starting the Quest
1. While on the Precursor, locate **Udom** at the local Council office.
2. Talk to **Udom** and tell him you have information about Wells.
3. Accept his request to help activate the new mission.

## Buying the Council Seal
1. Leave the Council office and go to **Gladys** at the Motel.
2. Purchase the **Council Seal** from **Gladys** for **8,000 Octos**.

## Earn the Required Money
1. Take on various side jobs around the Precursor to accumulate **8,000 Octos**.
2. Once you have the required amount, return to **Gladys** and buy the **Council Seal** if you haven't already.

## Complete the Quest
1. Return to **Udom** with the **Council Seal**.
2. Hand over the seal to finish the quest.
3. Receive the **navigation key of Byzantium**, which unlocks the next main quest.
