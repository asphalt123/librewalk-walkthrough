
## Main Objectives
- **Find Anton’s research**
- **Neutralize the Raptidons without killing them**
- **Obtain the secret‑lab access card**
- **Resolve the situation with Cassandra O’Malley**

## Step‑by‑Step Walkthrough

1. **Start the quest**
   - After listening to Anton Crane’s message during the “Distress Signal” mission, travel to **Roseway** and enter the laboratory.

2. **Activate the quest chain**
   - Enter the communications room and talk to **Crane**. This gives you new objectives and also unlocks the “Alone Against All” side mission.

3. **Reach the secret laboratory**
   - Head south toward the secret lab entrance.
   - Go through the main door, pass the security gates, and talk to **Porter**.  
   - Use **Persuasion** (≈45) to convince Porter to give you the **office access card** (optional but useful).

4. **Clear the first area**
   - Open the left‑hand door and **kill the robot** in the hallway.
   - Go down the stairs a short distance later, **destroy the second machine**.
   - Eliminate the **bandits** posted near the cafeteria.

5. **Search Anton’s office**
   - Go east to **Anton’s office**, open the chest on the right to update your objectives.
   - Exit the office, head north, then descend the side ramp to meet **Cassandra O’Malley**.

6. **Learn about Cassandra’s hold on the research**
   - Dialogue reveals that Cassandra possesses Anton’s research. You must first deal with the **Raptidons** before you can free her.

### Neutralize the Raptidons (without killing)

7. **Gather sedative gas canisters** (you need any 3 of the 4)
   - **Canister 1** – Behind the locked door of the cafeteria storage room.  
   - **Canister 2** – Near the central shelf inside the cafeteria storage room.  
   - **Canister 3** – In the workshop at the far south end of the lab, close to the cafeteria.  
   - **Canister 4** – In front of the entrance to the Raptidon maturation chamber (extra).

8. **Deploy the gas**
   - Go to the **Raptidon maturation room**, switch to **Stealth** mode, and avoid the wandering creatures.
   - Sneak to the ventilation system and install the three **sedative gas canisters**.
   - Exit the room, return to the **ventilation control terminal**, and activate the fans to disperse the gas. All Raptidons will fall asleep.

### Acquire the secret‑lab access card

9. Choose one of the following methods (depending on what you have):

   - **Persuade Porter** (45 Persuasion) – If you already have the office card, talk to Porter again and use his computer to fabricate a **secret‑lab access card**.  
   - **Pick the lock** on Porter’s office door (20 Lockpicking).  
   - **Negotiate a cease‑fire** (45 Persuasion) – Convince Cassandra to let the guards leave, then persuade Porter to abandon the area and hand over the card.  
   - **Kill Porter** – Eliminate Porter and his guards, then loot his body for the office key and craft the secret‑lab card.

10. **Enter the secret laboratory**
    - Return to the area near Cassandra, go through the door opposite the Raptidon maturation chamber, descend the stairs, and open the **secret‑lab door** using the card.

### Resolve Cassandra and obtain Anton’s research

11. Choose one of the following approaches (each leads to a different outcome):

    - **Bribe Cassandra** – Offer a cash bribe (amount varies) for the files.  
    - **Persuade Cassandra** (31 Persuasion) – Threaten to place a bounty on her head until she hands over the documents.  
    - **Let Cassandra go** – Walk away; the quest ends without a reward from Anton.  
    - **Kill Cassandra** – As a last resort, eliminate her and retrieve the research from her corpse.

12. **Return to Anton**
    - Bring the recovered **research** back to **Anton Crane**.
    - He rewards you with **3,750 Octos** and the **Researcher Lab Coat** (unique armor).
    - If you sedated the Raptidons instead of killing them, you also earn a reputation bonus with **Tatie Cleo**.

## Optional Alternate Ending

- **Lie to Anton** about the research, keep the files for yourself, and later sell them to **Gladys** during the “Distress Signal” quest.
