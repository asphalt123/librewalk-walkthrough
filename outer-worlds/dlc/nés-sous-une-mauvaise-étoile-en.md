
## Acquisition
Recruit **Nyoka**.

## Reward
**2,375 Octos** + **Framed Photo** (unlocks the **Team** skill for Nyoka).

## Associated Quest
**Radio Free Monarch**

## Walkthrough

1. After recruiting Nyoka, travel to **Monarch** with her.  
   - During the conversation, accept her proposal to start the mission.

2. Go to **Hayes' grave**.  
   - Eliminate the nearby creatures.  
   - Retrieve the **medallion** from the grave and give it to Nyoka.

3. Continue the **Radio Free Monarch** quest until you meet **Hiram**.  
   - Persuade Hiram to assist Nyoka.

4. Head to the **Edgewater** region and travel northwest to the abandoned outpost.  
   - Defeat the bandits.  
   - Interact with the door controls to unlock the building entrance.

5. Inside the outpost:  
   - Collect the **medallions** from the corpses.  
   - Read the information block on the bed.  
   - Talk to Nyoka to receive the next objectives.

6. Proceed to **Scylla**.  
   - Reach the three quest markers.  
   - Eliminate the rotting primates and gather **pheromone glands**.

7. Return the **pheromone glands** to Nyoka, then travel back to **Monarch**.

8. On the hostile planet, locate Nyoka’s former team hideout.  
   - Unlock the door using the terminal.  
   - Inside, kill the monsters and loot the two corpses for **medallions**.

9. Interact with the **Mantisaur pantry** and deposit the **pheromone glands**.  
   - Defeat all creatures emerging from the tunnel.

10. After the combat, speak with Nyoka to complete the quest.  
    - Receive the **framed photo**, which unlocks the **Team** skill for Nyoka.  
    - Optionally convince Nyoka to keep the medallions; they will appear in her room on the Imposter.
