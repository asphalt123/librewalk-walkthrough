
## Quest: “Distress Signal”

1. After receiving the quest from **Wells**, go to your cabin and pick up the **Holographic Veil** from your desk. This will let you disguise yourself in restricted areas.  
2. Use your ship’s navigation system to travel to **The Precursor**.  
3. When you arrive, go to the control station and talk to **Leonard** to gain access to the rest of the ship.  
4. Head to the motel.  
5. Enter the left‑most back room to meet **Gladys**.  
   - To obtain the navigation key for **Stellar Bay**, you have two options:  
     - **Purchase** the key for **10,000 Octo**.  
     - **Complete** the side quest **“Distress Signal”** if you don’t have enough credits.  
6. Leave the motel and proceed to the local council headquarters. Speak with **Udom**; he will lift the ordinance restricting your ship, allowing you to travel again.  
7. Return to **The Precursor** and, when you can, retrieve the navigation key for **Stellar Bay** from **Gladys**.  
8. Go back to **The Impostor** and talk to **Wells** to finish the main quest.
