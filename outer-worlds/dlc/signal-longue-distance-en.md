
## Main Quest
1. Activate the mission.  
2. Travel to **Phineas' laboratory**.  
3. Enter the main room.  
4. Use the **terminal** on the right side to **place the bug**.  
5. Return to **Sophia**.  
6. Speak with **Perceval** to finish the quest and claim your reward.  
7. **Perceval** will now sell you various items, including **two unique weapons**.  

## Alternative Path (Continue Working with Welles)
1. Talk to **Welles** in his laboratory.  
2. Use the **terminal** there to **install a corrupted tracking signal**.  
3. Continue with the main quest steps from the appropriate point.
