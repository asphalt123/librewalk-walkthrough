
## Acquisition
- Talk to **Célia** in **Stellar Bay** to receive the quest.

## Reward
- **375 Octos**

## Steps
1. Speak with **Célia** about her money problems to start the mission.  
2. Exit onto the street and locate **Sebastian** nearby.  
3. In conversation with **Sebastian**, choose any dialogue option that convinces him to spend time with **Célia**.  
4. Return to **Célia** and collect your reward.
