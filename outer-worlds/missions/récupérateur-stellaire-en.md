
## Rewards
- **XP**
- Access to the quest **“Syndicate of the Cosmocrime”**

## Associated Quest
- **“Passport to Adventure”**

## Steps
1. On the **Precursor**, talk to **Lee Flores** or **Gladys** to learn about **SubLight** and activate the mission.  
2. Go to the **SubLight** headquarters and take the elevator to the upper level.  
3. Speak with **Tobias**.  
4. Talk to **Lilya** and ask for work.  
5. Obtain the **Stellar Bay navigation key** from **Gladys**; she will then agree to hire you.  
6. After completing the above, you will unlock the faction mission **“Syndicate of the Cosmocrime.”**
