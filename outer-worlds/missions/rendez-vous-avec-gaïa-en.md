
## Acquisition
- **Minister Clarke** (no reward)

## Associated Quest
- **"The City and the Stars"**

## Steps
1. During the quest **"The City and the Stars"**, locate and speak with **Minister Clarke**.  
2. In the dialogue, the minister will automatically assign you the new mission.  
3. Follow the objectives of **"The City and the Stars"** to reach the Council headquarters on Halcyon.  
4. Inside the headquarters, enter the **former office of the minister**.  
5. Use the **computer** in the office to **send a message to Earth**.
