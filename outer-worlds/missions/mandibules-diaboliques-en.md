
## Quest Overview
- **Acquisition**: Variable  
- **Reward**: **Thunder** (unique weapon) + access to **E3 weapon cache**  
- **Associated Quest**: “Radio Free Monarch”

## Main Path
1. Travel to the **E3 barracks** near the summit of **Devil’s Peak**.  
2. Talk to the mercenaries.  
   - Use **bribery** or **persuasion** to avoid a fight.  
   - Do **not** mention coming from the caves, or they will attack.  
3. Offer your services to locate their leader **Berthold**.  
4. Follow the trail marker to the entrance of the caves.  
5. Inside the caves, go **left** and defeat the **giant regal mantis**.  
6. After the battle, speak with **Berthold**, then exit the caves.  
7. Return to the **E3 barracks** and speak with **Berthold** again.  
   - Receive the **E3 cache access card**.  
   - Persuade **Berthold** to accompany you during the assault on the relay station (part of the “Radio Free Monarch” mission).  
8. The mission is now complete. Enter the nearby building.  
9. Retrieve the unique weapon **Thunder** from the furniture near the door.  
10. Use the **E3 cache access card** to unlock the supply crates on the right side of the room.  

## Alternative Outcomes
- **Cave‑first approach**: If you reach Devil’s Peak through the caves, you can start the quest by killing the **giant regal mantis** and then talking directly to **Berthold**.  
- **Skill‑based persuasion**: With appropriate dialogue skills, you can convince the mercenaries to assist you without dealing with **Berthold** at all.  
- **Killing mercenaries**: If you kill the mercenaries, you will lose the ability to interact with **Berthold**, even after rescuing him in the caves.
