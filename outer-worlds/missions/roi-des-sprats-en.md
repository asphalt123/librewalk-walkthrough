
## Quest Overview
- **Acquisition**: **Nelson Mayson**  
- **Reward**: Variable (see alternate outcomes)

## Steps

1. **Talk to Nelson** at the Fallbrook outpost on Monarch.  
   - Accept his proposal to start the mission.  
   - *(Optional)* Attack him briefly to gain a reputation bonus with SubLight.

2. **Go to the eastern side of the area** and unlock the storage room door.  
   - Inside, **eliminate all Raptidons**.  
   - Search the dead **Sprats** and collect the **drug**.

3. **Return to Nelson** and report the findings to receive the next objectives.

4. **Leave the outpost**, follow the quest marker, and **enter the building on your left** to meet **Bertrand**.

5. **Dialogue with Bertrand**:  
   - Use **Persuasion** or **Medicine** to convince him you aren’t harming the Sprats.  
   - If the check fails, you must kill him.  
   - In either case, **collect the drug from the Sprats' excrement**.

6. **Return to Nelson** to complete the quest and receive your reward.

## Alternate Outcomes

- During the initial conversation with **Nelson**, use **Charm** to negotiate a higher payout (**1125 Octos** instead of **375 Octos**).  
- After obtaining the drugs, you can lie to **Nelson** and keep them for yourself, which will result in a reputation penalty with SubLight.
