
## Acquisition
- Talk to **Branson** at the Iconoclast Camp in Amber Heights on Monarch.  
- Offer your services to receive the list of tasks.

## Reward
- **375 Octos**

## Quest Steps

### Option 1 – Fix problems remotely *(requires 55 Engineering, 55 Science, 55 Hacking)*
1. Go to the small building north of the camp.  
2. Activate the **terminal**.  
3. Use the terminal to reactivate all defective Iconoclast systems.

### Option 2 – Fix problems manually  

#### 1. Bar area
1. Go to the back of the bar.  
2. Navigate around the exposed cable to reach the **lever**.  
3. Pull the **lever** down.

#### 2. Clinic area
1. Head to the clinic.  
2. Jump onto the rocks opposite the clinic.  
3. Leap over the steam jets.  
4. Pull the **lever** down.

#### 3. Bayside Terrace ruins
1. Leave the camp and travel north to the Bayside Terrace ruins.  
2. Pull the **lever** down to reactivate communications.
