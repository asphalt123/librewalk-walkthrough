
## Overview
- **Acquisition:** Silas  
- **Reward:** 300 Octos  
- **Associated quests:** “And the Electricity Was”, “A Long Tomorrow”, “He Beat the Robots”

## Steps
1. At the entrance of Edgewater, talk to **Silas** and accept the mission.  
2. Go to the cannery and talk to **Phyllis Granger**.  
   - Ask her for her royalty.  
   - Ask about **Eugène** to obtain information for the quest “And the Electricity Was”.  
3. Go to the barber shop at the entrance of Edgewater and talk to **Conrad**.  
   - He cannot pay right now; end the conversation.  
4. Examine the note near the corpse in the morgue to learn about **Eugène’s gold teeth**.  
5. Return to **Conrad**.  
   - Persuade him to pledge the gold teeth as payment to receive his royalty.  
   - Ask him about **Eugène’s death** for additional clues.  
6. Travel to the next marker and collect **Abernathy’s royalty**. This also activates the quest “A Long Tomorrow”.  
7. Leave the town, head to the landing strip, and talk to **Miller**.  
   - Collect his royalty and receive the quest “He Beat the Robots”.  
8. Return to **Silas**, report the completed royalties, and finish the quest.  

## Eugène and the Gold Teeth
- Gather information about **Eugène** from **Phyllis** and **Conrad** throughout the quest.  
- After finding the morgue note and discussing **Eugène’s** suicide with the barber, return to **Phyllis** for further questioning.  
- Speak to **Silas** about **Eugène**; you’ll discover that his corpse has vanished.  
- Go to the Botanical Laboratory, explore the back of the greenhouse, and retrieve the **gold teeth** from the furniture.  
- Use the gold teeth and the collected information to uncover the secret of Adelaide’s fertilizer, which helps resolve community problems at the end of “And the Electricity Was”.  
- **Selling the gold teeth:**  
  - **Silas** will pay 300 Octos.  
  - **Phyllis Granger** will pay 500 Octos.
