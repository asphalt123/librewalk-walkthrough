
## Acquisition
- Talk to **Sebastian Adams** in A Stellar Bay.

## Reward
- **375 Octos**

## Steps
1. Speak with **Sebastian Adams** in A Stellar Bay and inquire about **Franklin Pickett** to start the quest.  
2. Leave the town after the conversation.  
3. Follow the mission marker to the area south of **Amber Heights**.  
4. Defeat the **Royal Mantis**.  
5. Search the mantis corpse and collect **Pickett's Journal**.  
6. Return to **Sebastian Adams** to complete the quest.
