
## Acquisition  
Alonzo Vallejos  

## Reward  
**Certified Explorer Hat**

## Steps  

1. In Byzance, go to the Exploration Office and talk to Alonzo to receive the side mission.  
2. Leave the building, follow the quest marker, and interact with the first beacon.  
3. Travel to Edgewater and locate the second beacon marked by the next quest marker.  
4. Head to Scylla and approach the objective area. The beacon is outside the protection shield, so act quickly to avoid taking damage.  
5. Proceed to Stellar Bay and find the final beacon in the southwest part of the town.  
6. Return to Alonzo to complete the quest and receive the **Certified Explorer Hat**.
