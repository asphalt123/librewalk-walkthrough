
## Quest Start  
1. Go to **Jolicoeur Tailor Shop** in Byzance.  
2. Speak to **Celeste** and accept the mission.  

## Gather Armor Sets  
1. **Iconoclast Armor** + **Astronaut Armor** – Visit the **Quartermaster of the Iconoclasts** in Amber Heights and purchase the required armor pieces.  
2. **Marauder Armor** – Eliminate Marauders throughout Halcyon and loot their corpses for the needed components.  

## Equip and Report  
1. Once you have all three armor sets, return to **Celeste**.  
2. Equip each set one by one, then talk to Celeste again to validate the objectives.  

## Collect Crafting Materials  
1. **Primate Hide** – Travel to **Scylla** and harvest the hide from the local primates.  
2. **Raptidon Flank Hide** – Head to **Monarque** and collect the hide from Raptidon creatures.  
3. **Regalian Moth Chitin** – Go to the southwest of **Stellar Bay** and kill the giant Regalian moth for its chitin.  

## Return for New Objectives  
1. Bring the collected materials back to **Celeste**.  
2. After the dialogue, go to **Halcyon Parcel Service** and speak with **Olive**, then **Wanda**.  

## Retrieve the Parcel  
1. Leave the area and follow the new quest marker.  
2. Pick up the message hanging on the door.  
3. Return to **Halcyon Parcel Service**, talk to **Wanda** again, then **Olive**.  
4. Show the **Power of Attorney** given by Celeste to receive the parcel.  

## Confront Celeste’s Fate  
1. Travel to **Celeste’s store**.  
2. Talk briefly with the guards.  
3. Loot the **office key** from Celeste’s corpse.  

## Final Reward  
1. Open the left-hand door using the key.  
2. Retrieve the unique **Chimera Armor** to complete the mission.
