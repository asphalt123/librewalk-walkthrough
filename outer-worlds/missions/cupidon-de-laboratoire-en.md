
## Quest Details
- **Acquired from:** **Gilles Molina**  
- **Reward:** **1125 Octos**  
- **Associated quest:** *The City and the Stars*

## Steps
1. Go to the entrance of the Byzance maintenance tunnels.  
2. Talk to **Gilles Molina** to activate the mission.  
3. After the dialogue, take the side elevator to reach the tunnel area.  
4. Follow the quest markers and capture the **Sprats** at each location.  
5. When all **Sprats** have been captured, return to **Gilles Molina** to collect your reward.
