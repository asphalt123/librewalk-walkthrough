
## Acquisition
**Grimm**

## Reward
**625 Octos**

## Associated Quest
**“The Hidden People”**

## Steps

1. **Talk to Grimm** during your first visit to Stellar Bay and accept his request to start the mission.  
2. Go to **Célia** and ask her about the poster.  
3. Proceed to the local **cannery** and speak with **Velma** to learn more about the situation. You now have several options to obtain the poster:

   - **Bribe Velma** – Pay **612 Octos** for the poster immediately.  
   - **Intimidate Velma** – Use **55 Intimidation** to convince her the poster is in her best interest.  
   - **Persuade Velma** – Complete the quest **“The Hidden People,”** then return to Velma and use **20 Persuasion** to get the poster.  
   - **Steal the poster** – Go to Velma’s office on the upper floor of the cannery and pick the lock (**50 Lockpicking**) on the chest to take the poster.  

4. **Alternative route via Nell**  
   - Ask Velma about **Nell** to receive new objectives.  
   - Travel to **Left Field** and talk to the young woman there.  

5. Choose one of the two new options:

   - **Lie to Nell** about the poster (**55 Deception**).  
   - **Help Nell retrieve her cargo** of Tossball jerseys.  

6. If you decide to help Nell:

   - Leave the town and head to the marker in the south.  
   - Eliminate the bandits there.  
   - Search the container to recover the **Tossball jerseys**.  

7. Return to **Nell**, give her the recovered cargo to complete her objective.  
8. Finally, go back to **Velma** and obtain the **Tossball poster**.
