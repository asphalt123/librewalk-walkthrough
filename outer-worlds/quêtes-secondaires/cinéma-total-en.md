
## Acquisition
- **Maverick Johnson** (side mission)

## Steps
1. **Talk to Maverick Johnson** in front of the cargo hangar and accept his proposal to start the mission.  
2. Head **north‑west** and enter the **Odéon** building.  
3. Inside, take the **elevator** to the filming set.  
4. **Play through the entire scene**. Your dialogue choices and actions during the scene determine the final reward.

## Rewards
- **Poor performance** (bad dialogue choices): receive **625 Octos**.  
- **Skill‑focused dialogue** (use most skill‑based lines): receive **1,125 Octos**.  
- **Kill everything** (eliminate all characters): receive **1,125 Octos** plus a **Council reputation bonus**.
