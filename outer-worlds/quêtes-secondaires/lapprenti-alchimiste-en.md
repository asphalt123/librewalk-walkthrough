
## Acquisition
**Vaughn Cortes** (quest giver)

## Reward
**1875 Octos**

## Associated Quests
- “Distress Signal”
- “Roseway at Full Teeth”

## Steps
1. Go to the Roseway laboratory. Enter the housing complex and talk to **Vaughn** to receive the side quest.  
2. Travel south to **Tatie Cleo’s** secret laboratory.  
3. Enter Vaughn’s laboratory in the southern part of the complex and interact with the terminal to access the Raptidon gland preparation system.  
4. Choose one of the following options:  

   - **Recalibrate the experiment** (cost: 50 Science).  
     *If you have the required skills, modify the data to produce **2** samples of **Raptidon Gland**.*  
     Later you can use **45 Lie** to deceive **Vaughn** and keep one sample to sell to **Gladys** for a good profit.  

   - **Complete the experiment**.  
     *Obtain **1** sample of **Raptidon Gland**.*  
     You may either give it to **Vaughn** or sell it to **Gladys**.  

   - **Purge the sample**.  
     *You receive no gland sample.*  
     The quest can still be finished, but you gain no reward and your reputation with **Tatie Cleo** drops.  

5. **Alternative outcome** – If you obtained **2** samples, you can hand both to **Vaughn**. This grants a small experience bonus and nothing more.
