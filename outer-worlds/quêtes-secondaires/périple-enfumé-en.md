
## Quest Start  
1. At the entrance of Roseway’s secret laboratory, talk to **Lillian** and accept her request to start the mission.  
2. Follow the path to the **East** and head toward the objective area.  

## Retrieve the Cigarette Case  
1. When you reach the Raptidon enclosure, climb the hill on the right side of the area.  
2. Carefully descend to the walkway that overlooks the Raptidons.  
3. Use the **terminal** on the walkway to activate the electric barrier. This will neutralize the Raptidons, making them easy to defeat.  
4. Defeat the Raptidons.  
5. After the fight, go down into the enclosure and pick up **Lillian’s cigarette case** near the crates.  

## Return to Lillian  
1. Return to **Lillian** with the case.  

## Dialogue Choices (after returning the case)  

- **Keep the case** (requires 30 Lie)  
  - No immediate reward; you retain the **cigarette case**, which can be sold later for a good amount.  

- **Ask Lillian to contact her boss** (requires 30 Persuasion)  
  - No reward; this choice does not affect any other quests at this time.  

- **Return the case to Lillian**  
  - Lillian thanks you and gives you **625 Octos** and the unique weapon **Anarchist Recipe Book**.
