
## Acquisition
Talk to **Velma** at the canning plant in Stellar Bay to receive the quest.

## Reward
**625 Octos**

## Associated Quest
**“The Hidden People”**

## Options  

### Lie to Caleb (55 Lie)  
1. Go to the **Yacht Club** and talk to **Caleb** on the upper floor.  
2. During the conversation, **lie** to him about Velma so he agrees to resume work immediately.  

### Steal Caleb’s Savings (50 Lockpicking)  
1. If you learned about **Caleb’s Octos reserve** from the initial dialogue with Velma, head to the **restaurant** and go upstairs.  
2. Jump over the balcony onto the left building.  
3. **Lockpick** the safe on that building to retrieve the Octos reserve.  
4. Return to **Caleb** and tell him all his savings have disappeared, prompting him to resume work.  

### Convince Velma to Increase Employee Wages (55 Persuasion)  
1. Talk to **Velma**.  
2. If you have already completed the quest **“The Hidden People,”** **persuade** her that ending the strike is in her best interest.  

### Blackmail Velma (35 Lie or 55 Hacking)  
1. Talk to **Caleb** and ask about rumors of corruption involving **Velma**.  
2. Return to **Velma**.  
   - If you have **35 Lie**, **lie** and make her believe you have proof of embezzlement.  
   - Otherwise, go to her **office** on the upper floor and **hack** her computer (55 Hacking) to gather incriminating data.  
3. Return to **Velma** and explain the situation, convincing her to raise wages.
