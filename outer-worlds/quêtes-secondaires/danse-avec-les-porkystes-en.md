
## Acquisition
- **Clive** (from the quest “Clive the Butcher”)

## Reward
- **11250 Octos**
- **Clive’s Machete** (Fendoir de Clive)

## Steps
1. Meet **Clive** during the “Clive the Butcher” quest and agree to partner with him. This unlocks the “Assassinate Catherine” mission.  
2. Travel to **Fallbrook**.  
3. Locate **Catherine** in Fallbrook and kill her.  
4. A city‑wide fight with SubLight employees will trigger automatically; defeat all enemies.  
5. Return to **Clive** after the battle to finish the mission and claim your reward.  

*Note: Completing this mission will significantly lower your reputation with the company.*
