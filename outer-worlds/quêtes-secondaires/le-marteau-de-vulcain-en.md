
## Starting the Quest
1. Go to the **Roseway Laboratory** and talk to **Orson Shaw**. Accept his request to retrieve the weapon blueprint.
2. Leave the laboratory and head east to the **Storage Building**.

## Clearing the Warehouse
1. Enter the door on the right side of the building.
2. Eliminate all bandits in the warehouse.
3. Proceed to the southeast room and examine the note near the armory door.

## Obtaining the Armory Access Code
You must acquire the code to the armory in the old **Tatie‑Biotique Laboratory**. Choose one of the following methods:

- **Hack the reception computer** (requires 20 Hacking). The terminal will display the armory code directly.  
- **If you lack sufficient Hacking**, go to the laboratory’s upper floor:
  1. Access the computer in the north office.
  2. Check the two computers in the open‑space area.
  3. Use the messages on these machines to piece together the armory code.

## Unlocking the Armory and Collecting Items
1. Return to the **Storage Building** and unlock the armory door with the obtained code.
2. Inside the armory:
   - Retrieve the **Acidic Emitter** from the shelf (required for the “Cleaning Unit” quest).
   - Go down the stairs a short distance.
3. On the lower level:
   - Defeat the robot guarding the area.
   - Pick up the **Force Pistol Schematics**.

## Completing the Quest
1. Return to **Orson Shaw** and hand over the documents (the schematics and any other required items).
2. Quest completion rewards: **1875 Octos**, the experimental pistol **Ultimatum**, and later the unique weapon **Ulti‑Nature**.

## Later Rewards
1. After finishing other quests, speak with **ADA** on the **Impostor** to receive a message from Orson Shaw.
2. Visit Orson at the **Roseway Laboratory** to obtain the unique weapon **Ulti‑Nature**.

## Alternative Path (Optional)
- You may keep the **Force Pistol Schematics** and sell them later to **Gladys** during the “Distress Signal” mission for a large sum of money.  
- Choosing this option will cause the quest to fail, preventing you from receiving the **Ulti‑Nature** weapon.
