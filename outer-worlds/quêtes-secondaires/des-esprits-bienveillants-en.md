
## Steps

1. **Talk to Grace Romero** at the entrance of the Botanical Lab and accept the mission.
2. **Enter Zoé’s tent** in the camp:
   - Examine the notes on the left desk, the one on the floor, and the last note on the nearby table.
3. **Leave the tent** and speak with **Stefan Garcia**.  
   - Use **Persuasion** or **Lie** to convince him to give you the **Masked Vendor cassette**.
4. **Travel east** to the small marauder camp:
   - Clear the enemies (this also advances the “A Handful of Fingerprints” quest).
   - Talk to **Zoé**.
5. **During the conversation with Zoé**:
   - Give her the **Masked Vendor cassette**, **or** tell her that Stefan wants to watch the new episode together if you don’t have the cassette.
6. **Return to Grace Romero** to complete the mission and receive your reward: **Perforating Pistol**.

## Alternative Outcomes

- **If you have already met Zoé** during the “A Handful of Fingerprints” quest, you can skip searching her tent.
- **If Zoé is already dead**, retrieve the **ring** from her corpse and give it to Grace Romero.
