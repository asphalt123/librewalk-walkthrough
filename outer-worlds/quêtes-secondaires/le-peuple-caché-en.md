
## Quest Details  
- **Acquisition**: **Velma**  
- **Reward**: **625 Octos** + **Table Knife**  
- **Associated Quest**: “The Misfortunes of Grimm”  

## Steps  

1. Go to the Stellar Bay Canning Factory.  
   - Talk to **Velma** about **Braxton** to activate the mission.  

2. Head to the apartment complex, go upstairs, and talk to **Laura** for additional information.  

3. Leave the complex and travel west to the ruins.  
   - Enter the **Mather House**.  

4. Inside, speak with the family head **Edgar**. You now have two possible approaches:  

   ### Option A – Attack the Mather family  
   1. During the initial dialogue with **Edgar**, ask him to open the door and then choose to attack.  
   2. Kill all family members.  
   3. Loot **Edgar’s corpse** for the **Mather house key**.  
   4. Go to the top floor, unlock the bedroom door with the key, and rescue **Braxton**.  

   ### Option B – Sneak and pick the lock (35 Lockpicking)  
   1. After the discussion with **Edgar**, go to the second floor.  
   2. Pick the lock on the left door of the bedroom (requires 35 lockpicking).  
   3. Search **Braxton’s corpse** and collect his **badge** and the unique weapon **Table Knife**.  
   4. Choose one of the following:  
      - Escape through the first‑floor door, **or**  
      - Return to the ground floor to “deal” with the cannibals.  

5. Return to **Velma** and report the outcome to receive your reward.
