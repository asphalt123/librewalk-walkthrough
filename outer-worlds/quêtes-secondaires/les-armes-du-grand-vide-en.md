
## Activate the Mission

1. On the Impostor, go to your quarters.
2. Use the terminal to read Hawthorne’s reports and unlock the side mission.

## **Shrinking Ray**

1. Travel to Phinéas’s laboratory.
2. Proceed to the main lab area.
3. Pick up the **Shrinking Ray** from the tablet near the window.

## **Prismatic Hammer**

1. Go to the Precursor and head to the dormitories north of the main docks.
2. Jump onto the crates at the entrance to reach the bunks, then move toward the opening opposite them.
3. Eliminate all bandits on the other side of the opening.
4. Search the bandit leader’s corpse for the access card to the repair hangar.
5. Use the card to open the nearby door and loot the chest for the **Prismatic Hammer**.

## **Mandibular Restructurer**

1. On the Precursor, speak with Gladys and purchase the Hephaestus Mining Company archives from the “Quest Items” category.
2. Return to your ship and read the document at your cabin terminal to update your objectives.
3. Travel to the Scylla mining station.
4. Head east to the abandoned outpost and search the indicated building.
5. Find the **Mandibular Restructurer** inside the chest.

## **Gooey Weapon**

1. Travel to Monarch and go to the Fallbrook community.
2. Speak with Duncan and buy the UDL info block from the “Quest Item” category.
3. Go to the UDL Lab on Monarch (part of the “Hidden Errors” quest).
4. Enter the lab, go upstairs, and take the access card from the central table.
5. Return to the ground floor, insert the card into the terminal, and either hack the system (55 hacking) or answer the corporation‑president‑aligned questions.
6. Retrieve the **Gooey Weapon** from its compartment to complete the objective.

## **Mental Control Ray**

1. In Fallbrook, buy the SubLight info block from Duncan to update your objectives.
2. Travel to Cascadia and locate Rizzo’s secret laboratory (part of the “Cosmocrime Syndicate” quest).
3. Traverse the area to the final room of the complex and transfer the Alta‑Vitae to the Impostor.
4. From that position, jump to the platform east of you and climb the ladder.
5. Jump to the next ladder, then search the chest for the **Mental Control Ray**.
