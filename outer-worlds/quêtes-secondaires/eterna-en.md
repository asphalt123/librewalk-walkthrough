
## Acquisition  
- **Hortense**

## Reward  
- Variable

## Steps  

1. Go to the street north of Byzance and talk to **Hortense** to start the side quest.  
2. Proceed to the cargo landing area.  
3. Follow the signs marked **Retirees** to the southwest and use the terminal in the back room to unlock the door.  
4. Pass through the large door to reach the **Retiree Reception Center**.  
5. Walk down the corridor and open the next door using the terminal.  
6. Take the elevator at the far end to the lower level.  
7. In the lower corridor, destroy all robots that attack you.  
8. After the combat, use the terminal at the far end of the room to unlock the hatch, then open the hatch to escape.  
9. Return to **Hortense** and report.

## Outcomes  

- **Tell the truth** to **Hortense** – receive **1125 Octos** and a reputation penalty with the Council.  
- **Lie** to **Hortense** (40 lies) – send **Hortense** to her death and receive **1125 Octos** plus a reputation bonus with the Council.  
- **Alternative** – during the final dialogue, use persuasion or intimidation to gain an additional **2188 Octos**.
