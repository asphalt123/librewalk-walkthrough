
## Acquisition  
- Talk to **Catherine Malin** to receive the side mission. Exhaust all conversation topics to gather extra information.

## Enter the Porglier Factory  

### Option 1 – Main Entrance  
1. Go to **Duncan** and persuade him to give you a **Porglier Factory badge**.  
2. If persuasion fails, steal a badge from his locker.  
3. Approach the guards at the main entrance and use a high‑skill dialogue option (Persuasion, Intimidation, or Lie – 20 points) to convince them to let you in.  
4. Open the door, run across the courtyard, and enter the factory.  
5. Inside, take the stairs to the offices, open the office door, then go down the steps to the **porkystes breeding area**.  
6. Whenever your holographic veil is detected, talk to the guards or security bots again using the same skill to avoid triggering an alarm.

### Option 2 – Sewers (Lockpicking 35)  
1. Locate the sewer access behind the eastern waterfall.  
2. Pick the door (Lockpicking 35).  
3. Follow the walkway, climb the ladder to the upper level.  
4. Move left, pass the terminal on the right, and continue without being spotted.  
5. Retrieve a **Porglier Factory badge** from the back locker to move more freely.

## Meet Clive  
1. From the breeding area, go through the south‑west door and climb the stairs on the opposite side.  
2. At the top, use the terminal to call the elevator to **Clive’s office**.  
3. Return to the ground floor, take the left door, cross the canning area, and reach the north‑west elevator.  
4. Ride up, talk to **Clive** briefly, and tell him you need time to think before moving around the factory.

## Possible Approaches  

### Negotiate a Truce (55 Persuasion **or** 55 Medicine)  
1. During the conversation with Clive, explain why you’re there and propose a deal with **Catherine**.  
2. If you have **Medicine 55**, use it to convince Clive of the proposal’s merit.  
3. If you lack Medicine, go to the porkystes breeding room, read the information block on the shelf, then use **Persuasion 55** to convince the butcher (Clive) to cooperate with Catherine.  
4. Return to **Catherine** and persuade (or lie) her to follow the plan (requires high Persuasion or Lie – 55).

### Sabotage the Factory  

- **Alter the porkystes’ diet** (Medicine 55)  
  1. Go to the porkystes feeding stations.  
  2. Use the terminals in the center of the room to change the vitamin dosage in the animals’ food.  

- **Disrupt the canning system** (Engineering 55)  
  1. Reach the canning line and climb the ladder in the south‑west corner to access the terminal.  
  2. Use Engineering 55 to increase production cost and halt the line.  

- **Tamper with financial reports** (Remarkable Intelligence)  
  1. Enter the office at the factory entrance.  
  2. Use the terminal at the back of the room to alter the factory’s financial data.  

### Assassinate Clive  
1. Follow the usual route to **Clive’s office** and kill him.  
2. Exit through the left corridor of his office and take the elevator to the outside of the factory.  
3. Use the terminal outside to unlock the back door, then leave the area peacefully.  

## Additional Tips  
- Before dealing with Clive, speak with **Catherine** about the butcher’s case to earn **500 Octos**.  
- In the left corridor of Clive’s office, collect the three cans of food and consume them from your inventory. Afterwards, talk to the butcher about the tasting; he will reward you with **625 Octos**.
