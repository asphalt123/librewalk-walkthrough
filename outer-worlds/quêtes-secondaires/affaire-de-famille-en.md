
## Acquisition  
- **Quest Giver**: **Agnès Needham** (Stellar Bay)  
- **Reward**: **625 Octos**  

## Starting the Quest  
1. In **Stellar Bay**, go to the southeast exit.  
2. Talk to **Agnès** and accept the request to find her son. This activates the quest.  
3. Leave the town and travel south to the **Iconoclast camp**.  

## Meeting Tucker  
1. At the camp, locate the small building on the north side and enter.  
2. Inside you will meet **Tucker**. From here you have several possible approaches.  

## Options to Complete the Quest  

### 1. Kill Tucker  
- If you cannot reach a peaceful solution, kill **Tucker**.  
- Loot his corpse for **the ring**.  
- Return to **Agnès** to finish the quest (you will take a large reputation penalty with the Iconoclasts).  

### 2. Convince Tucker (Persuasion 55)  
- Use at least **55 Persuasion** to convince **Tucker** to return to his mother.  
- Travel back to **Stellar Bay** with him.  
- Speak to **Agnès** to complete the quest and collect the reward.  

### 3. Threaten Tucker’s Mother (Intimidation 35)  
- During the initial conversation with **Tucker**, use **Intimidation 35** to threaten his mother’s safety.  
- Force **Tucker** to go back to **Stellar Bay**.  
- Return to **Agnès** for the reward. You may then decide whether to let the threat linger or allow **Tucker** to leave.  

### 4. Simulate Tucker’s Death  
- Accept **Tucker’s** offer to retrieve his valuable **ring** (pretend he is dead).  
- Go back to **Agnès** and lie about **Tucker’s** fate to finish the quest and receive the reward.  
- Alternatively, you can reveal the truth to **Agnès**, which fails the entire plan.
