
## Acquisition  
1. Talk to **Nyoka** at the local bar in Stellar Bay.  
2. Ask about the informant and accept the task to retrieve her dose of stimulants.  
3. Proceed to the dispensary and speak with **Abigaïl**.  

## Options to Obtain the Medications  

### 1. Bribe **Abigaïl** (55 Persuasion + 2000 Octos)  
- During the conversation, do not reveal who you are retrieving the meds for.  
- Offer a bribe and receive the medication.  

### 2. Intimidate **Abigaïl** (55 Intimidation)  
- Discuss Nyoka’s case with **Abigaïl**.  
- Intimidate her to force an immediate hand‑over of a dose (she will react badly).  

### 3. Steal the Dispensary Key from **Abigaïl** (40 Stealth)  
- Position yourself behind **Abigaïl** and take the key to the dispensary storage.  
- Go upstairs and retrieve the medication from the storage.  

### 4. Pick the Storage Lock (30 Lockpicking)  
- Go directly to the upstairs storage.  
- Pick the lock and take the medication.  

### 5. Hack Nyoka’s Prescription (55 Hacking)  
- Ascend to the dispensary’s upper floor.  
- Hack the terminal at the end of the hallway.  
- Access Nyoka’s file and modify her daily dose data.  
- Return to **Abigaïl**; she will give you the medication.  

### 6. Retrieve the Dispensary Key from **Doctor Williams**  
- Ask **Abigaïl** about **Doctor Williams** to update your objectives.  
- Leave Stellar Bay and travel to the southwest cemetery.  
- Find **Doctor Williams’** corpse and take the dispensary key.  
- Return to the clinic and collect the medication from the storage.  

## Completion  
1. Return to **Nyoka** and give her the medication.  
2. Accept her offer to join your crew.  

## Reward  
- **1125 Octos**  
- **Nyoka** joins your crew  
- Associated quest: **“Free Radio Monarch”**
