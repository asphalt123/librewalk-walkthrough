
## Steps
1. **Talk to Leonard Wheeler** – Find the soldier at the control post on the Precursor and accept the mission.  
2. **Speak with Wanda** – Go to the infirmary and talk to Wanda to learn about the missing cargo.  
3. **Set course for Scylla** – Return to the Impostor and plot a jump to Scylla.  
4. **Reach the ground level** – Use the elevator on Scylla to get to the surface and head south.  
5. **Clear the bandits** – Eliminate all bandits at the far end of the asteroid.  
6. **Talk to Captain Irion** – Approach the captain after the fight.  
7. **Obtain Irion’s Silex Rifle** – Explain why you are there; the captain will give you **Irion’s Silex Rifle**.  
8. **Report back to Wanda** – Return to the Precursor, find Wanda, and give her your report to finish the quest.  

## Reward
- **1500 Octos**
- Various consumables
