
## Overview  
**Acquisition:** Anton Crane  
**Reward:** Variable  

## Main Steps  

1. **Listen to Anton Crane’s message** from the “Distress Signal” mission.  
2. Travel to **Roseway** and enter the laboratory.  
3. In the communications room, talk to **Crane** to receive new objectives and optionally activate the “Alone Against All” quest.  
4. Head south to the secret lab entrance and go through the main door.  
5. Pass the gates and speak with **Porter** to learn more about the situation. Persuade her (45 Persuasion) to give you her office access card – useful but not required.  
6. Through the left door, eliminate the robot in the hallway.  
7. Go down the stairs a short distance later and destroy another machine.  
8. Deal with the bandits near the cafeteria.  
9. Proceed east to **Anton’s office**, open the chest on the right, and update your objectives.  
10. Exit the office, go north, then descend the side ramp to meet **Cassandra O’Malley**.  

## Free Cassandra and Deal with the Raptidons  

1. Learn from Cassandra that she holds Anton’s research. You must free her to retrieve the files.  
2. **Neutralize the Raptidons without killing them** by using sedative gas canisters:  

   - **Canister 1** – behind the locked door of the cafeteria storage room.  
   - **Canister 2** – near the central shelf in the cafeteria storage room.  
   - **Canister 3** – in the workshop at the southern edge of the lab, near the cafeteria.  
   - *(Canister 4 is located at the entrance to the Raptidon maturation chamber but is not needed.)*  

3. Collect any three of the above canisters.  
4. Enter the Raptidon maturation chamber, switch to **Stealth** mode, avoid the roaming Raptidons, and quietly place the canisters in the ventilation system.  
5. Return to the ventilation control terminal and activate the fans to disperse the sedative gas, putting all Raptidons to sleep.  

## Obtain a Secret Lab Access Card  

Choose one of the following methods (depending on your earlier choices and skill levels):  

- **Persuade Porter** (45 Persuasion) – if you already have her office card, go to her computer and fabricate a new secret‑lab access card.  
- **Lockpick Porter’s office door** (20 Lockpicking) – if you lack the card, pick the lock to gain entry.  
- **Negotiate a cease‑fire** (45 Persuasion) – during the conversation with Cassandra, persuade her to let the guards leave, then persuade Porter to abandon his post and hand over the access card.  
- **Kill Porter** – eliminate Porter and his guards, then loot his corpse for the office key.  

## Free Cassandra and Retrieve Anton’s Research  

1. With the secret‑lab access card, return to Cassandra’s location.  
2. Open the door opposite the Raptidon maturation chamber, go down the stairs, and open the secret lab door.  
3. Inside the secret lab, obtain Anton’s research by one of the following approaches:  

   - **Bribe Cassandra** – offer a sum of Octo (amount varies) for the files.  
   - **Persuade Cassandra** (31 Persuasion) – threaten to place a bounty on her head until she hands over the documents.  
   - **Let Cassandra go** – if all attempts fail, you may allow her to leave; the quest ends without reward.  
   - **Kill Cassandra** – as a last resort, eliminate her and loot the research from her corpse.  

## Return the Research to Anton  

1. Bring the retrieved research back to **Anton Crane**.  
2. He will reward you with **3,750 Octo** and the **Researcher’s Lab Coat**, a unique armor piece.  
3. If you sedated (rather than killed) the Raptidons, you also gain a reputation bonus with **Aunt Cleo**.  

## Optional Alternate Path  

- You may lie to Anton about the research and keep it for yourself, then sell it to **Gladys** during the “Distress Signal” quest.
