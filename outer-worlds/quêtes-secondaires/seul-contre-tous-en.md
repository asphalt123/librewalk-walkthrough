
## Acquisition
- **Anton Crane**

## Reward
- **1250 Octos**

## Associated Quests
- “Roseway at Full Teeth”
- “Vulcan’s Hammer”
- “Cleaning Unit”

## Steps
1. During your first dialogue with **Anton** in the quest “Roseway at Full Teeth”, accept to help him find his apprentice. After the conversation, leave the area and head to the building located south of your position.  
2. Inside, approach the corpse in front of you and pick up the **access card** on the floor.  
3. Go through the corridor on the right and open the door at the far end.  
4. In the next room, eliminate all robots, then climb the ladder.  
5. At the top, enter the offices and kill all **Raptidons**.  
6. After the fight, use the **access card** to unlock the far door and talk to **Jameson**.  
7. Return to **Anton** to complete the mission and claim your reward.  

## Alternate Outcome
- If you take too long to rescue **Jameson** on the upper floor of the old lab, the **Raptidons** will kill him. You will need to loot his corpse before reporting back to **Anton**.
