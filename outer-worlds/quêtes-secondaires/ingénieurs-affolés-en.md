
## Start the Quest  

1. Go to the **Botanical Lab**.  
2. Talk to **Thomas Kemp** near the workbench and accept the quest.  

## Volume 1  

1. Travel north to the **Edgewater Community Center**.  
2. Go upstairs.  
3. Locate the document on a desk and pick it up.  

## Volume 2  

1. Head to the **Edgewater Canning Plant**.  
2. Enter the main hall.  
3. Climb to the upper level.  
4. Enter the old office belonging to Parvati’s father.  
5. Find the second volume on the lockers and collect it.  

## Volume 3  

1. Continue the quest **“And the Electricity Was”** until you reach the **Power Plant**.  
2. Move toward the central area of the building known as **The Abyss**.  
3. Descend the series of ladders to the lowest level.  
4. Retrieve the document next to **Higgins’ bunk**.  

## Return to Thomas  

1. Return to **Thomas Kemp** at the Botanical Lab.  
2. Hand over the three volumes.  

**Reward:** 800 Octos + Charged Surface Mod.
