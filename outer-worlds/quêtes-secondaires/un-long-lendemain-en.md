
## Acquisition
- Talk to **Martin Abernathy** in Edgewater to receive the side quest.

## Reward
- Variable (depends on how you complete the quest).

## Associated Quests
- “To Our (Too) Dear Deceased”
- “And Then There Was Light”

## Walkthrough
1. **Talk to Martin Abernathy** in Edgewater to start the quest.  
2. After the conversation, leave his house. **Esther Blaine** will approach you. Listen to her and then head north to the community center.  
3. Approach the entrance of the community center. Move carefully and **eliminate the marauders guarding the door**.  
4. Enter the building and **neutralize the nearest enemy**.  
5. **Obtain an access card** to open the first‑aid room. Choose one of the following methods:  
   - **Hacking (25)** – Use the terminal at the community‑center entrance to encode a new access card.  
   - **Lockpicking (5)** – Go to the southwest ground‑floor room, pick the lock on the crate, and retrieve a key.  
   - **Brute Force (100)** – Go upstairs, kill all marauders, then search the corpses for a key.  
6. With the key in hand, **enter the first‑aid room** and collect the **stash of medicines**.  
7. Return to Edgewater. You can finish the quest in one of three ways:  
   - **Give the medicines to Abernathy** – 500 Octos + 300 Octos (requires Intimidation/Persuasion 5).  
   - **Give the medicines to Esther** – 700 Octos + 300 Octos (requires Intimidation 5 or Charm).  
   - **Give the medicines to Reyes** – 500 Octos + reputation bonus with the Council.
