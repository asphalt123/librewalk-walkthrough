
**Acquisition:** Edna Ingmire  
**Reward:** 1500 Octos + **Silencieux**  
**Associated quest:** “Ship, Sweet Ship”

## Steps  

1. Complete the quest **“Ship, Sweet Ship.”**  
2. Talk to **Edna** in the engine room and accept her request for help.  
3. Return to the **Impostor** and set a course for relay station **PC‑23**.  

4. Upon arrival at PC‑23:  
   - Go through the first door.  
   - Turn left and lure the robot that is visible in the background.  
   - Defeat the robot, then destroy the mines in the area.  
   - Jump over the remaining trap to reach the next room.  
   - Exit through the door on the right.  

5. Inside the next area:  
   - Eliminate the robots in the left‑hand corner.  
   - Ascend the stairs.  

6. At the top of the stairs:  
   - Enter the office.  
   - Inspect the corpse at the far end.  

7. From the corpse, collect:  
   - The **access code**.  
   - The unique weapon **Silencieux**.  

8. Use the **access code** on the terminal to unlock it and reactivate the relay.  

9. Leave the relay station and return to **Edna** on the Precursor.
