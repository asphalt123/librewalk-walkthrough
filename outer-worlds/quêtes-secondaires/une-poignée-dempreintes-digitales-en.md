
## Acquisition  
1. Go to the Edgewater Police Station.  
2. Speak to **Agent Reyes** to receive the quest.  
3. Use the nearby terminal to view the wanted posters and learn about the three targets.  

## Targets  

1. **Bertie Cotton**  
   - Location: West of Edgewater, in a small raider camp.  
   - Eliminate the target.  
   - Loot one of his fingers (optional).  

2. **Guillaume Antrim**  
   - Location: Northeast of Edgewater, surrounded by several bandits.  
   - Eliminate the target.  
   - You will also meet **Zoé** here; talk to her as part of the “Kind Spirits” quest.  

3. **Dr. Maybell**  
   - Location: Northwest of Edgewater, inside a hidden cave in the mountain.  
   - Wait for the target and the raiders to exit the cave, then eliminate them.  

## Completion  
1. Return to **Agent Reyes** after all three targets are dead.  
2. Collect the reward of **1500 Octos** and the **Junior Deputy Agent Badge**.
