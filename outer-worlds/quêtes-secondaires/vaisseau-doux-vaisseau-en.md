
## Starting the Quest  
1. Go to the **Precursor Machine Room** and talk to **Junlei Tennyson**. Accept the mission.  
2. Exit the room and take the elevator directly opposite to reach the **Secondary Docks**.  

## Encounter with Captain MacRedd  
3. In the secondary docks, you will meet **Captain MacRedd** (if the “Who’s There?” quest is not finished).  
4. Choose one of the following approaches:  

   - **Negotiate (40 Persuasion/Intimidation)**  
     - Calm MacRedd, mention the part you need.  
     - Optionally bribe him for **602 Octos** or bluff with **40 Lie** to gain access to his hideout.  

   - **Attack**  
     - Fight MacRedd and any allies.  

## Retrieve the Heat Sink  
5. Regardless of the chosen approach, proceed to the far end of the room.  
6. Climb the stairs on the north‑west side to the upper level.  
7. Pick up the **Heat Sink** located at the back of the upper room.  
8. (Optional) Return to the main room, go down to the lower level, and speak with **Soldier Lettie Dixon**, who was sent by Junlei.  

## Return to Junlei for New Objectives  
9. Leave the area and talk to **Junlei Tennyson** again to receive further instructions.  
10. Head to the west side of the machine room and take the door on your right.  

## Clear Creatures and Access the Repair Area  
11. Use the elevator to go down one level.  
12. Eliminate all creatures in the vicinity.  
13. Climb the ladder at the far back and enter the first room on the right.  
14. Turn left, ascend the stairs, enter the next room, then descend the left‑hand ladder.  
15. At the bottom, use the terminal to **repair the heat sinks**.  

## Report Back and Claim Reward  
16. Return to **Junlei Tennyson** and report the completed work.  
17. Collect your reward: **3750 Octos** and **Welder Glasses** (a unique armor piece).
