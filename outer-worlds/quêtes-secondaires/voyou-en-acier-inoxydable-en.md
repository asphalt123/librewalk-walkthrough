
**Acquisition:** **Talamadge Kerr**  
**Reward:** **625 Octos**  

1. Go to the apartments south of town. A **panicked worker** will approach you. Talk to him.  
2. Enter the building and go to the first room on the right to start the quest.  
3. Inside, **inspect the document** on the table and **search the bag** in the back of the room.  
4. Proceed to **Left Field** and **interrogate Nell** about **Isaac** to obtain new information.  
5. Follow the marker to the indicated alley and approach the **thugs**. Choose one of the following options:  

   - **Kill the thugs** – the quest can finish normally, but you will receive a small reputation penalty with SubLight.  
   - **Intimidate the thugs** (requires 55 Intimidation) – persuade **Elijah** and his men to leave the area.  
   - **Find the weapon of the crime:**  
     1. Go to the docks near the cannery north of town.  
     2. Search the crate there and retrieve a **broken Tossball cross**.  
     3. Return to **Elijah**, tell him you have found the weapon, and he will agree to leave town peacefully.  

6. After resolving the situation, **talk to Sanjar** to complete the objective and receive your reward.
