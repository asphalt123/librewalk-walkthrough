
## Starting the Quest

1. Go to the Edgewater landing area and talk to **Ludwig Miller** to activate the mission.  
2. If you have a high Persuasion skill, ask Miller for an advance of **75 Octos**.  

## Small Discharge – Robot Encounter

3. Head to the small discharge site near the town and approach the robot.  
4. Choose one of the following actions:  
   - **Repair** the robot (this allows you to find it later in the power plant during the “And Electricity Was” quest).  
   - **Destroy** the robot (if you prefer to eliminate the threat immediately).  

5. Return to **Ludwig Miller**, report the outcome, then proceed to town and enter the cantina.  

## Cantina – Retrieve the Package

6. Inside the cantina, go to the far‑back corridor.  
7. Find and take the small package hidden behind the toilets.  

8. Report back to **Ludwig Miller**. He will give you a **Mod** and new objectives.  

## Power Plant – “And Electricity Was” Quest

9. Travel to the power plant.  
10. At the entrance, go down the ramp and continue to the lower‑level workshop.  
11. Enter the right‑hand room and locate **Jeremy**. Reactivate him so he can clear the building of enemies.  

12. Return to the upper floor, head north, and climb the stairs.  
13. Search the corpse of the security chief and obtain his **Access Card**.  

14. Go back to the plant entrance, unlock the locked door using the **Access Card**, and retrieve the **Logic Circuit**.  

## Finalizing the Quest

15. Return to **Ludwig Miller** with the required items.  
16. Complete the quest and receive the **Electric Staff** (a unique weapon) as your reward.
