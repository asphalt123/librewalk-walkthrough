
- **Acquisition**: Talk to **Commander Sanita** on the **Precursor**.  
- **Reward**: 1250 Octos + 2 Detox.  
- **Associated Quest**: “Ship, Sweet Ship”.

## Starting the Quest
1. Go to the **Precursor** ship.  
2. Speak to **Commander Sanita** near the control station and accept the quest.  
3. After the dialogue, head to the rear of the ship and take the elevator to the Secondary Docks.  
4. Choose any available path at the bottom to continue the quest.

## Negotiating with MacRedd (40 Persuasion / 40 Intimidation)
1. Talk to **MacRedd** and attempt to calm him.  
2. Tell him you are here to collect the bounty on his head.  
3. Use **Persuasion** (or **Intimidation**) to convince him to negotiate.  
4. He will give you his **lighter**.  
5. Return to **Commander Sanita** and report MacRedd’s “death” to finish the quest and claim the reward.

## Killing MacRedd
1. Enter **MacRedd’s** lair and eliminate all enemies.  
2. After the fight, pick up the **lighter** from MacRedd’s corpse and the unique **flamethrower “Montag.”**  
3. Loot the surrounding area.  
4. Return to **Commander Sanita** to complete the quest and collect your bounty.
