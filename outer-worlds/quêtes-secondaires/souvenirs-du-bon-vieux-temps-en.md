
## Acquisition: Ash

**Reward:** Variable  

1. Go to the **tavern** in Amber Heights and talk to **Ash** to start the quest.  
2. Follow the road southwest to the **abandoned refuge**.  
   - Eliminate the **bandits** inside.  
   - Enter the outpost, examine the documents on the bench, then read the messages on the terminal.  
3. Head south and cross the bridge over the river at the indicated location.  
4. Proceed to the marker and kill all **creatures** surrounding the corpses.  
5. Approach the **charnel** and pick up the **small box** from the human remains.  
6. Return to **Ash** and inform him of his companions' deaths.  
   - During the dialogue, reveal that you are aware of his schemes; he will reward you with **875 Octos** (instead of the usual 625).
