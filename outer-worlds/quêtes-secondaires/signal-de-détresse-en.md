
## Accept Quest from Gladys
- Talk to **Gladys** and accept the mission to investigate the distress signal from the Roseway laboratory.

## Travel to Roseway
- Go to the **Precursor** and listen to the recorded message (this activates the “Roseway at Full Teeth” quest).  
- Travel to the Roseway landing zone.  
- Proceed to the Roseway laboratory.

## Talk to Key NPCs
- Speak with **Anton Crane**, **Orson Shaw**, and **Vaughn Cortes**. These dialogues are part of the quests **Roseway at Full Teeth**, **The Vulcan Hammer**, and **The Apprentice Alchemist**.

## Complete Quest Objectives
- **Roseway at Full Teeth** – Retrieve the **Improved Toothpaste Formula** data.  
- **The Apprentice Alchemist** – Retrieve the **Raptidon Muscle** data.  
- **The Vulcan Hammer** – Retrieve the **FORCE prototype weapon** data.

## Sell Data to Gladys
- Return to **Gladys** and sell the collected information. You will receive:  
  - **Improved Toothpaste Formula** – 3,750 Octo  
  - **Raptidon Muscle** – 1,875 Octo  
  - **FORCE prototype weapon** – 1,875 Octo  
- Report back to **Gladys** to finish the quest and earn an additional **375 Octo**.

## Optional: Finish Without Selling
- Complete the three side missions, then return to the **receleuse** (information broker) and tell her you found nothing valuable. You won’t receive money from her, but the rewards from the Roseway side missions will more than compensate.
