
## Travel to Eridan
1. Open the navigation console and select **Eridan** as your destination.  
2. After arriving, speak with the attendant at the bottom of the elevator.

## Grand Colonial Hotel
1. Follow the mission marker to the entrance of the **Grand Colonial Hotel**.  
2. Enter the hotel and go to the **Grand Salon** to discover the crime scene.  
3. Talk to **Agent Keene**, then speak with **Dr. Goodnight** to obtain the **Anomaly Amplifier**.

## Examine Clues
- Pick up the **letter written by Helen** on the floor for possible later use.

## Complete the Objective
1. Equip the **Anomaly Amplifier**.  
2. Use it on the footprints on the floor to **analyze the traces**.  
3. The analysis fulfills the objective and ends the mission.
