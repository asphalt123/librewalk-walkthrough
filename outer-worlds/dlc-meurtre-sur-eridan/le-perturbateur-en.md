
## 1. Activate the Quest  
1. Complete **“A Fleeting Taste of Purple”**.  
2. The quest “The Perturber” will automatically become active.

## 2. Hotel – VIP Floor  

1. Return to the hotel lobby.  
2. Talk to **Dr. Goodnight** in his laboratory.  
3. Speak with the front‑desk receptionist to obtain **VIP floor access**.  
4. Take the elevator to the VIP level.  

### On the VIP Level  
5. Enter **Spencer’s** room and interrogate him.  
6. Return to the lobby and go to the kitchen to question **Chef Anwar**.

## 3. Leave the Hotel – Head to the Port  

1. Exit the hotel and follow the path to the bridge that leads to the port.  
2. Talk to **Sedrick** at the terminal to gain entry to the restricted area.  

## 4. Port – Security Office  

1. Enter the **law‑enforcement office**.  
2. Bribe or hack the **assistant sommelier** to obtain the security footage.  

## 5. Investigate the Office  

1. Proceed to the back office and examine **Maria Keene’s** corpse.  

## 6. Island Between the Wreckage and the Hotel  

1. Travel to the small island located between the wreckage and the hotel.  
2. Eliminate the enemies there.  
3. Search **Owen’s** corpse to update your objectives.  

## 7. Return to the VIP Floor  

1. Go back to the hotel’s VIP level.  
2. Confront **Spencer** with the evidence you have gathered and force him to confess.  

## 8. Finish the Quest  

1. Return to your own room.  
2. Report to **Ludovico** to complete the quest.  

*Optional:* You may choose to imprison Spencer during the final dialogue; this decision does not affect any other storyline events.
