
## Starting the Quest
- Go to the chief arborist’s office and speak to **DeWitt Hathaway** to activate the quest.  
- After the conversation, investigate the area for the sabotages.

## Sabotage Investigation

### 1. Process the Purplies
- Go to the designated location and examine the command console to complete this objective.

### 2. Bush Row
- Inspect the can on the ground to determine the chemical composition.

### 3. Resolve the Automech Issue  
You have two ways to complete this objective:

**Option A – Destroy Drones**  
1. Travel to the southern part of the zone.  
2. Destroy all drones present there.

**Option B – Hack the Drone Terminal**  
- Requires at least **55** in the Hacking skill **or** the drone terminal access card.  
1. Go to the Automech warehouse.  
2. Deactivate the drones.  
3. Open the user log to obtain information needed for the quest’s true conclusion.

### 4. Interrogate the Intern (Optional)  
- If you accessed the user log from the Automech terminal, go to the nursery warehouse.  
- Talk to **Anna** to learn the real reason behind the sabotages.

## Report to DeWitt
- Return to **DeWitt** and give your report. At this point you have several possible outcomes.

### Outcome A – Side with the SLUG
- Receive **2,250** credits from **Clyde**.  
- Small reputation boost with **SubLight Underground**.  
- Reputation loss with **Rizzo**.

### Outcome B – Side with Rizzo
- Receive **2,250** credits from **DeWitt**.  
- Reputation boost with **Rizzo**.  
- Reputation loss with **SubLight Underground**.

### Outcome C – Reconcile SLUG and DeWitt  
- **Persuasion**: Both **DeWitt** and **Clyde** are satisfied and each pay you **2,250** credits.  
- **Intimidation**: Both parties accept a deal, but you receive only one payment of **2,250** credits.

## Dealing with the Intern
- After resolving the conflict between the SLUG and Rizzo, address Anna’s fate.  
- To keep Anna employed, repeatedly select the first dialogue option in each conversation.  
- To have Anna fired, convince **DeWitt** to dismiss her; she will disappear from the game.  
- In either case, **DeWitt** gives you an additional **2,250** credits.

## Alternate Completion
- If you never accessed the intern’s information in the Automech terminal, you can still finish the quest normally.  
- You will miss the extra **2,250**‑credit bonus that **DeWitt** awards for uncovering the sabotage truth.
