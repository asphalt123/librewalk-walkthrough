
## Starting the Quest  

1. Go to the hotel pool area and talk to **Dolph McAllister** to start the quest.  
2. If you haven’t already, visit **Dr. Goodnight** and obtain the **Anomaly Amplifier**.  
3. Analyze the pool water, then examine the filter.  
4. Return to **Dolph** and head north to the greenhouse located behind the hotel.  
5. Read the employee’s journal on the desk in the greenhouse to learn about the chemical mixer.  

From this point you can choose one of two main approaches.

---

## Option 1 – Craft a Pool Cleaner  

1. Operate the machine levers to load chemicals **1‑5‑4** into the respective tanks.  
2. Use the terminal to prepare the cleaning mixture.  
3. Return to the pool area and insert the cleaner into the filter.  
4. Talk to **Dolph** to receive the **SLUG warehouse key** as a reward.  
   - You can also use **Persuasion** or **Intimidation** to earn an additional **1250 octo**.  

**Accessing the SLUG Warehouse (optional)**  

1. Enter the Botany Operations building and go down to the lower level.  
2. Open the warehouse door with the **SLUG warehouse key**.

---

## Option 2 – Sabotage the Pool  

1. After reading the employee’s journal, go to the north balcony of the hotel and speak with **Donovan**. He will ask you to sabotage the pool for **Spacer’s Choice**.  
2. Return to the greenhouse and mix any chemicals to create an unknown compound.  
3. Go back to the pool and insert the compound into the filter, turning the water into acid.  
4. Talk to **Dolph** and tell him you’re working for **Rizzo**. Use **Intimidation** during this dialogue to obtain the **SLUG warehouse key** (if you haven’t gotten it already).  
5. Return to **Donovan** to finish the quest and receive **1250 octo** as the reward.  

---

## Alternative Outcome  

- If you sabotage the pool **before** speaking with **Donovan**, the quest will be marked as failed.  
- You can still obtain the **SLUG warehouse key** from **Dolph** if your **Intimidation** skill is high enough.
