
## Starting the Quest
1. Activate the quest.
2. Head toward the **Distillation Station**.
3. Fight through the hosts to reach the side door of the building.

## Inside the Distillation Station
1. Enter the station.
2. Interact with the **intercom** on the side to learn the truth about **Helen**.

## Dialogue Choice
- After the dialogue, choose one of the following options:

### Accept to work with **Ludovico**
1. Unlock the quest **“The Infernal Explosive Machine of Helen Halcyon.”**
2. This path will eventually lead you to **condemn Eridan**.

### Side with **Helen Halcyon**
1. Activate the quest **“Factory Escape.”**
2. Continue the subsequent missions to **save the colony from parasites**.
