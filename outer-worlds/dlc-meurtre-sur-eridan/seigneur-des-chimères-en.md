
## Prerequisites
- Complete **“The Prince of Tossball”** quest **or** go directly to **Sedrick** at the port.

## Walkthrough
1. **Access the Port**  
   - Head to the port area where **SLUG** agents are present.  
   - Deal with them as you prefer: eliminate, intimidate, or lie.

2. **Enter the Captain’s Office** (located next to the agents).

3. **Interact with the Secretary**  
   - If playing a human character, you can attempt to bribe or persuade the secretary to see the boss.  
   - If playing a non‑human character, simply go upstairs, ignoring the secretary’s cries.

4. **Talk to Sedrick**  
   - Question **Sedrick** about **Helen**.  
   - Accept his request to help locate the missing cargo.

5. **Investigate Sedrick’s Gear**  
   - Examine **Sedrick’s weapons** using the **amplifier**.

6. **Search the Lounge**  
   - Look at the book placed on the table.

7. **Retrieve the Receiver**  
   - Go to the east‑side toilets and pick up the **receiver** from the floor.

8. **Proceed to the SLUG Warehouse**  
   - Inside, go upstairs and interrogate the team leader to receive new objectives.

9. **Travel to the Delivery Zone**.

10. **Examine the Automaton**  
    - Retrieve its data logs.  
    - Options:  
      - Take the information without disabling the security system, then fight the robots that appear.  
      - Let **Parvati** handle the data retrieval to avoid combat.

11. **Return to Sedrick**  
    - Deliver the gathered information to complete the objective.

12. **Report to Ludovico**  
    - Speak with **Ludovico** to finish the quest.
