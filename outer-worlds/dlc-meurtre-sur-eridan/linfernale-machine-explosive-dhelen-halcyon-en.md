
## Quest Steps

1. After the quest activates, head **south** and then follow the marker **west**.  
2. In the next room, skirt the **steam jets** on the right side and pass through the far door.  
3. On the opposite side, examine the fuels on the left shelf using the **Anomaly Amplifier** and **defuse the bomb**.  
4. Talk to **Ludovico**, then exit the area.  
5. Return to your suite and use the terminal to report to the **Administrator**.
