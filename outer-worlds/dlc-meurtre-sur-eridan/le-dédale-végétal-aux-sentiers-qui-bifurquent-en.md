
## Quest Start
1. Go to the entrance of the vegetial labyrinth in the Purple Orchard and talk to **Samuel Huttes** to begin the mission.  
2. After the dialogue, take the path on your left to meet **Salina Navarre**.  
3. Return to **Samuel**, then head right to meet **Emily Zimmerman**.  
4. When the conversation ends, go through the nearby passage and head west.  
5. At the next intersection, continue south.  
6. At the following crossing, turn south again, eliminate the robots, then proceed west to a small orchard guarded by drones.  
7. From there, follow the southern trail to a mausoleum‑like structure.  
8. From the mausoleum, take the north‑east path to reach the center of the labyrinth.  

## Center of the Labyrinth
- Inspect the bottle on the ground.  
- Collect the **Certificate of Completion** from the display stand.  
- Pick up the unique weapon **Captain Robin’s Courage** from the bench on your left.  

You now have several ways to finish the quest.

## Option 1 – Leave the Vodka with Carter
- Go to the greenhouse northwest of the labyrinth and talk to **Carter**.  
- Confirm that he has the original vodka and agree to let him keep the bottle.  
- Receive **1250 octos** as the quest reward.

## Option 2 – Convince Carter to Hand Over the Bottle
- Use high **Lie**, **Persuade**, or **Intimidate** skill to get **Carter** to give you the vodka bottle.  
- Return to any of the NPCs (**Samuel**, **Salina**, or **Emily**) and give them the bottle.  
- Receive **1250 octos**.

## Option 3 – Obtain Double Payout
1. Accept to let **Carter** keep the original bottle and collect the first **1250 octos**.  
2. After receiving the payment, steal the bottle back from **Carter** (or retrieve it from his corpse).  
3. Deliver the bottle to **Samuel**, **Salina**, or **Emily** for an additional **1250 octos**, totaling double the normal reward.

## Alternative Route – Skip the Labyrinth
- Steal the vodka bottle from **Carter** before entering the labyrinth.  
- Complete the quest and receive the reward without ever stepping inside the maze.
