
## Starting the Quest
1. Return to the hotel reception hall and speak with **Agent Keene** to request the suspect list.  
2. After the dialogue, go to **concierge Halston** at the hotel entrance. Persuade or bribe him to gain access to the **VIP floor**.

## VIP Floor – Bertie's Room
3. Take the elevator to the **VIP floor** and enter **Bertie's room**.  
4. Use the **anomaly amplifier** to examine the document in the trash bin.  
5. Use the **anomaly amplifier** again to inspect the footprints in the dressing room.

## Weather Station (The Helms)
6. Leave the hotel and travel to the **Helms**.  
7. Enter the nearby **weather station**.  
8. Inside, inspect the **mascot corpse** with the **anomaly amplifier** to update your objectives.  
9. Retrieve the **Armored Tossball Outfit** from the corpse.  
10. Pick up the **Rangers Mascot Head** from the floor (both items are unique).

## Unlocking the Port Area
11. Complete the quest **“A Fleeing Taste of Purple”** before proceeding.  
12. After finishing that quest, use the **terminal** near the bridge that connects the hotel to the port to contact **Sedrick** and gain access to the **port area**.

## Port Area – Bandits and Agent Gray
13. Deal with the **bandits** as part of the **“Lord of Chimera”** quest.  
14. Proceed to **Agent Gray’s mess hall** and locate **Bertie**.

## Interrogations and Final Report
15. Interrogate the **Tossball player**.  
16. Confirm his alibi by speaking with **Orick**, who is nearby.  
17. Return to your room and file a report with **Ludovico**. (You may choose to arrest a suspect, but it has no effect on the outcome.)
