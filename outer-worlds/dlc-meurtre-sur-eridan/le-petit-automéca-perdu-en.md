
## Acquisition
- **Mildred Lenox** (quest giver)

## Objective
- Complete **“A fleeting taste of purple”** (prerequisite)

## Steps
1. Go to the **Legal Hunter** store in the Wilds and talk to **Mildred Lenox** to start the quest.  
2. After the dialogue, travel west to locate **Betty** the robot.  
3. Choose one of the following approaches:

   - **Eliminate the canids**  
     - Defeat the canids to protect Betty.  
     - Return to **Mildred** after the fight.  
     - Receive **625 Octo** and the unique weapon **The Attractor**.

   - **Examine Betty**  
     - Approach the robot quietly and initiate dialogue.  
     - Use personal skills to open its maintenance hatch **or** call **Parvati** for assistance.  
     - Return to **Mildred** and persuade her to reveal the truth.  
     - Complete the quest and receive the same reward (**625 Octo** and **The Attractor**) plus **10 weapon parts**.

   - **Destroy Betty**  
     - Destroy the robot.  
     - Report back to **Mildred**.  
     - Quest ends with **no reward**.

## Alternative Outcome
- If you bring **Betty** back alive, you can intimidate **Mildred** to obtain an additional **750 Octo**.
