
## Acquisition
- **Quest Giver:** **Isabella Essary**  
- **Reward:** **750 octo**  
- **Prerequisite:** Complete “A fleeting taste of purple”

## Steps
1. Travel to the Ruins and talk to **Isabella** and **Lawrence** near the legal hunter shop to start the quest.  
2. Follow the quest markers and collect the **access cards** from each dead client you encounter.  
3. When you reach the client at the far north‑east of the area, read his **journal** to unlock additional dialogue options.

## Completion Options
- **Give the access cards to Isabella and Lawrence** – the quest ends immediately and you receive **750 octo**.  
- **Set a trap for the two employees** (requires having read the client’s journal):  
  1. Lie to **Isabella** and **Lawrence** to lure them into a deadly trap.  
  2. After the dialogue, teleport anywhere on the map to trigger the employees’ departure.  
  3. Return to the north‑east area and locate the bodies of the two thugs.  

Choosing the trap option does not affect the quest reward; it only provides narrative closure by avenging the dead clients.
