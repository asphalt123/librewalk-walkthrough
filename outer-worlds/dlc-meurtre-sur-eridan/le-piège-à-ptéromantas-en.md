
## Acquisition  
- Talk to **Thelbert Wolcott** to start the quest.  

## Rewards  
- **1500 Octo**  
- **Magnum Force** (unique weapon)  

## Steps  

1. Go to the **Timonerie** area and enter the **Navigation Bridge Museum**.  
2. Offer your services to **Thelbert** to activate the quest.  
3. After the dialogue, go upstairs and use **Thelbert’s terminal** to update your objectives.  
4. Collect the **battery** and **cutlery** at the locations indicated inside the museum.  
5. Exit the museum and enter the **Weather Station**.  
6. Pick up the **cables** from the floor.  
7. Follow the nearby marker and retrieve the **scrap metal**.  
8. Before returning to **Thelbert**, head west to the **Engineering Camp**.  
9. Pick up the **windmill** (quest item).  
10. Return to **Thelbert**, turn in all collected items, and receive the **Magnum Force** weapon.
