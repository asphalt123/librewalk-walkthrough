
## Activation
1. Go to your suite and check the messages on the bedroom terminal.  
2. Alternatively, talk to the porter **Norville** and persuade him to give information about the Prophetess.

## Complete Prerequisite Quest
1. Finish the quest **A Fleeting Taste of Purple** to unlock access to the Wild Wastes.  
2. Cross the bridge leading to the area and take the elevator.  
3. Once on the upper level, head northeast and enter the **Prophetess of Productivity** bunker.

## Inside the Bunker
1. Enter the room on the right, then talk to the Prophetess in her tent.  
2. Analyze the herbs on the bench behind you using the **Anomaly Amplifier** to obtain alibi information.  
3. Talk to **Aliyah Mason**, ask a few questions, then exit the conversation to trigger a crucial reveal.  
4. Access the nearby terminal and read **Ruth’s** message for a new clue.  
5. Leave the bunker and analyze the footprints on the road with the **Anomaly Amplifier**.  
6. Follow the trail and inspect the mound of earth.

## Confront the Prophetess
1. Return to the Prophetess and present all the gathered revelations.  
2. She will ask to be imprisoned and may even offer payment; decide whether to imprison her (the choice has no impact on the rest of the game).  
3. Return to your suite and report to **Ludovico** to complete the mission.
