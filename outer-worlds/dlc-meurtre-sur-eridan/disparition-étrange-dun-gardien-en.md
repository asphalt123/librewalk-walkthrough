
## Acquisition
- Talk to **Lillie Vargas** near the bridge that connects the hotel to the orchards to start the mission.

## Rewards
- **1250 octo**

## Walkthrough
1. After accepting the mission, head to the bridge that links the hotel to the wastelands.  
2. Eliminate all enemies there, then speak with the guard. Choose one of the following:
   - Be modest to receive a small reputation boost, **or**
   - Persuade him to give you **200 octo**.  
3. Approach the nearby building:
   - Pick the lock on the door, **or**
   - Climb the side ladder and enter through the roof.  
4. Inside, read the journal on the table to update your objectives.  
5. Follow the marker to the botanical operations area and locate the new document.  
6. Return to the surface and travel to the island between the orchard and the hotel.  
7. Fight your way to the building marked on the map and retrieve the **phonograph** from the bed. Listen to the recording.  
8. Return to the hotel and descend the stairs to the employee quarters.  
9. Follow the marker to **Room A2**. There:
   - Examine the base of the door with the **anomaly amplifier**.  
   - Search the floor tile to obtain the **access card**.  
10. Open the room, kill the **parasite**, then examine **Evans’ corpse** to trigger new objectives.  
11. Go back upstairs and head to the bridge leading to the helm.  
12. Interrogate **Amelia** to learn the truth about Evans’ death.  
13. After the conversation, meet **Lillie Vargas** at the landing pad and tell her what happened to Evans. (You may lie about Amelia’s intentions; the quest ends the same way and the reward does not change.)

## Alternative Paths
- You can return to **Vargas** immediately after finding Evans’ body and finish the quest without talking to Amelia.  
- After the dialogue with Amelia, you may choose to attack and kill her. This does not affect the overall outcome, except that Vargas will replace two employees instead of one.
