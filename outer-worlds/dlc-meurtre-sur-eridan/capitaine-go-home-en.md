
## Main Objective
1. Board your ship.
2. Talk to **ADA** to finish the DLC.

## If You Sided with **Helen Halcyon** (instead of **Ludovico**)
1. Before departing the planet, speak with **Sedrick Kincannon**.
2. Then have a brief conversation with **Helen Halcyon**.
3. Return to your ship and talk to **ADA** to complete the quest.
