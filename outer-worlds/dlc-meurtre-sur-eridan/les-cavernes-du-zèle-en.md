
## Acquisition
- Talk to **Virgil Henderson** at the small camp north of the wasteland to receive the quest.

## Requirements
- Complete **A fleeting taste of purple**.

## Rewards
- **750 octos**

## Steps
1. After receiving the quest from **Virgil**, head to the entrance of the **Pierced Caves**.  
2. Inside the caves, locate the three nearest markers and collect the first **geological studies**.  
3. Take the elevator on the south side of the caves to reach the next level.  
4. Go through the door directly ahead, eliminate all enemies, and pick up the final **geological study** from the floor.  
5. Return to **Virgil Henderson** to complete the quest.
