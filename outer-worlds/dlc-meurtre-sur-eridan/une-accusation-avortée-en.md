## Main Quest: A Botched Accusation  

### Prerequisites  
1. Complete **any two** of the following side missions:  
   - *The Prince of Tossball*  
   - *The Disruptor*  
   - *The Queen of Productivity*  
   - *Lord of the Chimera*  

   *Note: Starting this quest before finishing all four missions will cause the remaining missions to fail.*

### Quest Steps  

1. **Activate the quest** – it will trigger automatically after the prerequisite missions are completed.  
2. Go to **your bedroom** in the Habitat.  
3. Talk to **Ludovico** and select the dialogue option indicating you are ready to reveal the assassin’s name.  
4. When prompted for evidence, choose any suspect you prefer (you may even select **Ludovico** himself).  
5. Confirm your choice to complete the quest and unlock the next main objective.
