
## Acquisition
- Talk to **PHAETON** at the Navigation Bridge Museum to receive the mission.

## Rewards
- Variable (depends on choices)  
- **1250 Octos**  
- **Modified Light Assault Rifle**

## Associated Quests
- “To the Great Mollusks, the Great Remedies”  
- “Helen and the Slugs”

## Walkthrough
1. Follow the mission marker and take the elevator to the lower level.  
2. Move down the hallway and go through the western door.  
3. Eliminate all enemies, then pick up the **access card** from the chair.  
4. With the access card, go through the southern door and continue down the hallway.  
5. Meet **Captain Mabel Thomson**. Convince her to help you (requires the appropriate dialogue skills). Failure results in no reward.  
6. Head south and cross the engine room at the far end of the hallway.  
7. Enter the door on the other side of the engine room, eliminate the **canine**, then take the elevator on your left.  
8. On the lower floor, proceed to the marker and kill all **giant canines** in the large room.  
9. Call the freight elevator at the back of the room to reach the **control room**.  
10. Talk to **Mabel** in the control room; she will give you **1250 Octos** and a **modified Light Assault Rifle**.  
11. Use the terminal to realign the **gyroscopic axis**.  
12. After completing the objective, return through the hallway behind you and use the elevator to go back to **PHAETON**.
