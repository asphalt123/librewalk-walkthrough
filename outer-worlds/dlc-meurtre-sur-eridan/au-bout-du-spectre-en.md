
## Acquisition
- Activate the quest by interacting with the **terminal** near Dr. Blossom’s corpse while doing the main quest **“A fleeting taste of Purple”** in the DD&RR labs. Read the lab notes to unlock the mission.

## Rewards
- **Gatling Spectre** (unique weapon)

## Associated Quest
- **“A fleeting taste of Purple”**

## Step‑by‑Step Guide

1. **Leave the DD&RR labs** after activating the mission.
2. **Travel to the Nursery Warehouse** and access the inventory register on the **terminal** to update your objectives.
3. **Visit Sidney** at the Bazaar of the Purples and ask her about vodka stock.  
   *This action makes all required Spectre Vodka bottles appear on your map.*

4. **Collect the eight Spectre Vodka bottles** (see locations below).

5. **Return to the DD&RR labs**, use the **terminal** to open the compartment, and claim the **Gatking Spectre**.

## Vodka Spectre Locations

- **Orange** – Purchased from **Sidney**.  
- **Red** – Purchased from **Sidney** or the bartender at the Grand Colonial; also found at the **sacked picnic area**.  
- **Black** – Purchased from the bartender at the Grand Colonial; also found at the **sacked picnic area**.  
- **Blue** – Purchased from the bartender at the Grand Colonial; also located on a table in the **eastern section of the vegetative maze**.  
- **Indigo** – Purchased from the bartender at the Grand Colonial; also located in a **cabinet inside the gardener’s hut**.  
- **Violet** – Purchased from the bartender at the Grand Colonial; also located in **Dr. Blossom’s office** in the DD&RR labs.  
- **Yellow** – Found in the **control room of the hive**.  
- **Green** – Found in a **crate at the Nursery Warehouse**.  

Collect each bottle, then follow the final step to obtain the **Gatling Spectre**.
