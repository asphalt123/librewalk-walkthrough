
## Acquisition
- **Dash Whitlock**

## Rewards
- Variable (depends on choices)

## Condition
- Complete “A fleeting taste of Purple”

## Steps
1. After finishing “A fleeting taste of Purple”, go to the **Pirée Spaceport** and meet **Dash Whitlock** in the **Grey Agent Cantina**.  
2. Talk to **Dash**, then head to the **Law Enforcement Headquarters**. Speak with **Cassidy** in the cells and give her the **password** to learn where the **cargo** is located.  
3. Obtain the **cargo**:  
   - **Option A** – Go to the **Delivery Zone**, talk to **Benton**, and either pay him or persuade him to hand over the cargo.  
   - **Option B** – Enter the delivery zone through the southeast breach and retrieve the cargo from the marked crate.  
4. Return to **Cassidy** after securing the **cargo**.  
5. Talk to **Joseph** in his shop. Give him the **password** and choose dialogue options that convince him to let you into the backroom. Hand him the package and gain access to his illegal shop.  
6. Choose how to handle the payout:  
   - **Keep the full 3750 Octos** – Keep all the money during the final dialogue with **Dash**. The mission is marked as a failure.  
   - **Give 1000 Octos to Dash** – Keep 2750 Octos for yourself. The mission ends normally, but the smugglers become hostile (no effect on the main story).  
   - **Give 2000 Octos to Dash** – Pay the agreed amount. The smuggler thanks you and the mission ends successfully.  
   - **Denounce the smugglers** – Return to the **Law Enforcement Headquarters** and report the smugglers for a reward of 1250 Octos. You can denounce them at any time, even before retrieving the cargo, but the quest will be considered a failure.  
7. **Optional:** After keeping the money from **Joseph**, you can still turn it in to the police for a doubled payment.
