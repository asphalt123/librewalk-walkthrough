
## 1. Initiate the Quest
1. After speaking with **Helen**, leave the area.
2. Head toward the **Port of Piraeus**.
3. Fight through the degenerated hosts and enter the **SLUG Warehouse**.

## 2. Recruit Sedrick and Retrieve Sprat Essence
1. Inside the warehouse, talk to **Sedrick** and explain the situation until he agrees to join you.
2. Proceed to the **Team Leader’s Office** and collect the **Sprat Essence**.

## 3. Gather Parasite Egg Pods
1. Exit the port area and travel north to the entrance of the **Pierced Caves** located beyond the wild wasteland.
2. Inside the caves, move toward the southwest corner.
3. Search the small chest there to obtain **Parasite Egg Pods**.

## 4. Prepare the Antiparasitic
1. Return to your hotel suite.
2. Use the **Centrifuge** to combine the **Sprat Essence** and **Parasite Egg Pods**, creating the **Antiparasitic**.

## 5. Complete the Quest
1. Leave the hotel and head to the **Museum of the Helmsman**.
2. Go up to the floor where **PHAETON** is waiting.
3. Deploy the **Antiparasitic** to finish the quest.
