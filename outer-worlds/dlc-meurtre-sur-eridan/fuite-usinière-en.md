
## Steps

1. After activating the **Flee Factory** quest, head **south**, then **west**.  
2. In the back room, avoid the steam jets and wait for **Helen** to open the next door.  
3. Continue down the corridor and cross the far‑right walkway.  
4. Pull the lever on the right side.  
5. Go through the opposite door and speak with **Helen**.  
6. After the dialogue, use the nearby ladder to climb back to the surface.  
7. Proceed to your hotel suite and talk to **Helen** to complete the mission.  
8. If you have at least **40 Persuasion**, convince **Helen** to give you **the Switchblade**, her signature pistol.
