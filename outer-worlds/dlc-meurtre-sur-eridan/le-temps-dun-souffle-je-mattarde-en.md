
## Steps

1. **Activate the quest** and head toward the **Distillation Station**.  
   - Fight your way to the main entrance.  
   - Open the door marked by the quest marker and take the elevator down.

2. On the lower level:  
   - Go through the door on the west side, then the door to the southeast.  
   - Eliminate all enemies in the area.  
   - Ascend the stairs to the next floor.

3. On this floor:  
   - Clear another group of enemies.  
   - Use the elevator located on the opposite side of the walkway to go up.

4. On the next floor:  
   - Proceed northwest, then cross the corridor on the west side.  
   - At the far end, open the rear door to find **Administrator Ludovico**.

5. After the dialogue with Ludovico, the **Queen of Parasites** fight begins.  
   - Defeat her by using your most powerful weapons **or** hack the terminals in each corner of the room to activate the steam distribution systems.

6. When the Queen is defeated:  
   - Search Ludovico’s corpse.  
   - Retrieve his **access card**, the unique item **Salve Nova**, and the **Administrator’s Attire**.

7. Return to the quest marker, talk briefly with **Harwell**, then follow the indicated access point to leave the area and complete the quest.
