
## Starting the Quest
1. After examining the crime scene, go to the reception and speak with **concierge Halston** to obtain access to your room.  
2. Take the elevator to the **luxury suite**.  

## Activate “The Queen of Productivity” and Access the Orchards
1. Inside the suite, enter the room on the right and read the messages on the **terminal** to start the **quest “The Queen of Productivity.”**  
2. Use the **terminal** in the lounge to contact administrator **Ludovico**; he will grant you access to the **Pourpreilliers Orchards**.  

## Find Clues in the Orchards
1. Leave the hotel and head southwest to the bridge; cross it and the next bridge to reach the orchards.  
2. At the orchard entrance, talk to **Sidney** and ask about **Helen** for new information.  
3. Examine the footprints on the road, then continue forward and speak with the nearby gardener.  
4. Follow the footprints west to the greenhouse; cross it.  
5. Continue following the footprints south toward the generator, then veer southwest to the ruined picnic area.  
6. Analyze the **Tossball bat** and pick up the **access card** to **DD&RR** found on its cover.  
7. Proceed to the nearby **DD&RR laboratories**.  

## Investigate the DD&RR Labs
1. In the labs, go to the back corridor and use the intercom to talk to **Dr. Blossom**. Accept her proposal to receive new objectives.  
2. Take the elevator located behind you.  

### Non‑Violent Approach (Release Anesthetic Gas)
1. Advance down the corridor and turn right.  
2. Hide behind crates, enter the left‑hand room, then exit through the door a short distance to the right.  
3. Quickly cross the corridor and enter the next room.  
4. Continue forward, open the far‑back door, and enter the workshop ahead.  
5. Enter the following room and walk along the right wall to its far end.  
6. Go through the hole in the wall and use the **terminal** to release the anesthetic gas throughout the labs.  
7. Return upstairs to find **Dr. Blossom** (her body will be dead). Instead of interrogating her corpse, examine her **terminal** for recent events.  

## Activate “At the End of the Specter”
1. Use the **Gatling terminal** to start the **quest “At the End of the Specter.”**  
2. Examine the corpses on the floor.  
3. Check the ventilation duct at the north end of the corridor.  
4. Exit the laboratories.  

## Optional: Deal with the Hornet Swarms
1. Back in the orchards, you’ll notice swarms of killer hornets. If you wish to clear them:  
   - Head to the hive located south of the orchards.  
   - Eliminate all hornet swarms in the area.  
2. Enter the shed and use the **terminal** to stop the pollination cycle.  
3. Analyze the multifunction tool on the floor for additional clues.  

## Finish the Quest
1. Return to your room at the **Grand Colonial**.  
2. Speak with administrator **Ludovico** to complete the current quest and trigger the next one.
