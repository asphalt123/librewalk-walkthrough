
## Starting the Quest  
1. Complete **“A fleeting taste of purple.”**  
2. Travel to the Wild Ruins and head to the hermit’s cabin in the north‑west.  
3. Speak with **Cyrus Wahlgren** and offer your assistance.  

## Main Options  

### 1. Eliminate the Hunters  
1. Follow the quest marker to the hunters’ location.  
2. Defeat all hunters.  
3. Return to **Cyrus**.  
4. Receive **1500 octo** and the unique weapon **“Pen.”**  

### 2. Accept Victoria’s Proposal  
1. Talk to **Victoria** at the hunters’ camp instead of initiating combat.  
2. Agree to retrieve the **title deed** for her.  
3. Return to the hermit and use **persuasion**, **lie**, or **intimidation** to obtain the deed.  
4. Go back to the hunters’ camp and complete the quest.  
5. Receive the (less valuable) reward.  

## Alternative Outcome  
- If you cannot convince the hermit to hand over the deed, you must kill **Cyrus** and **Caliban**.  
- Loot **Caliban’s Heart** from the corpse.  
- You can then admire the still‑beating organ on your desk aboard the **Impostor**.
