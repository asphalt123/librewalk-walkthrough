
## Begin the Mission
1. Travel to the **helm** and enter the **Navigation Bridge Museum**.  
2. Fight through the **SLUG soldiers** inside.  
3. Talk to **PHAETON** to update your objectives.  

## Reactivate the Reactor
1. Head down to the **basement** area.  
2. Use the **south elevator** (if you have already completed “The Monster of PHAETON”, you may use the **north elevator** instead).  
3. Proceed to the **main maintenance room**.  
4. Eliminate the **SLUG soldiers** there and kill **Sedrick Kincannon**.  

## Trigger the Reactor Lever
1. After the fight, pull the **lever** indicated.  
2. Return to the upper floor and locate **PHAETON**.  

## Confront Helen Halcyon
1. Approach the computer where **Helen Halcyon** appears.  
2. Talk with her briefly.  
3. Kill **Helen Halcyon**.  
4. Loot the unique items from her corpse:  
   - **The Needle**  
   - **Day Outfit** (option 2)  

## Complete the Quest
1. With the items in your inventory, activate the **gas dispersion procedure** from **PHAETON** to finish the quest.
