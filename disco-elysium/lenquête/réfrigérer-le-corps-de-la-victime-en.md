# Réfrigérer le corps de la victime

## Finding the fridge

1. Fail the legendary **Perception** test to locate the bullet in the victim's skull during the autopsy.  
2. Talk to **Cuno** about a fridge. If you have a decent relationship, pass a **Rhetoric** test (intermediate 11) to obtain more information.  
3. (Optional) You can also interrogate **Garte** or the shopkeeper at **Frittte**; this does not affect the investigation.  
4. Go to the librarian west of the crime scene and speak with **Plaisance**.  
5. After asking her about the fridge, open the curtains on the right.  
6. Return to **Plaisance**, ask about the back room and the curse, then offer your services to resolve the problem.  
7. Pass a **Dramatic Art** test (intermediate 11) so she agrees to let you investigate the “Investigate the cursed commercial zone” task.  
8. **Plaisance** gives you the **key** to the back room. Open the door behind the curtains. (If you cannot convince her, you may force the door, losing 1 HP.)

## Access the fridge and refrigerate the body

1. Go through the door, ascend the stairs, and open the far‑back door to reach the basement.  
2. Inspect the giant **fridge**.  
3. Move the corpse together with **Kim** into the fridge to complete the task.

## Secret: Find the bullet after refrigeration

1. With the body now refrigerated, retry the **Perception** test; this time you will locate the **bullet** with an additional bonus.
