# Décrocher le cadavre

## Use Kim's Firearm
1. Propose the idea of using Kim’s gun to Kim and let the sequence play out.  
2. After Kim fails, demand the weapon from her.  
3. Close your **left eye** to boost success chances.  
4. Attempt the **Coordination visiotactile** test (intermediate 7).  
5. If successful, the **hanged body** falls from the tree.  
6. Optional:  
   - Hit Kim’s hand to unlock the **Reflection “Pose‑m’en‑zinc”**.  
   - If your **Mechanical Affinity** is high enough, hit her hand a second time to obtain the **Reflection “Tape m’en zinc”** instead.

## Ask for Help at the Port
If you cannot free the body on your own, proceed to the port for assistance.

### Enter the Port on Your Own
1. Go to the right side of the crime scene and inspect the metal sheets near the shack.  
2. Talk to **Cuno** about the sheets, then return and make a **Perception** check (intermediate 9) to discover the shack’s hidden entrance.  
3. Enter the shack, climb out the ladder, and cross the ruins.  
4. Locate the **police cape** hanging low; perform a **Staging** test (intermediate 6) to jump onto it.  
5. If you succeed, take the **MCR patrol cape** and continue across the port.  
6. Enter the far‑right container, speak with **Evrart**, and ask him to help free the corpse.  
7. Leave the area, return to the port’s arrival point, enter the building, and exit the opposite side.  
8. Talk to **Jauge‑Caboche** and relay Evrart’s orders so he agrees to handle the victim’s body.

### Convince Jauge‑Caboche to Let You Into the Port
1. Go to the port entrance and climb the stairs to meet **Jauge‑Caboche**, who guards the door‑unlock button.  
2. Choose a method to overcome him:

   **Prove Physical Superiority**  
   - Push Jauge‑Caboche to get past him.  
   - Attempt the **Physical Instrument** test (Terrifying 13).  
   - Accept failure gracefully, then tell Kim “I can’t promise you…’’ to gain a small bonus for the next attempt.  

3. After the test, speak with the strike‑leader at the base of the door and ask him to have **Jean‑Luc** give you advice that provides an additional bonus.  
4. Return to Jauge‑Caboche, ask about his tattoos, and use one of the first three dialogue options to gain a further bonus for the upcoming **Physical Instrument** test.  
5. Perform the **Physical Instrument** test again; with the accumulated bonuses you should succeed.  
6. When Jauge‑Caboche is destabilized, execute a **360° spinning back kick** to knock him down.  
7. Unlock the port door.

### Introspect Jauge‑Caboche’s Theory (Alternative Path)
If you cannot prove physical superiority:

1. Attempt a **Conceptualisation** test (intermediate 10) to unlock the **Reflection “Advanced Theory of Races.”**  
2. Internalise this reflection as part of the “What is the Riddle of the Races?” task.  
3. Return to Jauge‑Caboche, claim you have solved the riddle, and respond however you wish until he agrees to open the port door.
