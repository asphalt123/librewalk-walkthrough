# Réaliser l'autopsie sur site

## Preparation
1. After securing the corpse, begin the autopsy with **Kim**.
2. **Optional:** If you touch the corpse without first wearing the **gardening gloves** given by the gardener, you will later unlock the task **“Get rid of the smell of death.”**

## External Examination
1. Follow Kim’s instructions and fill out the examination form as professionally as possible.  
   - **Note:** Omit any mention of the **boots** in your report to complete the task **“Recover the boots of the hanged.”**
2. When Kim finishes her external examination, repeatedly **stroke the corpse’s head**. You will notice a **bump on the skull** – an important clue for the next steps.
3. Kim will ask you to cut the strap tied around the neck:  
   a. Retrieve the **wire cutters** from the toolbox in her car.  
   b. Return to the corpse and attempt the **physical instrument test**.  
   - If you fail the test, Kim will take over the cutting for you.

## Internal Examination and Description of Injuries
1. Look inside the corpse’s mouth, then continue following Kim’s guidance.  
2. Describe every injury you find as **“Non‑fatal.”**  
   - Pay special attention to the **ligature marks on the neck**; marking them as non‑fatal grants an extra bonus during the final body examination.

## Secret Bullet Retrieval (Optional)
1. After completing the autopsy, you may attempt a **Perception test (Legendary 14)** to uncover an additional clue.  
2. If the test succeeds:  
   a. Insert your fingers into the corpse’s mouth and **touch everything** to locate the bullet.  
   b. Perform a **Mechanical Affinity test (Easy 9)** to extract the **bullet** from the mouth.  
3. If the perception test fails:  
   a. During your dialogue with Kim, **confirm that you feel you missed something**. This unlocks the task **“Refrigerate the victim’s body.”**  
   b. Refer to the alternative method for retrieving the bullet in the relevant section of the guide.
