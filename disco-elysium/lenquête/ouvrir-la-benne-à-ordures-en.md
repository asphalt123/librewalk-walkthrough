# Ouvrir la benne à ordures

## Activate the task
- Inspect the **trash bin** at the crime scene to start the objective.

## Ways to open the bin
- **Brute force**
  1. Retrieve the **crowbar** from Kim's car and equip it.  
  2. Pass a **Physical Instrument test (Legendary 14)** to force the bin open.

- **Ask for the key**
  1. Visit **Garte**.  
  2. Ask him for the **key** to the **trash bin** to unlock it without force.

## Additional investigation
- While inspecting the **trash bin**:
  - Pick up the rags to find the victim's **jeans** and **shirt**, which triggers the task **“Who threw the clothes?”**.  
  - Search the food waste for the **yellow man's can** and a **damaged register**. Refer to the task **“Find your papers”** for more details.
