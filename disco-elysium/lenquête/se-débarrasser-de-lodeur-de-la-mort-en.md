# Se débarrasser de l'odeur de la mort

## Steps

1. **Complete the autopsy** and handle the corpse without wearing gloves.  
2. Remain in **Whirling in Rags** until the end of the day when **Kim** leaves you alone.  
3. Go to your **bedroom**.  
4. Use the **bathtub** to wash your hands (or your whole body) and eliminate the corpse odor.
