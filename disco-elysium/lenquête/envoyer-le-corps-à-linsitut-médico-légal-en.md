# Envoyer le corps à l'insitut médico-légal

## Preparation
- Ensure you have collected the **hanged man's bullets** before sending the body; otherwise the equipment will be lost permanently.

## Complete the autopsy
- Finish the autopsy of the corpse.

## Send the body to the institute
1. Talk to **Kim** and ask him to transport the body to the institute.  
2. Help Kim load the corpse into his car.  
3. Decide when Kim should depart:  
   - To have Kim leave you alone for the rest of the day, let him depart immediately after loading.  
   - To keep Kim as a partner for the day, schedule the transport for **6 PM**.  
     - If it is already past that time, you will still load the body, but Kim will only leave the next morning.

## Consequences of sending early
- Sending the body before discovering the bullet lodged in the skull will trigger later difficult tests:  
  - A high **Teamwork** level may cause a vision of the forensic doctor finding the bullet.  
  - A high **Logic** level may reveal inconsistencies in Titus’s testimony during the “Interrogate the Justices” quest.

## Optional actions while Kim is away
- Use the free time to give **Speed** to **Cuno** or consume drugs, if desired.
