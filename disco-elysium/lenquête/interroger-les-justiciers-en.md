# Interroger les justiciers

## Morning of the second day (Tuesday)

- Talk to **Kim** in the Whirling Hall to learn about the syndicate members occupying the box and complete the objective **Discover who is in the syndicate box**.  
- Approach the box and talk to **Elizabeth the Gardener**, who is actually the lawyer for the Hardie Boys.

## Analyze the footprints

- Instead of speaking to **Titus** immediately, go to the crime scene.  
- Attempt an **Analytical Projection (Intermediate 11)** roll on the footprints.  
- If successful, analyze the footprints to gather information useful for the interrogation.

## Interrogate Titus

- Talk to **Titus** and assess the room, reviewing the gang members and comparing their builds with the footprints you analyzed.  
- During the interrogation, bring up the following topics (order does not matter):

  - The footprints on the floor and the missing **8th Hardie**.  
  - The corpse in the courtyard and the dockworker’s strap around its neck.  
  - Who gave the orders on the night of the murder, suggesting the missing Hardie might be the true boss.  
  - The motive for the murder, revealing that a young woman was assaulted by the victim.  
  - How the victim was killed – point out inconsistencies in Titus’s story, then attempt a **Cold Blood (Difficult 12)** roll to see which Hardie Boy might crack under pressure.  
  - Mention the bullet you found in the dead man’s head and press Titus for an explanation; he cannot account for the projectile.

## After the interrogation

- You cannot progress further in the interrogation until you advance the main story.  
- The task ends here; the next objective is **Demonstrate your authority to Titus Hardie**.
