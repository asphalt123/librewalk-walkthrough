# Questionner la jardinière

## Steps

1. **Interrogate Cuno** at the crime scene. Ask him about the **greenhouse** to obtain extra information about the **gardener** and activate the **Questionner la jardinière** task.  
2. Visit the **gardener** (the young woman) and attempt an **Analytic Projection** roll (skill level **Intermediate 11**) to realize she is lying.  
3. The task concludes automatically after this interaction, granting a small amount of experience regardless of success.
