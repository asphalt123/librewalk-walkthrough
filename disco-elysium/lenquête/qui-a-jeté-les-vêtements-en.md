# Qui a jeté les vêtements ?

## Access the dumpster  

1. Open the **garbage dumpster** at the crime scene.  
2. Inspect the interior and pick up the **victim’s clothes** and the **yellow mug**.  
3. Talk to **Cuno** and ask him about the crime scene and the victim’s clothes.  
4. (Optional) Activate the quest **“Buy the FALN pants from Cuno.”**  
5. Give the **dumpster key** to **Garte** and question him about the clothes.

## Optional: Access Gary’s apartment  

1. Meet **Evrart Claire** and accept the quest **“Open the apartment door for Evrart.”**  
2. Obtain the apartment key from **Mañana**.  
3. Go to the vacant lot behind the crime scene and use the key to open the right‑hand door.  
4. Enter the apartment, check the left‑hand shelf, and compare the mugs there with the **yellow mug** you found in the dumpster.

## Find Gary  

1. Progress the investigation until **Wednesday** (the third day).  
2. Go to the lock (the “ecluse”) and pull the lever to unlock a new area of Martinaise.  
3. Head north past the fishing village to locate **Gary** with **Morell, the cryptozoologist**.  
4. Talk to **Gary** and ask about the **yellow mug**; link him to the mug.  
5. Gary will admit that he threw the **victim’s clothes** into the dumpster.  
6. After the admission, you’ll hear a strange clicking from Gary’s clothes.  
   - Question him about the clicking and about the **victim’s armor**.  
7. Attempt a **Cold Blood** skill check (Legendary 14) to break Gary’s resistance.  
   - If your Cold Blood is low, remind him of the quest **“Open the apartment door for Evrart”** to increase pressure before the roll.  
8. If successful, exhaust the dialogue options and ask Gary for the **mercenary’s armor** (the **Fairweather T‑500 breastplate**).  
9. End the conversation to complete the quest.  

*If you fail to convince Gary now, you’ll have another chance when he returns to the **Whirling in Rags** bar with Morell.*
