# Inspecter le corps de la victime

## Reach the victim

- Leave **Whirling in Rags** and head north until you find the hanging victim on the tree.
- Approach the corpse to begin inspection.

## Overcome the smell

- **Option 1 – Endurance check (Legendary 14)**: Pass the check to keep your intestines under control and inspect the body immediately.
- **Option 2 – Strengthen defenses**  
  1. Attempt to inspect the corpse; you will vomit.  
  2. **Kim** gives you her **handkerchief**.  
  3. Go south and talk to the gardener near **Whirling in Rags** to obtain **ammonia**.  
  4. Return to the corpse, try again, vomit a second time, and pause the inspection for now.

## Unlock the “Volumetric Shit Compressor” thought

- The failed attempts unlock the thought **Volumetric Shit Compressor**; place it in your **Thought Drawer**.  
- While internalizing the thought, talk to **Cuno** and ask for advice on not vomiting.  
- You receive the bonus **Cunoified anti‑vomit** for corpse inspection.  
- After the thought is internalized, retry the inspection; you should now be able to control your intestines and examine the body fully.

## Examine the corpse

- **Boots**  
  - Inspect the boots to unlock the task **“Where is the rest of the armor?”**.  
  - Strike the boot, then continue investigating to discover a serial number, activating the task **“Search armor number”**.  
  - Attempt to remove the boots; accept your psyche’s suggestion to activate the task **“Retrieve the hanging man’s boots”**.  

- **Tattoo investigation**  
  - Examine the victim’s tattoos.  
  - Let **Kim** take a photo, then ask her for the picture.  
  - This activates the task **“Victim’s tattoos”**.  

- **Eye contact**  
  - Look the victim in the eyes, guess their eye colour, and make an **Inner Court** roll.  
  - If successful, you can **interrogate** the victim.  

- **Finish examination**  
  - End the examination to proceed to the next stage: **Victim’s unhooking**.
